Chi of Yore — statue puzzles

Two near Qingce Village. Only one has an order.

1 · NORTH
2 · SOUTH-EAST
3 · NORTH-WEST
4 · NORTH-EAST
5 · SOUTH-WEST
Facing north that reads on screen as top → bottom-right → top-left → top-right → bottom-left.
Messed it up? Activate all the statues to reset, then start again.

Puzzle 2 — Wuwang Hill summit

Order does not matter here — you just pick the right four of eight. Full diagram with a compass in wuwang-statue-puzzle.html.

Short version: activate N · SE · S · SW. Or, counting clockwise from the statue facing the climbable stone cliff as #1, activate 2, 3, 5, 8.
Both are fragments for The Chi of Yore, started by Granny Ruoxin in Qingce Village. A third sits west of Qingce on a bamboo-covered hill. All three open the Vault of Qingce.

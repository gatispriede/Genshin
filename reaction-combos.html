Worth building around

Ice + Lightning — Superconduct Your single best combo. Kaeya and Lisa are both Lv 20, and the −40% Physical RES is exactly what Kaeya's normal attacks want. This is your default.
Fire + Ice — Melt Your biggest single hit. Kaeya applies Cryo, Amber's charged shot melts it for 2× damage. Slow, so save it for bosses and Ruin Guards.
Wind + anything — Swirl Funii groups enemies and spreads whatever element is on them. Not big damage by itself, but grouping is what makes every other reaction hit four enemies instead of one.

Traps

Fire + Lightning — Overloaded knocks enemies apart It does real damage, but the knockback scatters the pack Funii just grouped and interrupts Kaeya's attack string. Don't run Amber and Lisa as your reaction core.
Wind does not swirl Dendro Anemo swirls Pyro, Hydro, Electro and Cryo only. Your Dendro character gets nothing from Funii.

The hole in your roster

You have no Hydro, and that costs you four reactions Vaporize (the other 2× multiplier), Freeze (the strongest early-game crowd control there is), Electro-Charged, and Bloom are all locked behind one element you don't own. Barbara is free from the Mondstadt Archon Quest. Getting her roughly doubles your reaction options.

Yaodie Valley

Chenyu Vale: Southern Mountain, Liyue — eastern side. South of Teatree Slope, north of Mt. Xuanlian. Added in 4.4.

Hidden world quest here: Silently the Butterfly Crosses the Valley. Teleport to Yaodie Valley and enter the cave immediately on your right — that's the whole trigger. No marker, easy to walk past.
What else is in the valley
• Valley of Vibrant Butterflies — Viewpoint, free Primogems
• Adeptus's Repose of the adeptus Herblord, with Lingshu Courtyard beside it where he made medicine
• Knocking Vases scattered around
• Heavy Clearwater Jade concentration between here and Mt. Xuanlian
• Chili Con Cloudy quest starts just east
• Clear Skies Over Xuanlian viewpoint — on a rock northeast of the waypoint south of Yaodie Valley

Lore: an adeptus's abode where Fujin sheltered peoples displaced by war, preserving them through great floods.

Starfell Valley — what not to miss

Numbered by priority. Red = do now, cyan = recurring farm spots, grey = nice-to-have.

Yellow = teleport waypoints you haven't unlocked yet. Touch every one you walk past.
Green = chest-dense zones, approximate. The landmark areas and camp clusters where chests concentrate — ruins, cliff ledges, lake shores, enemy camps. Not pin-exact locations.

Do these first

1
Temple of the Wolf — the gold-highlighted domain A one-time domain at Thousand Winds Temple, AR 15. Site of the Prologue Act I quest domain Deep Within the Temple of the Wolf, run with Kaeya during Crash Course. Clear once for rewards and a Mondstadt Shrine Key — it does not respawn. Separate from Confront Stormterror, a Trounce Domain at AR 25.
2
Statue of the Seven — Starfell Lake Unlocks map fog and lets you offer Anemoculi. Statue levels raise your Stamina cap — the biggest early quality-of-life upgrade there is.
3
Statue of the Seven — Windrise Second statue in this region. 66 Anemoculi exist across Mondstadt.
4
Mondstadt city The Anemoculus Resonance Stone pings the nearest uncollected Anemoculus. Corrected: it isn't sold by Marjorie — you craft it via Alchemy after reaching Mondstadt Reputation Level 2 (400 EXP, about four world quests). Also Katheryne for daily commissions and bounties, Wagner to forge weapons and compasses, Lisa's library for world-quest books.

Farm spots you'll return to for months

5
Midsummer Courtyard Artifact domain (Thundering Fury / Thundersoother), unlocks at AR 22. 20 resin per run.
6
Starsnatch Cliff — Cecilia + Crystalflies Cecilia is Venti's ascension flower and grows almost nowhere else. Also one of the densest Crystalfly routes in the game. Both respawn every ~48h.
7
Windrise — Dandelion Seeds Hit a dandelion with Anemo to pop the seed. Needed by Jean, Barbara, Klee and several weapons.
8
Anemo Hypostasis — Stormbearer Point (just off the top edge) Drops Hurricane Seeds, required to ascend every Anemo character. Resin-gated, not weekly.

Worth a detour

9
Thousand Winds Temple ruins — Ruin Guards Hardest early enemy, best early artifact drops. Shoot the glowing orange core on its back or chest with a charged bow shot to stagger it.

Chests

There are several hundred chests in this view alone. Use the official HoYoLAB Interactive Map: filter to chests, and clicking one marks it collected against your account. That's the only sane way to clear a region.
★
Shrines of Depths — the only chests genuinely worth hunting Each is a guaranteed Luxurious Chest: ~40 Primogems and a 4-star artifact, one time only. 10 across Mondstadt, and they do not show on the in-game map. They need cyan Mondstadt Shrine Keys — marker 1 hands you one, as do Temple of the Falcon, Temple of the Lion and Eagle's Gate, plus 3 from Adventure Rank Ascension 1.
⌖
Anemo Treasure Compass Points at the nearest unopened chest, 30s cooldown. Forged from a diagram given at Mondstadt Reputation Level 6. Reputation itself unlocks at AR 25.

Where chests actually come from

·
Clear any enemy camp Every hilichurl camp, Treasure Hoarder camp and Fatui group drops a chest when the last one dies. Most of your chest income early.
·
Follow the Seelie Chase it to its Seelie Court and it unlocks a chest.
·
Elemental monuments Hit all the pillars with the matching element within the time limit.
·
Time trials A floating cyan hourglass. Clear the checkpoints before the clock runs out.
·
Unlit braziers Light every torch in a cluster with Pyro.
·
Things that look wrong A suspicious rock pile, a lone stump, a stack of crates. Genshin rewards poking at scenery.
Reality check on value: Common chests give 0–2 Primogems, Exquisite 2–5, Precious ~5–10, and Mondstadt sits at the low end. Chests are for Adventure EXP, Mora, Anemo Sigils and early artifacts — not for pulls. 100 chests opened is an Adventurer's Handbook objective that pays out a Shrine Key, so it does loop back.

Scions of the Canopy — Route 1

17 Saurians · start at the Tecicpac Peak waypoint

Eight sit together at the water’s edge southeast of the waypoint. If you only have five minutes a day, do those eight and leave. The map finds a tighter cluster still — 28 around Tezcatepetonco Range, so look at it rather than read about it.

  1. 8 near the water’s edge and the cliff southeast of the waypoint.
  2. Climb the cliff: 1 to the north, 1 to the northeast.
  3. 2 under the southern cliff.
  4. Head east (or follow the river) to 4 on the cliff near the bridge.
  5. Go to the wooden scaffolding north, 2 in the valley below it.

Rules that actually change your plan

Level gate
Only Saurians at Lv. 60 or above drop Tyrant’s Fang. Below that you get Juvenile and Seasoned only — still progress, they craft upward.
✓ Cleared: World Level 5 as of 30 Oct 2026, putting overworld Saurians around Lv. 69.
Crafting
3 Seasoned Fang → 1 Tyrant’s Fang at any Alchemy table, for a small Mora fee. Never leave Seasoned Fangs in the bag when you’re short.
Respawn
Overworld Saurians reset on the daily server reset (04:00 server time). This is a multi-day farm — run your best route once a day.
Shop top-up
The Stardust Exchange in Paimon’s Bargains sells the base tier only — Juvenile Fang, 3 for 5 Masterless Stardust, on a small monthly cap that resets on the 1st. Worth a couple of Tyrant’s Fang a month, never the bulk.
Type follows tribe
Each species clusters near its own tribe. Fangs are shared across all species, so farm whichever region is densest, not whichever species you like.

Reputation

The compass is the only reputation reward worth rushing — it finds chests for you, which pays back every hour you spend on it. This is the shortest path to each one.

The thing that changes your plan

Weekly reputation is shared, not per region

Three bounties and three requests per week — in total, across every region and tribe, not three each. So you cannot grind Mondstadt and Inazuma in parallel; splitting the week between two regions halves the rate for both. Pick one region, finish its compass, then move on.

Claiming a bounty’s reward instantly refreshes the slot everywhere except Natlan, and only the hardest bounties are worth taking — 200 EXP against 120 for the easiest, for roughly the same time.

The shortest path, in order

  1. Clear that region’s world quests first. They pay 100 EXP each regardless of how long they take, so the short ones are pure profit and there is no weekly cap on them. Liyue alone has 57.
  2. Claim exploration at 20%, 40% and 60%. 100 EXP and 20,000 Mora each, one-time, and you have probably already earned several without claiming them.
  3. Archon quests count too — 200 EXP each in their region, which you likely already have banked.
  4. Only then grind weekly. Three bounties at 200 and three requests at 80, on one region at a time.

Correction — this page used to claim otherwise

The shortest quests are already ranked, elsewhere

This page previously said no per-quest length data existed and that any “shortest first” ranking would be guesswork. That was wrong. The Genshin notes project measures it from the wiki: the numbered objective count and the dialogue length of every quest, pulled from each quest’s own page.

172 world quests across Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru and Fontaine are ranked shortest first, tiered from “Very short” upward, and 147 of them carry a start-location map showing exactly where the quest begins. That is the list to work from for a compass, not anything here.

It lives in D:\Work\Genshin impact project — open quest-map.html for the interactive version (every quest on the live game map with teleports; click a dot for the NPC, length and start screenshot), or reputation-quests.html for the ranked list. Run tools\serve.cmd there, or the genshin-notes launch entry.

What still can’t be mapped

Reputation bounties and requests cannot be mapped. They rotate weekly from a per-region pool and have no fixed coordinates, which is why they appear in none of the 793 categories the official map tracks. World quests can be mapped, and are — though those pins are unnamed, which is why the wiki-sourced per-quest maps in the notes project are the better tool for picking a quest.

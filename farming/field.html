Observed in game — not in any guide

Fangs drop outside Natlan too

Owl-shaped Saurians (Iktomisaurus / Iktomisaurus Chick) at Lv. 69–70 near Kamisato Estate, Inazuma drop Tyrant’s Fang directly, alongside Juvenile Fang, at a good rate.

Confirmed in single player, own world — so this is a real, repeatable spawn, not a co-op guest’s map. Every published guide says “Natlan Saurians only,” and the official interactive map holds 946 Saurian pins with none outside Natlan. Both are behind the game. The Lv. 60+ gate holds; the region claim does not.

Blue name = 3★ = Tyrant’s Fang. Grey = 1★ Juvenile. Green = 2★ Seasoned.

Fishing spots

Gold pin = the best single spot in that region. Blue = solid alternatives.

Genshin's in-game map doesn't mark fishing spots at all. There's no icon to look for — go to the coordinates below and watch for ripples on the water with fish circling.

Mondstadt

Stormbearer lake alone yields Medaka, Aizen Medaka, Crystalfish, Golden Koi, Tea-Colored Shirakodai, Dawncatcher and Venomspine Fish — the widest spread in the region. Start here.

Also in Mondstadt but off this view: the coast south of Dawn Winery (pufferfish + Akai Maou) — see the Windwail map. Plus two spots in Stormterror's Lair and one south of Wyrmrest Valley in Dragonspine.

Liyue — the farming route

The route: Qingce Village (3 spots in walking distance — Sweet-Flower Medaka, Crystalfish, Bitter Pufferfish, Brown Shirakodai, Betta, Dawncatcher) → Luhua Pool (Akai Maou, Rusty Koi, Golden Koi) → Liyue Harbor. The last two sit south of this view.

Mt. Aocang doubles as your ore route — see 07-ore-farming.md.
Day/night matters more than location. Every Redrot Bait fish — Lunged Stickleback, Betta, Venomspine Fish, Akai Maou, Snowstrider — appears only at night. Each spot has separate day and night pools, so a "cleared" spot is usually half full. Once both are exhausted it's a 72-hour respawn.
Why fish at all: The Catch — a 4★ polearm and the best F2P option for Xiangling — comes from Kujirai Momiji in Inazuma. You have 0 Electroculi, so park that goal until Inazuma is open.

Mondstadt's vendor is Nantuck, just outside the city — he sells the Redrot, False Worm and Fake Fly bait blueprints for 3 Medaka each.

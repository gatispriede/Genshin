Wuwang Hill summit — 8 statues

"The highest point of Qingce Village." Order does not matter — you just need the right four.

Activate these four Leave alone
Activate: N · SE · S · SW. Any order. The other four stay untouched.
How to find north in-game: your minimap (top-left) has an N marker on its rim. Rotate your camera until that N sits at the top of the minimap — you are now facing north and the diagram lines up with what's in front of you: north statue far side, south statue nearest you.

The full map screen (M) is always north-up, so you can orient from that too.

Alternative if you can't tell direction

Find the statue facing the climbable stone cliff — call that #1. Count clockwise, then activate 2, 3, 5 and 8.

The compass version above is tied to the riddle's landmarks, so it's the safer of the two.

Getting up there

Go behind the village elder's shrine in Qingce for a set of ladders, climb those, then free-climb the cliff face to the top. Take stamina food, or grab Anemograna on the way for the −30% climbing stamina.

You'll know you're there: water, glowing orange crystals, and the rune marker in the middle giving you the riddle.

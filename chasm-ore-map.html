The Chasm — ore

One of the richest mining regions in the game. The surface is decent; the Underground Mines are the real haul.

The Underground Mines are a separate map layer. Switch to it with the layer toggle on your map — the surface barely hints at how much is down there. In order of value:
1 · Main Mining Area — the bulk of the Crystal Chunks, in dense clusters
2 · Serpent's Cave / Ad-Hoc Main Tunnel — two waypoints sit beside chunks. Don't glide into the Ruin Serpent arena by accident
3 · Underground Waterway — the mop-up leg
Take Noelle. Claymores break a node in 3–4 hits; swords and bows take far longer. Geo attacks also shatter ore, so your Geo Traveler doubles up.

Surface bonus: the terraces have "investigate" sparkle spots that yield iron and never show on the map — not even with Ningguang. Poke at anything that glitters.

Respawn is 48–72 hours. Ask a blacksmith in Liyue Harbor which regions have reset before committing to a route.

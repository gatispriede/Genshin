Set

4pc Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City is every guide's first pick: the 2pc refunds 6 energy when a party member triggers a Nightsoul Burst, the 4pc gives the party a 12% Elemental DMG Bonus for 15s after she triggers a reaction. Only one Scroll 4pc buff applies at a time, so she and Valeriy should not both carry it in one team. In mono-Geo teams, 4pc Husk of Opulent Dreams is the alternative.

Weapon

Prototype Starglitter (Lv 60, R1, 33.8% ER) is what gives her 153.8% ER, so keep it. Better picks: Favonius Lance (energy for the team) or Footprint of the Rainbow (DEF substat, +DEF% after her Skill), once her ER is covered. Neither is confirmed owned.

Investment order

  1. Level the Scroll sands and the Geo goblet to +16. Cheapest DEF and Geo DMG on the account.
  2. Collect Scroll flower, plume and circlet to finish the 4pc. Natlan domain, same one as Valeriy's set.
  3. Character level and talents only once she has a team slot. She has none today.

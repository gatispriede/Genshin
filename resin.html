Where resin comes from

Five sources, two of which you already have and one that's locked behind a single quest · checked 1 Nov 2026.

There is no way to farm resin. It arrives on a clock — 1 point every 8 minutes, 180 a day, capped at 200. Everything else on this page is a one-off top-up, not a tap you can open. The only real lever is not letting the cap overflow, and the one unclaimed source you have is the Serenitea Pot.

What the claim screen offers now

At a domain or ley line outcrop you pick how much to spend. Since Version 6.0 "Luna I" the options are:

SpendReward setsResin per set
20 Original Resin1×20
40 Original Resin2×20
1 Condensed Resin3×20
1 Fragile or Transient Resin3×20

Every row is 20 resin per reward set. Condensed Resin buys you travel and loading time, never resin. It is worth crafting when you are farming one domain hard, and worth skipping when you want more independent rolls at the same expected value — which is the case while you are still hunting main stats.

That was true before 6.0 "Luna I", which moved Condensed to 60 resin for triple. The resin-per-reward ratio did not change — only the batch size — so none of the farming advice built on it was wrong. The Fragile/Transient 3× option is only offered when your Condensed Resin count is 0.

Do these, in order

1
Unlock the Serenitea Pot. One quest — A New Star Approaches — and you passed its AR 28 requirement long ago. It is the only repeating resin source you don't have: a Transient Resin every week from the Realm Depot once you reach Trust Rank 6. It also pays roughly 300,000 Mora a week and lets you park an Alchemist's Crafting Bench indoors.
2
Count your Fragile Resin. Inventory → Materials. You climbed AR 33 → 45 in about ten days, and most of those ranks hand one over, so there is probably a pile sitting unspent. At AR 45 domain level IV drops a guaranteed 5★ artifact, which is the first point in this account's life where burning them is clearly worth it.
3
Stop the cap overflowing. 180 a day against a 200 cap means you have to spend about 180 a day or you are throwing it away, and from empty it refills in under 27 hours. Two logins a day covers it; one does not.
4
Keep Crystal Cores coming if you want Condensed Resin. They drop from Crystalflies, which sit in Mondstadt and Liyue — your two most-explored regions. Nothing else gates the craft.
5
Don't buy refills with Primogems. 50 Primogems for 60 resin on the first one looks cheap and the sixth costs 200. Across a month that is wishes you didn't make. The existing advice in research/01-mora-farming.md — never buy resin for Mora — holds for everything else too at this stage.

What it's worth spending on

Costs are verified in reference/verified-facts.md: domains, talent domains and ley lines 20; weekly bosses 30 discounted (3 claims a week, shared across all Trounce Domains) and 60 after; world bosses 40. Condensed Resin does not work on bosses — domains and ley lines only.

Unverified. The Fragile Resin total from Adventure Rank level-ups is quoted as 76 by one source and 79 by another, and the Battle Pass count may have moved since those guides were written.

Furina

5★ Hydro sword · off-field (the game's own role tag) · pulled 12 Nov 2026 · Lv 27/40. Stats read live from the character screen that day.

Her party buff does not read her level, her HP or her ATK. It reads Fanfare stacks and Elemental Burst talent level — nothing else. A Lv 27 Furina and a Lv 90 Furina hand the party the same damage bonus. That makes her burst talent the single highest-return investment on this account, and it is the exact opposite of Varesa, who is Lv 90 and underperforming because her gear is absent.

CRIT Rate matters only because of the sword. Favonius Sword's Windfall needs a CRIT hit to have its 60% chance to make particles. With no crit the passive never fires and you are left with the substat alone. 44.6% is enough; don't chase more.

The burst is the whole character

Let the People Rejoice — 60 Energy · 30s duration · 15s cooldown.

It puts the party into Universal Revelry. Every time a party member's HP rises or falls, Furina gains 1 Fanfare per percentage point of their Max HP, and Fanfare raises the DMG dealt and Incoming Healing Bonus of every nearby party member. Maximum 300 Fanfare.

Party DMG bonus = Fanfare × conversion ratio. The ratio is set by burst talent level, and it more than triples from 1 to 10.

She is C0 and will stay C0. No 5★ on this account has a constellation, so talent 10 is the real ceiling — levels 11–13 need C3 and C5. That is fine: 1 → 10 is already a 3.5× increase in the buff.
To actually reach 300 Fanfare you need HP churn. It counts changes in either direction, so a healer in the party feeds it as much as damage taken does. Barbara is the natural partner — and Furina's own Salon Members drain the active character's HP, which also counts.

Do not build Elemental Mastery on her. EM only affects reactions she personally triggers — and in an Electro-Charged team you specifically want a Lv 90 doing the triggering, not her. Electro-Charged damage scales off the level and EM of whoever applies the second element. EM on Furina buys a trigger you are trying to avoid.

Her Energy Recharge is already past what a 60-cost burst needs, so treat this as headroom to move artifact rolls off ER and onto HP% rather than as a reason to stack more.

The team she belongs in

Varesa (main dps, Electro) · Furina (buff + Hydro) · Barbara (heal — feeds Fanfare) · one Electro (holds the aura)

Measured on 12 Nov: adding her roughly doubled Varesa's damage — at burst talent level 1 and Lv 27. Two things are happening at once:

  • The Fanfare buff, which every party member gets.
  • Hydro application, which turns Varesa's Electro into Electro-Charged.

It is also the first team on this account built entirely from owned characters rather than Imaginarium Theater trials.

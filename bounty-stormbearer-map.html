Bounty — Ruin Guard

"A strange machine … stood up, swinging its two long arms." Near the tower between Stormbearer Mountains and Stormbearer Point.

Note: the circle marks the corridor between the two named areas. Hit Check Map on the bounty in-game for the exact search radius.
Invulnerable to Physical DMG. Un-infused normal attacks do zero. Kaeya swinging raw will achieve nothing.
All Element RES decreased. Every element works and is boosted — bring whoever hits hardest. Skirk is ideal: in Seven-Phase Flash her attacks convert to Cryo, so she bypasses the Physical immunity completely.
Kill it fast: shoot the glowing orange core on its chest or back with a fully-charged aimed shot to stagger it flat, then burst it while it's down. Use Fischl — charged aimed shots deal the character's element, so hers land as Electro rather than Physical.

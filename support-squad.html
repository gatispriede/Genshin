The six you actually play

Barbara · Mika · Sucrose · Alyosha · Kaeya · Chongyun. Live gear read 27 Oct 2026, straight off the Battle Chronicle.

None of these six is a damage dealer. Barbara, Mika and Alyosha are all healers. Sucrose groups. Kaeya and Chongyun apply Cryo. It's a support bench with nobody to support — your actual damage is Varesa, Skirk and Fischl.
The good news: three of these six — Barbara, Mika and Kaeya (or Chongyun) — are exactly the team Skirk's Ascension 4 passive wants. All Hydro/Cryo, three Death's Crossing stacks, plus the party-wide Elemental Skill +1. Drop Skirk into this squad and it becomes the strongest team on the account.

Two farming trips cover four of them

1
Valley of Remembrance Windwail Highland, Mondstadt, near Dawn Winery · AR 25. Drops Maiden Beloved (Barbara) and Viridescent Venerer (Sucrose) from the same room.
2
Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern Minlin, Liyue, north of Mt. Aocang. Drops Noblesse Oblige — the set both Mika and Alyosha want, for the party-wide +20% ATK on Burst.

Which team each belongs to

A
Skirk · Barbara · Mika · Kaeya or Chongyun All Hydro/Cryo. Three Death's Crossing stacks and everyone's Elemental Skill level +1. Barbara freezes to make Void Rifts; Chongyun adds attack speed and Cryo RES shred. This is the strongest team you can field.
B
Varesa · Fischl · Sucrose · Alyosha Double Electro keeps Fischl's energy requirement at 100%. Sucrose groups for Varesa's plunge AoE and shreds Electro RES. Alyosha heals and hands Varesa an ATK buff.
✗
Don't put Sucrose or Alyosha on Skirk's team Anemo and Electro give her no Death's Crossing stack and break the all-Hydro/Cryo passive. They generate Void Rifts, but rifts aren't her bottleneck.

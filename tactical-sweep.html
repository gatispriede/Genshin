Tactical Sweep — Foothills of Wind and Snow

The Grand Defense: Defy the Unjust Judgment · version 7.2 · runs to 16 Dec 2026 · stage entrance at the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp. Checked 10 Nov 2026 against the Fandom event page.

Two things decide your score, and neither is how well you play the boss. The difficulty you pick multiplies the score ×1, ×6 or ×18 — and the trial characters are Lv 90, so the hardest tier is not gated by your own roster. Then Morale 30 gives the whole party +40% ATK. Deploy 2 Knights of Favonius detachments + Bennett alone. That is 10/10 from three units. Bennett is Pyro and a Sword user, so he satisfies three of the five requirements by himself. Klee, Fischl, Prune, Mona, Rosaria and both Joint Contingents stay free for the next node.

That is the whole game of this mode: read the requirement list and find the one unit that ticks the most boxes. Deployed units are busy until the operation resolves, and the tutorial notes that a well-stocked roster can finish events early and hand the unit back sooner — so spending three units where three will do is what keeps the later nodes payable.

Morale — what the thresholds actually give you

MoraleEffect
10A character who has completed "Witch's Homework" triggering an Elemental Reaction unleashes a True DMG shockwave at the opponent. Once every 6s.
20Stormterror fires targeted "Finale" wind bullets for substantial Anemo DMG. Also a reward objective — 40 Primogems.
30With the "Hexerei: Secret Rite" effect on the party, all party members' ATK +40%. Characters who completed "Witch's Homework" also gain extra Support Progress on triggering reactions.

Morale is the running total on that bar, carried into the Boss Hunt. Never start the boss below 30. The difference between 29 and 30 is +40% ATK on every hit.

Score comes from how much of the boss's HP you remove inside the time limit, then gets multiplied. The reward tiers are 6,000 / 18,000 / 36,000. Your best is 11,052 — past the first tier, short of the second. On ×1 you would have to take off thirty-six thousand points' worth of HP to finish the chart; on ×18 the same run clears everything. The tier choice is worth more than any team change.

The boss is a Cryo Regisvine

"Snow-Tombed Aberrant Tree" is the event's name for an Ever-Corroding Ancient Frost Tree — mechanically a Cryo Regisvine. Two consequences:

  • Leave Skirk at home. A Cryo boss resists Cryo. This is the one fight on your account where your second-best carry is the wrong answer.
  • It is a weak-point boss — break the bulb, then the root — and Pyro is the classic counter. Morale 10 rewards triggering reactions, which also points at Pyro and Electro rather than more Cryo.

Use the trial characters

The stage hands you five at Lv 90: Xilonen · Klee · Prune · Fischl · Bennett.

Your own best unit is Varesa at Lv 70 with talents around 1 and a DEF% goblet. These trials are Lv 90 with real kits, and Klee and Bennett are Pyro into a Cryo boss. Take the trial team. This is the rare content where your roster's state does not matter — see 100k on screen for why your own characters are not ready for an enemy-level-90 fight.

Steps, in order

1
Finish both Allies in Action events for this region if you have not — Allies in Action: Klee unlocks Klee / Prune / Rosaria, and Allies in Action: Bennett unlocks Bennett / Fischl / Mona. Your deployment screen already shows all six, so this is done.
2
Clear nodes for the full 10 each. Open each one, read the five requirements, and pick the smallest set of units that ticks all five — favour units whose element and weapon both count.
3
Reach Morale 30 before you touch the Boss Hunt. It unlocks after Round 3, but unlocking it is not a reason to enter it. 30 is +40% party ATK.
4
Enter on the highest difficulty you can actually damage — the ×18 tier if the Lv 90 trial team holds up, ×6 otherwise. Re-running a cleared tier at ×1 cannot reach 36,000 however well you play.
5
In the fight: trial team, Pyro-led, no Cryo. Keep an Elemental Reaction going roughly every 6s so the Morale-10 True DMG shockwave never idles. Break the weak point rather than chipping the body.

Tactical Sweep — all four stages to 36,000

The Version 7.2 flagship event The Grand Defense: Defy the Unjust Judgment, open until 16 Dec 2026, 03:59 (GMT+8). How score works, what each boss needs, and who to field. Merged from the plan and the boss-mechanics pages.

Score is an elemental puzzle, not a stat check. Every boss has a state where your damage multiplies 4–6×, and the gate is always a mechanic: a shield to break, a gauge to fill, a Morale buff to trigger. Pick characters for that, then for level.

What is worth chasing

Primogems sit only at 20 overall Morale and a 6,000 top score per stage. The 18,000 and 36,000 tiers pay Mora, Mystic Enhancement Ore and Sanctifying Unction (2,500 artifact EXP each). The wiki and Game8 disagree on the 36,000 tier's Mora (30,000 vs 60,000); the in-game reward screen settles it.

Four rules that decide the score

  1. Brutal Eradication is already the ceiling. Standard ×1, Crippling Blow ×6, Brutal Eradication ×18 (enemies Lv 90). Past that, only damage raises the score.
  2. Morale comes from the deployment phase. Each round lists objectives (number of members, an element, weapon types, a named Support Squad); harder operations pay more. Deploy the minimum that meets a round and hold back anyone a later round names, because deployed characters are spent.
  3. Field whoever fires the Morale 30 buff, trial or own. On the Natlan stage, swapping in Nightsoul characters took one party from 18,288 to 24,000 with no farming.
  4. Break the shield first. Above 75% RES the damage multiplier is 1 ÷ (4 × RES + 1): a shielded Regisvine takes 18.5% of your damage.

Mondstadt: a switch, not a damage problem

Both Mondstadt Morale buffs need characters who finished Witch's Homework (owned, Lv 70+). The trial lineup (Xilonen, Klee, Prune, Fischl, Bennett) holds exactly two Hexerei, Klee and Fischl, and Klee + Bennett give double Pyro for the shield. Unverified: that trial characters count as having done Witch's Homework; one run tells you.

Sumeru: fill the Fury bar

Quicken-family reactions add 15% Fury, Electro hits 2%, and a Pyro hit drains 34%. A full bar means one attack, then about 15 s of paralysis at 0% global RES. With the Morale 30 buff on top, Dendro RES goes 80% → −45%, about 6× Dendro damage. The trials have no Electro, so bring your own; trial Tighnari cashes in the Dendro shred.

Inazuma: Geo for the Morale, any reaction for the shield

Breaking the shield paralyses the Vishap and removes its RES boost. Its Cleansing Shower drains 10 Energy from the active character. The Morale 10 shockwave resonates off Geo Constructs for up to two extra True DMG hits, so Itto, Gorou and Yelan (Crystallize) fit; a Geo Traveler's stele may add the construct (untested).

Natlan: kill every feather

The Tyrant spends up to 45 s immune and airborne behind its shield. Destroy every Flamewind Feather (2 GU Pyro objects, break them with reactions) and its Dive leaves it low and open to melee; miss one and it flies back up. A Charged Attack applies −50% all RES for 6 s on a 6 s cooldown, so a charged shot every 6 s holds it permanently (about 1.33× damage). Bows (Chasca, Ororon) do this naturally; Varesa, Chasca and Ororon are Nightsoul and Natlanese, which also puts Nightsoul Burst at its 9 s minimum.

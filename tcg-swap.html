Switching in TCG — what it actually pays

Every Event, Equipment and Support card in Genius Invokation was scanned for switch effects. This is the complete set, quoted rather than summarised, split by whether the card rewards a swap or only discounts one.

Why this matters for your deck. Varesa's Elemental Skill "deals 2 Electro DMG … then switches to the next character," and her passive turns 2 Nightsoul points into Apex Drive: "After Varesa is switched in to be the Active Character: Prepare Skill: Guardian Vent: Volcano Kablam" — 3 Electro DMG for 0 dice. Her damage exists because she leaves the field and comes back. She is the swap bonus; the cards below just let her do it more often.

How to read this

Efficient Switch Your next switch costs 1 less Elemental Die. The cheap currency.
Agile Switch Your next switch is a Fast Action instead of a Combat Action — it does not end your turn. This is the expensive one, and the reason a swap deck can work at all.
"Pays you" The card converts a switch into damage, healing, energy or dice.
"Cheaper only" The card reduces what a switch costs. Still good — just not a bonus.
Your deck has a named counter. Falls and Fortune makes both sides spend 1 extra die to switch for a round. Everything on this page stops working the turn someone plays it, so keep a line of damage that does not need a swap.
Where this came from. The three card-list pages on the Genshin Impact Wiki — 194 Event, 284 Equipment and 289 Support rows — filtered for switch and again for Plunging, since Varesa's free hit counts as a Plunging Attack. Card text is quoted exactly. Balance changes land on this game often, so re-check after a version bump rather than trusting the date. How to get cards at all is on the other TCG page.

Sucrose — Anemo catalyst, grouper and Elemental Mastery battery

She is built for one stat and almost nothing else. Elemental Mastery in every slot — sands, goblet and circlet — because her own damage and her team buff both run off it, and CRIT does close to nothing for her.

Why EM three times over. Her Swirl damage is a transformative reaction, which ignores ATK, CRIT and talent levels entirely and scales only on character level and EM. On top of that her A4 gives every other party member 20% of her EM for 8s, and her A1 adds a flat +50 EM to anyone matching the swirled element. Her EM is a team stat, not a personal one.

Artifacts

Effects are read live from the game data, so they cannot go stale.

A 2pc + 2pc option exists. Instructor and Wanderer's Troupe together give +160 EM with no set bonus — perfectly reasonable while you are still farming a real Viridescent Venerer, and better than four mismatched pieces.

Weapons — 4★ and 3★

Sacrificial Fragments best 4★

Elemental Mastery substat

The premier pick: a large EM substat and a chance to reset her Skill cooldown. The extra cast means more particles, more grouping and a lower Energy Recharge requirement — it solves two of her three problems at once.

Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers if one drops

HP% substat

Hands the next character +48% ATK on switch at R5. A pure support weapon — no EM at all, so take it only if she is buffing an ATK-scaling carry rather than driving reactions. It is a 3★, so it turns up eventually.

Favonius Codex energy fix

Energy Recharge substat

CRIT hits make particles for the team. This is the one case where a CRIT Rate circlet is defensible on her — you need crits for the passive to fire at all.

Magic Guide Owned use this today

Elemental Mastery substat

A 3★ with the exact stat she wants. You own it, and it is genuinely fine — the gap to Sacrificial Fragments is the cooldown reset, not the stat line. Do not leave her on a weapon without EM while this sits in the bag.

Emerald Orb Owned 3★ backup

Elemental Mastery substat

Also EM, slightly lower base ATK. Fine if Magic Guide is on Lisa or Barbara — no reason to fight over them.

The Widsith Owned wrong character

CRIT DMG substat

Your best catalyst by rarity and the wrong one for her. CRIT DMG does nothing for Swirl, and its buff is random on switch-in. Keep it for an on-field catalyst carry — it is a real weapon, just not hers.

What to equip today: Magic Guide, EM sands, EM goblet, EM circlet, and climb toward 160% Energy Recharge in substats before chasing more EM. She works at C0 with a 3★ weapon — this is the cheapest meaningful upgrade available to your Electro and reaction teams.

# Mora Farming

**Researched:** 2026-10-23 · **Game version:** 7.1
**Account context:** AR ~25, World Level low, Liyue Archon Quest in progress

---

## The short answer

**Blossom of Wealth ley line outcrops are the only resin-efficient Mora source.** Everything
else is a supplement. If you need Mora, you spend resin on gold ley lines — there is no
Mora domain and no alternative that competes.

---

## 1. Blossom of Wealth (primary)

The gold cloud icon on the map. Touch the outcrop, kill the enemies, pay **20 Original
Resin** to claim.

**Yield scales with World Level, not Adventure Rank directly.**

| World Level | Mora per 20 resin |
|---|---|
| WL 6+ (AR 45+) | ~60,000 — the ceiling |
| Mid WL | ~52,000–60,000 |
| **Low WL (where you are)** | **Substantially less** |

> ⚠️ The 60,000 figure quoted all over the internet is the **WL 6+** number. At your
> current World Level you will see a fraction of that. Don't budget around 60k yet.
> The WL5 → WL6 jump is the last big increase, which makes **AR 45 the real sweet spot**.

**Unlocks at Adventure Rank 12.** You have this.

### Resin options
- **20 Original Resin** — baseline.
- **1 Condensed Resin** — doubles rewards for one run. Same Mora *per resin*, but half the
  travel time. Worth it once you're farming seriously.
- **Fragile / Transient Resin** — since 5.7 these can be spent directly for **3× rewards**.
  Most time-efficient way to burn a stockpile.

### Battle Pass interaction
Two different BP missions touch ley lines and they count differently:

| Mission | Requires resin? |
|---|---|
| "Complete Ley Line **deposit challenges** N times" | **No** — the fight alone counts. Kill and walk away. |
| "**Collect** N rewards from blossoms of wealth or revelation" | **Yes** — counts the claim, so you must pay. |

Optimal play: fight at **every** outcrop you find (free progress on the first), but only
pay the 20 resin at **Wealth** outcrops (progresses the second *and* gives Mora).

Do **not** use Condensed Resin for the collect mission — it counts claims, so Condensed
costs 40 resin for the same single tick.

---

## 2. Ley Line Overflow (the best window)

A recurring event that **doubles** ley line rewards a limited number of times per day.

At WL 6–8: 60,000 → **120,000 Mora** per claim, 3 doubled claims/day = **360,000 Mora/day
for 60 resin**, around **2.5 million** across a full seven-day event.

**Important:** the double only triggers on **Original Resin**. Condensed Resin does *not*
get the bonus. Claim your doubles with raw resin first, then craft Condensed afterwards
for domains.

**Action:** when Ley Line Overflow appears in the events tab, drop everything and farm it.
It is the single biggest Mora window in the game.

---

## 3. Resin-free supplements

These cost time, not resin. Community estimates — treat as rough.

| Source | Rough yield | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Artifact destruction | 20k–50k/day | Feed junk artifacts into the Destroy option |
| Expeditions | ~25k | Set 4-hour/20-hour expeditions every login. Free. |
| Elite enemy routes | 40k–50k per ~20 min | Ruin Guards, Mitachurls, Abyss Mages |
| Chest collection | Varies | Already part of your exploration |
| Daily commissions | Modest but guaranteed | Do these anyway for AR |
| Weekly bounties | Modest | Reputation-gated |
| Spiral Abyss | Lump sums | Needs AR 20+ and a built team |

Combined, these can total **100,000+ Mora/day without resin** — but that is a full-time
grind, not a casual session.

### Battle Pass
**720,000 Mora at Battle Level 49.** Significant lump sum. Worth pushing BP missions.

### Serenitea Pot
Roughly **300,000 Mora weekly** via conversion once the Pot is running and furnished.
Locked behind *A New Star Approaches* + AR 28. *(This line used to point at

## 4. What NOT to do

- **Never spend Primogems on resin refills for Mora.** Worst conversion rate in the game.
- **Don't sell artifacts.** Destroy them instead — you get Mora *and* EXP for upgrading
  the keepers. Selling gives Mora only.
- **Don't buy Mora with Stardust** unless you have a surplus. Stardust is better spent on
  Intertwined Fates.
  - *Niche exception:* EXP ley lines give slightly better Stardust efficiency (65.33 vs
    60 Stardust per resin), so some players farm EXP and buy Mora with the surplus. Not
    worth optimising at your stage.

## 5. Your action plan right now

1. **Push AR.** Every Mora number in this document scales with World Level. AR 45 roughly
   doubles your return on identical effort. Daily commissions are the fastest route.
2. **Spend all resin on Blossoms of Wealth** until your Mora anxiety stops.
3. **Set expeditions every single login.** Free, and you're already opening the game.
4. **Destroy junk artifacts** rather than selling them.
5. **Watch for Ley Line Overflow** in the events tab.

### Known one-off injections
- **Frostbearing Tree, Dragonspine: 600,000 Mora** — 50,000 per offering level × 12,
  plus 120 Mystic Enhancement Ore, 2,400 Adventure EXP, 10 Hero's Wit and a Crown of
  Insight. **The largest one-off on this list, and the largest resin-free lump short of
  the Battle Pass.** Costs **110 Crimson Agates**; only 80 exist in the world, so the
  last 30 come from Crimson Wish at 5 per Mon/Fri reset. Dragonspine is **5.9% explored**,
  so essentially all of it is unclaimed. Full reward breakdown in
  `02-wishes-and-primogems.md` — it pays **no Primogems**, which is why it is filed here.
  *Checked 2026-11-08.*
- *Guizhong* quest chain (Liyue Act II): **24,250 Mora**
- Archon Quest chains generally: 10k–25k per act
- Blossom collect BP mission: **25,000 Mora** on completion

# Artifact Sets — Damage · Support · Utility

**Researched:** 2026-10-24 · **Game version:** 7.1

There are ~59 sets in the game. This covers the ones that matter for **your roster**.
For the full table, use [Icy Veins' artifact database](https://www.icy-veins.com/genshin-impact/artifacts)
or [genshin-build.com/artifacts](https://genshin-build.com/artifacts).

> ⚠️ **Sets get reworked.** Thundering Fury was changed in the 7.x Lunar-reaction update.
> Numbers marked **(stable)** have held for years; numbers marked **(verify)** are worth a
> glance in-game before you commit resin.

(Crit Rate), Martial Artist, Brave Heart, Gambler (Skill DMG). Usable as **2-piece ATK%**
stopgaps; never worth 4-piece.

# 2 · SUPPORT SETS

Worn by someone whose job is improving *other* characters' output or survival.

| Set | 2-Piece | 4-Piece | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Viridescent Venerer** ✅ | Anemo DMG **+15%** | Swirl DMG **+60%**, and the swirled element's **RES −40%** for 10s | **The strongest support set in the game.** Sucrose, Lynette |
| **Deepwood Memories** ✅ | Dendro DMG **+15%** | Enemy **Dendro RES −30%** for 8s — works **off-field** | Tighnari, Collei |
| **Noblesse Oblige** | Burst DMG +20% *(stable)* | After Burst, **whole party ATK +20%** for 12s *(stable)* | Any burst support — Barbara, Mika, Xiangling |
| **Maiden Beloved** | Healing effectiveness +15% *(stable)* | +20% healing received for 10s after Skill/Burst *(stable)* | Barbara, Mika |
| **Ocean-Hued Clam** | Healing effectiveness +15% *(verify)* | Converts healing into damage *(verify)* | Barbara |
| **Instructor** *(3★)* | Elemental Mastery +80 *(stable)* | After a reaction, **party-wide EM +120** for 8s *(stable)* | Cheap, genuinely useful on reaction supports |
| **The Exile** *(3★)* | Energy Recharge +20% *(stable)* | Burst restores energy to the party *(stable)* | Budget battery |

> **Instructor and The Exile are 3★ sets that punch far above their rarity.** If you find
> them while farming, keep them for supports — they stay relevant for a long time.

# 4 · WHAT TO COLLECT, PER CHARACTER

Tiers: **S** = best-in-slot · **A** = strong · **B** = good interim · **Now** = what to use today

### Skirk — Cryo main DPS ⭐ *your priority*
| Tier | Set |
|---|---|
| **S** | **4pc Finale of the Deep Galleries** — her burst uses Serpent's Subtlety, so her Elemental Energy is **permanently 0** and the 4pc is **always active** |
| **A** | 4pc Blizzard Strayer — +40% Crit Rate vs frozen, and Barbara freezes for you |
| **B** | **2pc Finale + 2pc Blizzard Strayer = +30% Cryo DMG** — far easier than either 4pc |
| **Now** | Any **Cryo DMG% goblet** + **Crit DMG circlet**, regardless of set |

Main stats: **ATK% sands · Cryo DMG goblet · Crit DMG circlet**.
**No Elemental Mastery. No Energy Recharge.**

### Fischl — Electro off-field sub-DPS
| Tier | Set |
|---|---|
| **S** | **4pc Golden Troupe** — Oz is summoned by her **Elemental Skill** and fights **off-field**, so both halves of the 4pc apply |
| **A** | 2pc Thundering Fury + 2pc ATK% |
| **Now** | Any **Electro DMG goblet**. ~~Her real problem is 284 ATK.~~ *Updated 27 Oct: she's at **852 ATK** on Rust Lv 50 — the weapon is fine now. Her problem is **five 3-star artifact pieces** and **7.1% CRIT Rate**.* |

### Varesa — Electro plunge DPS ⭐ *highest level on the account*
| Tier | Set |
|---|---|
| **S** | **4pc Long Night's Oath** ✅ **confirmed and already equipped.** She is a plunge DPS; the set gives up to **+100% Plunging Attack DMG** (25% from 2pc + 75% from five 4pc stacks) |
| **A** | 2pc ATK% + 2pc Electro DMG |
| **Now** | 🔴 **Replace her DEF% goblet.** Target **Electro DMG%**; anything beats DEF% on a DPS. Then level her three +0 pieces. |

### Barbara — Hydro healer
**S:** 4pc Noblesse Oblige (party ATK +20% — helps Skirk) · **A:** Maiden Beloved
Main stats: **HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus**. Never ATK.

### Mika — Cryo HP-scaling healer
**S:** Noblesse Oblige · **A:** Maiden Beloved · Main stats: **HP% throughout**

### Sucrose / Lynette — Anemo
**S:** **4pc Viridescent Venerer.** No alternative. The −40% elemental RES shred is the
best support effect in the game.

### Xiangling — Pyro sub-DPS ⭐ *build her*
**S:** 4pc Emblem of Severed Fate (her burst is everything) · **A:** Crimson Witch
Main stats: **ER or ATK% sands · Pyro DMG goblet · Crit circlet**

### Lisa / Beidou / Alyosha — Electro
**S:** Thundering Fury (reworked, strong) · **A:** Emblem of Severed Fate · **B:** Instructor

### Tighnari / Collei — Dendro
**S:** 4pc Deepwood Memories

### Noelle — Geo
**S:** Husk of Opulent Dreams · **Now:** keep her for **exploration** (claymore for ore and
Ancient Rime), not combat

# 5 · FARMING ORDER

*Updated 2026-10-27 for AR 40.*

1. **Derelict Masonry Dock** (Atocpan, Natlan) — ⭐ **everything you need is here.**
   📍 [`derelict-masonry-dock.html`](../derelict-masonry-dock.html). It
   drops **Finale of the Deep Galleries** (Skirk) *and* **Long Night's Oath** (Varesa),
   and at AR 40 it drops **5★ pieces**. Both sets are already equipped, so you are
   farming for **main stats**: a **Cryo DMG goblet** and an **Electro DMG goblet**.
2. **World bosses** — 40 resin, and at AR 40 they drop 5★ artifacts *plus* the ascension
   gems you need. Best resin-per-purpose on the account.
3. **Midsummer Courtyard** (Mondstadt, AR 22) — 2pc Thundering Fury for Fischl. Cheap,
   low priority.
4. ~~Everything else waits for AR 45.~~ **At AR 45** you get domain level IV (a
   *guaranteed* 5★ per run) and the **Artifact Strongbox**. Hoard spare 5★ pieces at
   **+0** from now on as Strongbox fuel.
5. **Peak of Vindagnyr** (Dragonspine) — Blizzard Strayer. **No longer needed for Skirk.**
   Do *In the Mountains* for the Cryo Hypostasis (Shivada Jade) instead.

> **The rule that still holds:** at your level, **correct main stat beats set bonus.**
> A random Cryo DMG goblet on Skirk outperforms a matched 4-piece with wrong main stats.

# Snezhnaya — Traversal & Ice Mechanics

**Researched:** 2026-10-23 · **Game version:** 7.1 · Region: Snezhnaya (Everfrozen Earth etc.)

---

## Prime Ice Constructs — the regional toolkit

Unlocked by the **Eye of Graeae** gadget, given when you first arrive in Snezhnaya.
Open the construct menu with **T** (default).

**Works only inside Snezhnaya.** It deactivates the moment you leave the region.

### The two construct types

| Construct | Use |
|---|---|
| **Ice Cube** | Obstruction or foothold — hold doors open, block mechanisms, build platforms. Can be shaped and resized. |
| **Basic Heat Source** | **Melts ice formations**, revealing hidden chests and passages |

### Costs and resources
- A Basic Heat Source costs roughly **25 Prime Ice + 40% energy**
- **Short on Prime Ice?** Dismantle your existing structures, or special ice blocks in the world
- **Energy** comes from interacting with heat sources — **Kresnik torches** are the main refill

## Melting ice (the common blocker)

Ice walls and ice-encased objects are everywhere. Two ways through:

1. **Basic Heat Source** — the efficient way
2. **Pyro attack** — works, but the game itself flags it as **less efficient**

### How to actually use it
1. Open the construct view (**T**), switch to **Basic Heat Source** in Fab-Vision
2. **Place it near the ice** you want melted
3. Or use **"Take Heat Source"** to **pick it up and carry it** to ice elsewhere
4. Just **stand near the ice while holding it** — it melts passively
5. **If the heat runs out**, go back to the source and take it again
6. **If it's too far from the target**, pick it up and reposition closer

> You only need to melt **enough to pass or reach**, not the entire block. Don't waste
> energy fully clearing a large formation.

### Already-placed heat sources count
If you find a **Basic Heat Source** sitting in the world, you don't need to build one —
**"Take Heat Source"** lets you carry that one to your ice.

## Other ice interactions

- **Heavy / plunging attacks shatter some ice surfaces**, creating large holes. Useful with
  a claymore — **Noelle** — or any plunge.
- **Puzzle use:** some puzzles (e.g. Enraged Jack Frost) require melting Cubes down to
  *specific sizes*, dismantling at the right moment, then building a new Cube to mirror
  opposing frames. Precision matters, not just brute melting.

## The cold gauge

Snezhnaya has a Dragonspine-style cold mechanic, but inverted in presentation: you have a
temperature meter and **at 0% you start taking damage**.

Refill by interacting with:
- **Kresnik torches**
- Heat particles
- Warming crystals

Same instinct as Dragonspine — never be far from a heat source.

## Gatherables worth knowing

| Item | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Red Beet** | Everfrozen Earth — densest **between Morepesok and the Inverted Glacier Domain**; also **Okurov** | Looks like **small blue petals** on snow; the red root is buried. 100 total in Snezhnaya. **No vendor sells it.** |
| Rimecurrant | Snezhnaya | Weapon diagram material |
| Snowball Flower | Snezhnaya | Weapon diagram material |
| Rainbowdrop Crystal | Snezhnaya | Weapon diagram material |

**Valley of Fire Feathers** holds ~28 Red Beets in clusters, but needs the quest
***In the Abode of Life*** partially completed before they appear.

**Permafrost-area Expeditions** return Red Beet passively — set them before logging off.

### What they're for
Weapon **diagrams** from **Kuzmichev** at the **Kuznets Smithy, Snezhnograd**. Snezhnaya
added five forgeable 4-stars in 7.0.

| Weapon | Type | Lv90 stats |
|---|---|---|
| **Blade of Atonement** | **Claymore** | 564.8 ATK · **27.6% ATK%** |
| **Emberwell** | **Sword** ⚠️ *(not a catalyst)* | 510 ATK · **165 Elemental Mastery** |
| **Echoes of the Heart** | Catalyst | 565 ATK · 27.6% ATK% |
| Song of the Vigil | Polearm | — |
| Covenant of Frost and Snow | Bow | — |

Passives are similar in shape: triggering an **Elemental Reaction** grants EM or ATK%, and
triggering a **Stellar Glimmer** reaction grants ATK% or Stellar Glimmer DMG, for 12s.
They work **off-field**.

> ⚠️ **Sources disagree on the diagram material lists.** One gives Blade of Atonement as
> Rainbowdrop Crystal ×10 / Snowball Flower ×10 / Red Beet ×10; another lists entirely
> different materials. **Read the actual cost at Kuzmichev before farming for it.**

### The diagram is the cheap part
Forging is where the real cost sits. Blade of Atonement needs:
**500 Mora + Borderland Claymore Billet + 50 Rainbowdrop Crystal + 50 White Iron Chunk.**

That **White Iron Chunk** requirement links straight to
[`07-ore-farming.md`](07-ore-farming.md) — Mt. Aocang has ~21 nodes.

### Verdict for this account
**Neither Red Beet diagram is a priority.**
- **Emberwell's 165 EM is wrong for Skirk** — her damage is direct Cryo, not reaction damage
  (see [`05-current-loadouts.md`](05-current-loadouts.md))
- **Blade of Atonement** has the better statline, but your only claymore users are Noelle
  (who scales on DEF) and Chongyun at Lv 10
- **Echoes of the Heart** — the catalyst that would actually help Varesa — **needs no Red Beet**

Your real weapon problem is **underlevelled weapons**, not missing ones. Mystic Enhancement
Ore fixes that; a new diagram doesn't.

See [`redbeet-map.html`](../redbeet-map.html) for the marked search corridor.

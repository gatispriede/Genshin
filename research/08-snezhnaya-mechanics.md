# Snezhnaya — Traversal & Ice Mechanics

**Researched:** 2026-10-23 · **Game version:** 7.1 · Region: Snezhnaya (Everfrozen Earth etc.)
**Shadow Realm section added:** 2026-11-07 against **7.2.0** (the installed build, read from `config.ini`)

---

## Prime Ice Constructs — the regional toolkit

Unlocked by the **Eye of Graeae** gadget, given when you first arrive in Snezhnaya.
Open the construct menu with **T** (default).

**Works only inside Snezhnaya.** It deactivates the moment you leave the region.

### The two construct types

| Construct | Use |
|---|---|
| **Ice Cube** | Obstruction or foothold — hold doors open, block mechanisms, build platforms. Can be shaped and resized. |
| **Basic Heat Source** | **Melts ice formations**, revealing hidden chests and passages |

### Costs and resources
- A Basic Heat Source costs roughly **25 Prime Ice + 40% energy**
- **Short on Prime Ice?** Dismantle your existing structures, or special ice blocks in the world
- **Energy** comes from interacting with heat sources — **Kresnik torches** are the main refill

## Melting ice (the common blocker)

Ice walls and ice-encased objects are everywhere. Two ways through:

1. **Basic Heat Source** — the efficient way
2. **Pyro attack** — works, but the game itself flags it as **less efficient**

### How to actually use it
1. Open the construct view (**T**), switch to **Basic Heat Source** in Fab-Vision
2. **Place it near the ice** you want melted
3. Or use **"Take Heat Source"** to **pick it up and carry it** to ice elsewhere
4. Just **stand near the ice while holding it** — it melts passively
5. **If the heat runs out**, go back to the source and take it again
6. **If it's too far from the target**, pick it up and reposition closer

> You only need to melt **enough to pass or reach**, not the entire block. Don't waste
> energy fully clearing a large formation.

### Already-placed heat sources count
If you find a **Basic Heat Source** sitting in the world, you don't need to build one —
**"Take Heat Source"** lets you carry that one to your ice.

---

## Other ice interactions

- **Heavy / plunging attacks shatter some ice surfaces**, creating large holes. Useful with
  a claymore — **Noelle** — or any plunge.
- **Puzzle use:** some puzzles (e.g. Enraged Jack Frost) require melting Cubes down to
  *specific sizes*, dismantling at the right moment, then building a new Cube to mirror
  opposing frames. Precision matters, not just brute melting.

## The cold gauge

Snezhnaya has a Dragonspine-style cold mechanic, but inverted in presentation: you have a
temperature meter and **at 0% you start taking damage**.

Refill by interacting with:
- **Kresnik torches**
- Heat particles
- Warming crystals

Same instinct as Dragonspine — never be far from a heat source.

## The Shadow Realm — Snezhnaya's shooter mode

Unlocked by the Archon Quest ***Gunfire in the Silent Lands***. Inside it the game
becomes a **third-person shooter**: Visions don't work in the Shadow Realm, which is
the in-world reason Snezhnaya invented **firearms**.

Also applies to **Pale Crown Palace**, **Flamefeather Valley** and **Old Kitezhgrad**.
Exploring **Flamefeather Valley** and **White Birch Snowgrave** drops you into a Shadow
Realm variant automatically — chests and oculi are still there, but **Elemental Sight is
disabled**, so bring the **Cryo Treasure Compass** or you will walk past them.

### What your account is inside it

**Your build does not matter here.** The Traveler is given fixed stats:

| | |
|---|---|
| HP | 3,000 |
| ATK | 1,000 |
| DEF | 600 |
| Stamina | 200 (+40 per Analyzer level, to level 5) |
| Reactions | Lv 20 base damage values |

**Enemy levels are static across all World Levels.** No food, no Elemental Sight, no
some gadgets. Prime Ice constructs *do* work — except in Edge of Survival.

This is the one piece of Snezhnaya content that **ignores how underlevelled this account
is**. Everything else in the region scales against you; this doesn't.

### Guns

Start with **Shatanaya's Frostsilver** (assault rifle) and **Balsag's Sunwheel**
(grenade launcher). **Ashamez's Thunder**, **Sosruko's Flame** and **Apsat's Mountain
Hunt** unlock through specific World Quests and vaults. Carry **two mains + one frag
crystal type**; swap loadout through the **Eye of Graeae**'s Fab-Vision.

Ammo comes from boxes in the world — **blue-tipped arrow = 75 rifle rounds, yellow disc
= 6 grenades, red spike = 1 frag crystal**. Boxes respawn on a short timer in the large
realms, but are **one-time-use in mini realms and in Edge of Survival**.

Some enemies spawn behind a barrier that blocks all damage. **Grab the highlighted frag
crystal and throw it at them** — crystals respawn if you miss.

### Mini Shadow Realms — the part that pays

Listed in the **Snezhnaya Adventure Journal** (**U**), and visible on the minimap as
**dark purple mist** while uncleared, with an Energy Commission official standing
outside. Enter through the rift, kill everything, fight the boss at the end.

**First clear of each pays 20 Primogems, 10,000 Mora and an Incomplete Data Block.**

There are **five**: Facility, Floating Base, Maintenance Center, Ice Quarry and Ruins.
The count is corroborated three ways — five bosses listed, five Edge of Survival stages
each unlocked by one, and the **Shadow Realm Vault Access Card caps at level 6** starting
from level 1, so exactly **five Data Blocks** are consumable.

### Edge of Survival — the biggest payer

Endgame scored mode, reached through the Shooting Range in your train. Four difficulties
— Normal (levels 1–2), Advancing (3–5, score ×2.5 / enemy HP ×2.0), Hard (6–8, ×5.0 /
×3.0), Extreme (9, ×10.0 / ×4.5). **Extreme needs 300,000 points banked on a lower
difficulty first.** Leftover time converts at **1 second = 1,000 points**, and kill
streaks pay a bonus.

Picking more **Difficulty Clauses** before a run raises enemy HP, ATK and skills, and
**raises the points each kill is worth** — that is the lever for reaching the high tiers,
not grinding the same run repeatedly.

**Each stage runs its own score ladder**, claimed in the *Challenge Rewards* tab:

| Challenge Score | Medal | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| 50,000 | 1 | **Primogem ×15** · Mora 20,000 · Mystic Enhancement Ore ×3 |
| 200,000 | 1 | **Primogem ×15** · Mora 20,000 · Mystic Enhancement Ore ×3 |
| 400,000 | 1 | Mora 20,000 · **Fragile Resin ×1** · Mystic Enhancement Ore ×3 |
| 600,000 | 1 | Mora 20,000 · Sanctifying Unction ×3 · Mystic Enhancement Ore ×3 |

So **30 Primogems per stage**, plus 4 medals, 80,000 Mora and **12 Mystic Enhancement
Ore**. Across the five documented stages that is **150 Primogems and 60 Mystic
Enhancement Ore** — and Ore is the material this file already identifies as this
account's real weapon bottleneck, so the Ore may matter more than the Primogems.

**Medals buy cosmetics, not currency:** 1 → Ashamez's Thunder gunstock, 3 → Balsag's
Sunwheel sight, 6 → *Keen Edge* avatar frame, 9 → Balsag's gunstock, 12 → *Stardust
Trail* cape, 15 → *Siege Breach* frame. Claim gun parts at the shooting range trophy
case. The **Ace of Survival** achievement (20 Primogems) wants **9 medals**, so it needs
200,000+ on at least three stages.

> ⚠️ **Open: five stages or six?** The wiki documents five — Formation Breaker, Frigid
> Storm, Close-Quarter Frenzy, Fiery Feather Pursuit, Ice-Imprisoned Citadel — but the
> in-game stage selector (**Q**/**E**) shows **six slots**. If there is a sixth, add
> **30 Primogems**. *30-second check: press E through the selector and count.*

### What it's actually worth in Primogems

| Source | Primogems |
|---|---|
| **Edge of Survival score ladders** — 5 stages × 30 | **150** |
| *Land of Surging Shadows (I)* — 15 achievements | **140** |
| Mini Shadow Realm first clears — 5 × 20 | **100** |
| *Land of Surging Shadows (II)* — 7 achievements | **45** |
| *Bounty of the Shadow Realm: Snow Blanketed Nation* (I) + (II) | **20** |
| ***The Shooting Range and the Deep Shadow Realm*** (the only quest that pays) | **20** |
| **Reachable total** | **475** |
| Shadow Realm Analyzer, final tier | *30 — see below* |
| A sixth Edge of Survival stage, if it exists | *30 — see above* |

Plus two namecards, *Enigmatic Core* and *Stillness*.

**The Analyzer's 30 Primogems may not be reachable yet.** Levelling it needs **Shadow
Wingdrops** (bat icon on the minimap, one hit to drop, deposited at the Analyzer in your
train). The final tier wants **72 cumulative**, and the wiki records only **58 obtainable
as of 7.0** — enough for level 5 with 6 spare. Whether 7.1/7.2 added more is **unverified**.
Levels 1–5 pay no Primogems but do pay **Crown of Insight ×1, Fragile Resin ×1, Acquaint
Fate ×1, Sanctifying Essence ×5 and 180,000 Mora**, which is worth the detour regardless.

> ⚠️ **The three-part *Remnants of the Shadow Realm* chain pays zero Primogems.** Mora
> 20,000, Hero's Wit ×2 and 150 Adventure EXP per part. Do it for the **Firearm Accessory
> Archive** it unlocks, not for currency.

### Gates on this account

- **Shadow Realm Treasure Vaults need AR 45.** The 30 Oct snapshot has this account at
  **AR 40**, so the 20 Primogems of vault bounties — and the 5-star artifact sets and
  Dust of Enlightenment inside — are out of reach for now. Each vault also wants **three
  matching Shadow Remnant Guides**, which drop from Precious and Luxurious chests in that
  same area.
- **Snezhnaya exploration is 6%.** Treat all of the above as a plan, not a to-do list.

### For scale

**All 125 Snezhnaya quests together pay 1,260 Primogems**, across the 41 that pay
anything — the five best are *Down in the Dumps*, *Forbidding Doors of Melancholy*,
*Hatred, and the Throne of Those Who Hate*, *Let Not All Rivers Run Dry* and *The Stone
Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated*, at 50 each. So ordinary Snezhnaya world quests are worth
about **2.7× the whole Shadow Realm** — a real gap, but not the rout it first looked
like once Edge of Survival is counted. The Shadow Realm's better argument is still that
it is **level-independent**: 475 Primogems and 60 Mystic Enhancement Ore that a
40-something account can actually take, against world quests that scale against it.

**Not counted:** chests inside the realms. They do pay Primogems — 5–10 Precious, 10–20
Luxurious at Snezhnaya's zone scaling — but there is no per-realm chest census on the
wiki, and the Shadow Realm page is still marked a stub and under construction.

## Gatherables worth knowing

| Item | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Red Beet** | Everfrozen Earth — densest **between Morepesok and the Inverted Glacier Domain**; also **Okurov** | Looks like **small blue petals** on snow; the red root is buried. 100 total in Snezhnaya. **No vendor sells it.** |
| Rimecurrant | Snezhnaya | Weapon diagram material |
| Snowball Flower | Snezhnaya | Weapon diagram material |
| Rainbowdrop Crystal | Snezhnaya | Weapon diagram material |

**Valley of Fire Feathers** holds ~28 Red Beets in clusters, but needs the quest
***In the Abode of Life*** partially completed before they appear.

**Permafrost-area Expeditions** return Red Beet passively — set them before logging off.

### What they're for
Weapon **diagrams** from **Kuzmichev** at the **Kuznets Smithy, Snezhnograd**. Snezhnaya
added five forgeable 4-stars in 7.0.

| Weapon | Type | Lv90 stats |
|---|---|---|
| **Blade of Atonement** | **Claymore** | 564.8 ATK · **27.6% ATK%** |
| **Emberwell** | **Sword** ⚠️ *(not a catalyst)* | 510 ATK · **165 Elemental Mastery** |
| **Echoes of the Heart** | Catalyst | 565 ATK · 27.6% ATK% |
| Song of the Vigil | Polearm | — |
| Covenant of Frost and Snow | Bow | — |

Passives are similar in shape: triggering an **Elemental Reaction** grants EM or ATK%, and
triggering a **Stellar Glimmer** reaction grants ATK% or Stellar Glimmer DMG, for 12s.
They work **off-field**.

> ⚠️ **Sources disagree on the diagram material lists.** One gives Blade of Atonement as
> Rainbowdrop Crystal ×10 / Snowball Flower ×10 / Red Beet ×10; another lists entirely
> different materials. **Read the actual cost at Kuzmichev before farming for it.**

### The diagram is the cheap part
Forging is where the real cost sits. Blade of Atonement needs:
**500 Mora + Borderland Claymore Billet + 50 Rainbowdrop Crystal + 50 White Iron Chunk.**

That **White Iron Chunk** requirement links straight to
[`07-ore-farming.md`](07-ore-farming.md) — Mt. Aocang has ~21 nodes.

### Verdict for this account
**Neither Red Beet diagram is a priority.**
- **Emberwell's 165 EM is wrong for Skirk** — her damage is direct Cryo, not reaction damage
  (see [`05-current-loadouts.md`](05-current-loadouts.md))
- **Blade of Atonement** has the better statline, but your only claymore users are Noelle
  (who scales on DEF) and Chongyun at Lv 10
- **Echoes of the Heart** — the catalyst that would actually help Varesa — **needs no Red Beet**

Your real weapon problem is **underlevelled weapons**, not missing ones. Mystic Enhancement
Ore fixes that; a new diagram doesn't.

See [`redbeet-map.html`](../redbeet-map.html) for the marked search corridor.

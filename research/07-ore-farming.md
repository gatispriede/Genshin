# Ore Farming Routes

**Researched:** 2026-10-23 · **Game version:** 7.1
**Why you need this:** your weapons are badly underlevelled — Mika's Halberd is **Lv 1**,
Fischl's Slingshot and Noelle's Debate Club are **Lv 20**. Weapon EXP comes from
Enhancement Ore, and Enhancement Ore comes from mining.

---

## Bring Noelle

**Claymore users break ore fastest** — one hit per node instead of three or four. You have
**Noelle at Lv 32**, which is plenty; mining doesn't care about damage numbers.

Your **Traveler is currently Geo**, and Geo attacks also shatter ore. Running both makes
a route noticeably quicker.

---

## The headline route (~10 minutes)

**Mt. Aozang Crystal Cave → Mt. Aocang → Stormterror's Lair**

Reported yield with a claymore: **130–160 Crystal Chunks and 80+ White Iron Chunks**.
That is a genuinely large haul for the time invested.

## Liyue — the ore-rich region

You're already here, and Liyue has the densest clusters in the game.

| Spot | What's there | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Mt. Aocang** ⭐ | **~21 White Iron nodes** | Best single stop. Teleport to the **high** waypoint and work **downward** — gravity does half the walking |
| **Mt. Aozang cave** ⭐ | **15–20 Crystal Chunks** | The best concentrated Crystal Chunk source |
| **Lingju Pass** | ~10 White Iron | Teleport to **Lisha** (highest-elevation waypoint), then work **west** — roughly 8 nodes sit between you and the Lingju Pass stash |
| **Tianqiu Valley** | Mixed | Good secondary |
| **Guyun Stone Forest** | Mixed | Island chain, slower travel |
| **Cuijue Slope** | Crystal Ore | |
| **Qingyun Peak** | Crystal Ore | |
| **Bishui Plain** | Crystal Ore | You've already explored near here |
| **Mt. Tianheng** | Mixed | Right by Liyue Harbor — convenient top-up |

## Mondstadt

| Spot | What's there | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Brightcrown Canyon** ⭐ | **12–14 White Iron** | Follow the **northern canyon wall**, loop back along the southern edge |
| **Musk Reef** | 8 White Iron | Teleport **Cape Oath**, mine the clusters facing each other on the island's inner arch |
| **Stormbearer Point** | Plenty of White Iron | From the waypoint, head **north** to the big rock |
| **East of Wolvendom waypoint** | White Iron + **1–2 Crystal Chunks** | Quick stop |
| **Stormterror's Lair** | Lots of Crystal Chunks | High volume but **spread out** — only worth it as part of the big route |

## Dragonspine

Worth knowing since you're heading there anyway for Peak of Vindagnyr: Dragonspine has
**Starsilver** (its own ore type) alongside normal nodes. Mine opportunistically while
doing *In the Mountains* rather than making a dedicated trip — Sheer Cold makes it slow.

---

## Mechanics worth knowing

- **Ore respawns every 48–72 hours.** Don't revisit a route sooner.
- **Blacksmiths in Mondstadt and Liyue will tell you which areas have fully respawned.**
  Ask before committing to a route — saves a wasted trip.
- Across all regions there are **~228 White Iron nodes** (249 counting Enkanomiya), so a
  full sweep is a real time investment. Stick to the dense spots.
- The **official HoYoLAB interactive map** has an "ores" layer pinning every White Iron
  and Crystal Chunk node. Use it to build a route around *your* unlocked waypoints.

## What the ore turns into

| Ore | Becomes | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Chunk | Enhancement Ore | Weapon EXP (low tier) |
| **White Iron Chunk** | **Fine Enhancement Ore** | Weapon EXP (mid tier) |
| **Crystal Chunk** | **Mystic Enhancement Ore** | Weapon EXP (high tier) ⭐ |
| Iron / White Iron | **Pine Wood** | Serenitea Pot furnishing — 3 Iron **or** 2 White Iron → 1 Pine Wood |

> Check the exact conversion ratios at the crafting bench in-game; they're shown in the
> recipe UI and are easier to read there than to memorise.

**Crystal Chunk is the one that matters most to you.** Mystic Enhancement Ore is what takes
a weapon from Lv 20 to Lv 60+ without burning hundreds of lower-tier ores.

---

## Your plan

1. **Take Noelle and Geo Traveler.**
2. **Start with Mt. Aocang** — teleport high, work down, ~21 White Iron.
3. **Then Mt. Aozang cave** for the Crystal Chunks.
4. **Convert Crystal Chunks → Mystic Enhancement Ore** and dump it into:
   - **Mika's Halberd** (Lv 1 — the worst offender)
   - **Fischl's Slingshot** (Lv 20, and her 284 ATK is strangling Oz's damage)
   - **Noelle's Debate Club** (Lv 20)
5. **Ask a blacksmith** which regions have respawned before your next run.

See [`05-current-loadouts.md`](05-current-loadouts.md) for why those three weapons are the
priority.

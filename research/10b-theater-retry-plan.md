## Act 6: the loop (from 10a, re-planned for this cast)

The boss raises a **Deepdark Shield (45% of its HP)** and summons four Fishers.
Only elemental damage hurts the shield; each Fisher killed removes 25% of it.

**If the shield isn't broken within ~18s,** the boss eats the Fishers, fires an
arena-wide attack (200% ATK, true damage), then **drops all RES by 70% and is
paralysed for 10s**. That window is where the damage goes.

1. **Shield phase:** Zhongli shield up. Kill **three** Fishers, leave the fourth, build
   energy. Furina's buff stacks from HP changes, so the arena attack also feeds it.
2. **Arena attack:** take it behind Jade Shield, near full HP.
3. **Paralysis, 10s:**
   - Zhongli's −20% stacks with the −70%.
   - Yelan's Hydro and Varesa's Electro make Electro-Charged. Let the **Lv 90**
     (Yelan) be the one who triggers it, not Lv 60 Furina, because the reaction
     scales with the triggering character's level.
   - Varesa does her Burst and plunge chain inside the window.
4. Repeat.

On 12 Nov the best result was **40% of the boss left**, with an ungeared Varesa,
Barbara and two bare Lv 60s. Two things changed:
- Varesa now hits ~60k.
- Furina joins, plus Zhongli and Yelan arriving fresh.

That is enough to expect a clear. The ⭐ is a stretch.

  is the one to take.
- **A failed Act costs no Vigor.** Retry freely.
- **Rewind** goes back to the last Climactic Showdown (Act 3), and the Acts after it
  must be re-cleared. Don't finalize straight after a rewind.
- The season ends **1 Dec**. December's cast is Pyro / Hydro / Geo (leaked), where
  Varesa and Barbara don't qualify. **This is Varesa's last Theater month.**

**What changed from the written plan, and why it worked:**

1. **Zhongli, not Flins, filled Act 1.** Act 1 can only use the six trials, so one
   gold had to play it. Zhongli's other play was the late game anyway. This kept a
   carry in every Act after the first: the 12 Nov run failed Act 2 because it had none.
2. **Flins took Act 6, Varesa Act 5** (the user's call). The boss card reads "Lunar
   reactions have an advantage", and its shield takes 300% from Lunar reaction damage;
   Flins with Yelan's Hydro plays into that. Varesa had already cleared Act 5 ungeared
   on 12 Nov.
3. **Valeriy played Acts 4, 5 and 6** as the fixed support: shield, +100 EM (Lunar
   reactions scale on EM) and +10% Electro CRIT Rate on enemies he hits.
4. **Alyosha's Burst taunt saved the Defense act**: it pulls enemies off the monolith.
   Bring her to any Defense Sequence.
5. Drafts didn't follow the priority list: Alyosha came before Varesa. Re-planning
   at each draft screen beat a fixed table.

**Act 6 went from "best result 40% HP left" (12 Nov) to cleared with the star.**
The difference: a Lunar-Charged carry for a Lunar-weak boss, Furina at Lv 60, and
Valeriy's shield and EM.

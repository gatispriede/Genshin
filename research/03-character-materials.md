## Investment priority

You have 22 characters and resources for maybe four. Spreading them is the classic
mistake. In order:

### 1. Skirk (Lv 57) — finish her
Already your most-invested. **Ascension Phase 2 unlocks talent levelling** — if her talents
are still Lv 1, that is the single biggest damage gain available to you anywhere.
Talent priority: **Skill > Burst**. Normal attack last.

### 2. Xiangling (Lv 1) — the biggest untapped asset
She is one of the strongest 4-stars in the entire game and has been for the game's whole
life. Her burst Pyronado is enormous off-field damage that works in almost any team.
Going from Lv 1 to Lv 70 is a larger power gain than anything else on this list.

### 3. Fischl (Lv 50) — already halfway there
Oz provides consistent off-field Electro. For Skirk specifically that means **Superconduct
on a steady rhythm → Void Rifts → Serpent's Subtlety**.

### 4. Barbara (Lv 40) — your healer
Keeps Skirk alive, enables **Frozen** (the most reliable Void Rift generator), and
maintains the >90% HP condition if you run Harbinger of Dawn on Skirk.

### Deliberately deprioritised
**Varesa (Lv 50)** is a 5-star and tempting, but she is a *second main DPS* competing with
Skirk for the same field time and the same resources. Park her until Skirk is finished.

**Noelle (Lv 32)** — don't build for combat, but **keep her in your party for exploration**.
She's your only claymore at a usable level: ore nodes, Ancient Rime in Dragonspine, and
stone-encased chests all want a claymore.

## Talent book domain schedule (verified)

All talent domains run the same rotation: **Mon/Thu**, **Tue/Fri**, **Wed/Sat**, and
**Sunday gives everything**.

| Region | Domain | Mon/Thu | Tue/Fri | Wed/Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mondstadt | Forsaken Rift *(south of Springvale)* | Freedom | Resistance | Ballad |
| Liyue | Taishan Mansion *(Jueyun Karst lake edge)* | Prosperity | Diligence | Gold |
| Inazuma | Violet Court *(island south of Ritou)* | Transience | Elegance | Light |
| Sumeru | Steeple of Ignorance *(west of Chatrakam Cave)* | Admonition | Ingenuity | Praxis |
| Fontaine | Pale Forgotten Glory *(underwater)* | Equity | Justice | Order |
| Natlan | Blazing Ruins *(Coatepec Mountain)* | Contention | Kindling | Conflict |
| Nod-Krai | Lightless Capital | Moonlight | Elysium | Vagrancy |

> **Sunday is the day to farm.** Every book from every region is available. If you can only
> play one day a week for talents, make it Sunday.

**Which book does a given character need?** Open their talent screen and click the book
icon — the game shows you the domain and the days. Faster and more reliable than any list,
since characters from newer regions don't always follow their home nation's books.

## Ascension gems by element

Every character needs their element's gem line (Sliver → Fragment → Chunk → Gemstone),
dropped by **world bosses** of that element and by **Elemental Hypostases**.

| Element | Gem | Your characters |
|---|---|---|
| Cryo | **Shivada Jade** | Skirk, Mika, Kaeya, Chongyun |
| Electro | **Vajrada Amethyst** | Varesa, Fischl, Lisa, Beidou, Alyosha |
| Hydro | **Varunada Lazurite** | Barbara, Candace, Dahlia |
| Pyro | **Agnidus Agate** | Amber, Xiangling |
| Anemo | **Vayuda Turquoise** | Sucrose, Lynette |
| Geo | **Prithiva Topaz** | Noelle |
| Dendro | **Nagadus Emerald** | Tighnari, Collei |

**Your Cryo and Electro needs are heaviest.** Farm Cryo and Electro bosses preferentially.

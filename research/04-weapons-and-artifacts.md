## 3-star weapons that are genuinely good

Most 3-stars are filler. These are not — several compete with 4-stars at high refinement,
and refinement is easy because 3-stars are everywhere.

| Weapon | Type | Why it's good |
|---|---|---|
| **Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers** | Catalyst | **The best 3-star in the game.** On swap-out, grants the incoming character up to **+48% ATK at R5**. Matches or beats many 4-star support weapons. HP ascension stat. |
| **Harbinger of Dawn** | Sword | Up to **46.9% Crit DMG + 28% Crit Rate at R5** — while HP stays above 90%. Low base ATK. Needs a healer or shielder to hold the condition. |
| **Magic Guide** | Catalyst | Elemental Mastery stat. The F2P consensus pick for EM-scaling characters. |
| **White Tassel** | Polearm | Best 3-star polearm — high base ATK, Crit Rate substat. |
| **Slingshot** | Bow | Strong for **close-range** bow damage. Passive does not apply at long range. |
| **Dark Iron Sword** | Sword | Niche pick for Electro reaction teams. |

**Skip entirely:** Frostbearer, Debate Club, the Skyrider series.

> **Refinement matters more than rarity here.** An R5 Thrilling Tales beats a lot of R1
> 4-stars. Feed duplicate 3-stars into each other rather than hoarding them.

## Per-character recommendations

### Skirk — Cryo sword, your main DPS
**Weapon:** **Harbinger of Dawn**, refined as high as you can get it.
The >90% HP condition is normally the catch, but **you run Barbara**, so it's maintainable.
The Crit it provides is worth far more than the base ATK you give up, because you have no
artifacts supplying Crit yet.

**Artifact main stats:**

| Slot | Main stat |
|---|---|
| Sands | **ATK%** |
| Goblet | **Cryo DMG%** |
| Circlet | **Crit Rate** or **Crit DMG** |

**Do not build Elemental Mastery.** Her damage is direct Cryo, not reaction damage.
Frozen deals zero damage; Superconduct's RES shred is flat and irrelevant to her anyway
since she deals Cryo, not Physical.

**Do not build Energy Recharge.** Her burst spends Serpent's Subtlety, not Energy.

**Long-term target** *(far off — noted for direction only)*: Azurelight as signature,
Finale of the Deep as the craftable alternative; 4pc Finale of the Deep Galleries, or
4pc Marechaussee Hunter in a Furina team. Blizzard Strayer is a fallback that depends on
Freeze uptime.

---

### Barbara — Hydro catalyst, healer
**Weapon:** **Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers.** This is close to best-in-slot for her
regardless of budget. She swaps out after healing → Skirk comes in with up to +48% ATK.
The weapon's HP ascension stat also boosts her healing, which scales on her Max HP.

**Artifact main stats:** HP% sands, HP% goblet, **Healing Bonus** circlet (or HP%).
Her healing scales on HP — ATK does nothing for her.

---

### Lisa — Electro catalyst, support
**Weapon:** Thrilling Tales also works well here, but **Barbara gets first claim** — she
swaps out more naturally. Give Lisa Magic Guide if you want her enabling reactions, or a
second Thrilling Tales if you have one.

**Artifact main stats:** EM or ER sands, Electro DMG% goblet.

---

### Mika — Cryo polearm, HP-scaling healer
**Weapon:** nuanced. **White Tassel** has the better raw stats, but Mika's healing scales
on **Max HP**, so the HP% substat on **Black Tassel** is arguably more useful for his
actual job. If he's purely a healer, Black Tassel; if you want him contributing damage,
White Tassel.

**Artifact main stats:** HP% across the board.

---

### Fischl — Electro bow, off-field sub-DPS
**Weapon:** raw **ATK and Crit**. Her damage comes from **Oz**, who is summoned and fights
independently — so weapon passives that trigger on *her* attacks (including Slingshot's
close-range bonus) mostly **do not apply**. Pick the highest base ATK bow with an ATK% or
Crit substat you own.

**Artifact main stats:** ATK% sands, Electro DMG% goblet, Crit circlet.

---

### Kaeya — Cryo sword
**Weapon:** **Harbinger of Dawn** if you have a spare (same healer condition applies).
Otherwise highest base ATK sword.

**Artifact main stats:** ATK% sands, Cryo DMG% or Physical DMG% goblet, Crit circlet.

---

### Traveler / Funii (Manekin)
Both are sword users and mirror each other's element. **Harbinger of Dawn** suits them.
Remember Funii equips its **own** weapon and artifacts — it inherits nothing from the
Traveler, so it needs a separate kit.

---

### Amber — Pyro bow
Low build priority. She earns her slot on **utility** (lighting braziers, ranged puzzle
shots), not damage. Give her whatever bow Fischl isn't using.

---

### Varesa — Electro catalyst, 5★
⚠️ **Needs research.** Her kit and scaling aren't covered here yet. Before building her,
confirm whether she scales on ATK, HP or something else, and whether her damage is
on-field or off-field — that determines everything about her artifacts.

## Artifact main-stat cheat sheet

| Slot | Fixed? | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Flower | ✅ Flat HP | — |
| Feather | ✅ Flat ATK | — |
| Sands | ❌ | ATK% · HP% · DEF% · EM · Energy Recharge |
| Goblet | ❌ | **Elemental DMG%** · Physical DMG% · ATK% · HP% · EM |
| Circlet | ❌ | **Crit Rate** · **Crit DMG** · Healing Bonus · ATK% · HP% · EM |

The **goblet and circlet are where builds are won or lost.** A correct-element goblet and
a Crit circlet are worth more than any set bonus at your stage.

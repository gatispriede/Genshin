## You can now build artifacts instead of only farming them

This is the part the AR 40 plan got wrong, and it matters more than anything else here.

> ⚠️ **The old text said Sanctifying Elixir "needs 25 five-star artifacts to unlock" and
> to "revisit at AR 45."** Wrong. The **Artifact Transmuter** is a *gadget*, claimed from
> the **Adventurer Handbook → Embattle: Chapter IV**. The 25 is the maximum number of
> artifacts you can select in one extraction batch, not an unlock threshold.

**Definition** — spend Sanctifying Elixir to create an artifact with a chosen **set**,
**main affix** and **two minor affixes**:

| Piece | Elixir cost |
|---|---|
| Flower of Life · Plume of Death | **1** each |
| Sands of Eon | **2** |
| Circlet of Logos | **3** |
| **Goblet of Eonothem** | **4** |

**Only 2 pieces per set per cycle.** The goblet being the most expensive is unlucky,
since a Goblet is exactly what Varesa needs.

**Extraction** — turn spare artifacts *into* Elixir. Pieces must be **5-star at Level 4
or higher**, up to 25 at once, **100 Extraction Progress per Elixir**, and **at most one
Elixir per cycle**. Level breakpoints are the efficient stopping points:

| Artifact level | Progress points |
|---|---|
| 4 | 4 |
| 8 | 10 |
| 12 | 18 |
| **16** | **30** |
| **20** | **50** |

**Where Elixir comes from without extracting** — and one of these is sitting in front of
you: **Snezhnaya Meeting Points, Construction Level 5, pays ×5**. Also Natlan exploration
(Tribal Secret Spaces ×6, Tona's Flame's Restitution at Lv 10/20/30/40/50), Nod-Krai
Meeting Points Level 5 ×6, Frost Moon's Ancient Moon's Remnant Level 5 ×2, Gnostic Hymn
Battle Pass Lv 26, and Imaginarium Theater — **which you are already clearing to act 3**.

### Reshaping — and why Stygian Onslaught suddenly matters

Spend **Dust of Enlightenment** to pick **2 minor affixes** and reroll an artifact's
substat allocation, guaranteeing **at least 2** enhancements land on your picks. You keep
either the original or the result.

- Every Dust spent gives **1 Hallowed Exegesis**
- At **6 Exegesis** → **Advanced Reshape**: at least **3** enhancements on your picks
- Third consecutive Advanced Reshape → **Decreed Reshape**: at least **4**

Costs 1 Dust for a Flower or Plume, 2 for a Sands, Goblet or Circlet.

> **This is the payoff for Stygian Onslaught.** `02-wishes-and-primogems.md` notes that
> Stygian's tiers above the first three pay **Dust of Enlightenment rather than
> Primogems** — which read as a downside when that doc assumed you couldn't clear it.
> You are at **Stygian index 3**. The Dust is the point: it is the only way to aim
> substats, and substats are where this account's CRIT problem lives.

## The targeting problem, unchanged

**You cannot choose a farmed artifact's main stat.** Goblet main stats split across HP%,
ATK%, DEF%, EM, Physical DMG% and all seven elements — a *specific* element goblet is
roughly 1-in-11 on top of 1-in-5 for getting a goblet at all.

What has changed is that **Definition bypasses this entirely** for 4 Elixir. Farming is
for volume and Strongbox fuel; Definition is for the one piece you actually need.

## Resin plan

Roughly 180 resin a day — nine 20-resin runs.

| Priority | Target | Resin/day |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Derelict Masonry Dock** at Lv 90 — now a *guaranteed* 5-star per run | 60–80 |
| 2 | **World boss of the week** — gems plus 5-star artifacts | 40 |
| 3 | **Talent domain** for Skirk / Varesa (`03-character-materials.md`) | 40 |
| — | Ley Line Blossoms — at WL 7 these pay the full ~60,000 (`01-mora-farming.md`) | remainder |

**Don't use Condensed Resin for main-stat hunting.** It doubles the drop and the variance;
more rolls at 20 resin is the same expectation with less swing.

### Levelling rule — updated for AR 51

- Level to **+8** to see substats. Two CRIT substats → take it to **+16** or **+20**.
- 4-star pieces: **+12 maximum.** At Lv 90 domains you will replace them quickly.
- ⚠️ **The old rule said "sacrifice artifacts at +0 — extra levels are wasted."** That is
  right for the **Strongbox** and wrong for **Extraction**, which requires **Level 4
  minimum** and pays best at **16 and 20**. Sort spares by destination before levelling:
  Strongbox fuel stays at +0, Extraction fuel goes to a breakpoint.

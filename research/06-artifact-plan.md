## What AR 40 actually unlocked

| | |
|---|---|
| **World Level 5** | Enemies hit harder; all drops scale up with you |
| **Ascension Phase 5** | Character and weapon level cap rises to **80** (Lv 90 needs AR 50) |
| **5-star artifacts** | Start dropping — from **domain level III** and from **all world bosses** |
| **Still locked** | Domain **level IV** (AR 45), which *guarantees* a 5-star per run |
| **Still locked** | **Artifact Strongbox / Mystic Offering** (AR 45) — turn three spare 5-stars into one of a chosen set |

The practical line: **5-star pieces are now in the loot table but not guaranteed.**
Roughly a 37% chance of one per 20-resin run at level III, versus a guaranteed one at
level IV. That is the difference between "farm it" and "farm it twice as fast later."

## The thing nobody tells you about targeting

**You cannot choose an artifact's main stat.** It is rolled randomly on drop. There is no
domain that "gives Cryo goblets." Goblet main stats are split across HP%, ATK%, DEF%, EM,
Physical DMG% and all seven elements — so a *specific* element goblet is roughly a 1-in-11
roll on top of the 1-in-5 chance of getting a goblet at all.

Expect this to take a while. Keep **every** goblet and circlet with a usable main stat
regardless of set — at your level a correct main stat beats a set bonus.

### Sanctifying Elixir — now reachable, not yet worth chasing
It lets you craft an artifact and pick its main stat and two substats. Unlocking it needs
**25 five-star artifacts**, and producing more consumes 5-star pieces at +4 or higher.
At AR 40 5-stars finally drop, so this stops being impossible — but you have one 5-star
piece on the whole account (Skirk's Gladiator goblet). Revisit at AR 45.

> **Sanctifying Unction** is a different item — artifact EXP from the Realm Depot at Trust
> Rank 8. It does not change main stats.

## Resin plan

You have roughly 180 resin a day — nine 20-resin runs.

| Priority | Target | Resin/day |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Derelict Masonry Dock** — goblets and sands for Skirk and Varesa | 60–80 |
| 2 | **World boss of the week** — gems you need *and* 5-star artifacts now | 40 |
| 3 | **Talent domain** for Skirk / Varesa (see `03-character-materials.md`) | 40 |
| — | Ley Line Blossoms for Mora only if you're short | remainder |

**Do not** condense resin into Condensed Resin for domains you're only farming main stats
from — condensed doubles the drop but also doubles the variance. For main-stat hunting,
more rolls at 20 resin is the same expectation with less swing.

### Levelling rule at AR 40
- Level a piece to **+8** to check its substats. If two of the four are CRIT, take it to
  **+16** or **+20**.
- 4-star pieces: **+12 maximum**. They cap at +16 and you'll replace them.
- Do not level anything you're going to feed to the Strongbox at AR 45 — sacrifice
  artifacts at **+0**, extra levels are wasted.

## The crit problem is a weapon problem, not an artifact problem

Both your DPS characters are crit-starved: **Skirk 20.5%**, **Varesa 24.3%**. Neither of
their sets grants CRIT Rate, so it has to come from the weapon, the circlet, or substats.

The cheapest fix on the account isn't an artifact at all — it's the **Exaiphanes Blade**
(5-star, CRIT Rate 33.1% at Lv 90, free, levels without materials) sitting at Lv 20 on
your Traveler. See `05-current-loadouts.md`.

Varesa's circlet is already ATK% 31.3%; a **CRIT Rate circlet** would serve her better
once the goblet is sorted.

## Your plan, in order

1. **Level the Exaiphanes Blade** on Cryo Traveler at Snezhnaya statues, then equip it on
   **Skirk**. Free, and the largest gain available.
2. **Ascend Varesa, Skirk, Barbara, Fischl and Tighnari toward Lv 80.** AR 40 lifted the
   cap and nothing else uses those materials.
3. **Farm Derelict Masonry Dock** for a **Cryo DMG goblet** (Skirk) and an **Electro DMG
   goblet** (Varesa). Everything else is secondary.
4. **Replace Varesa's DEF% goblet immediately** with anything that isn't DEF% — even an
   ATK% goblet is a straight upgrade while you wait for Electro.
5. **Level the +0 pieces** both of them are already wearing. Varesa has three pieces at
   +0; that's free damage sitting unclaimed.
6. **Weekly boss + world boss every day** for gems and 5-star artifact chances.
7. **At AR 45:** domain level IV (guaranteed 5-stars) and the **Strongbox** both open.
   Start hoarding spare 5-star pieces at +0 now so you have Strongbox fuel ready.

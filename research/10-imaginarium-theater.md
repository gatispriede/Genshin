# Imaginarium Theater

**Researched:** 2026-11-01 · **Season 29** (1 Nov – 1 Dec 2026) · **Game version:** 7.1 → 7.2

---

## The rule for this account

> **Do not open with the strongest team.** Spend the weakest viable four on the early
> Acts and keep the Lv. 90 trials fresh, so the last two Acts go down fast.

Characters spend **Vigor** when they perform, so the same four cannot carry a whole run.
Early Acts are deliberately soft — Act 1's Star Challenge allows **80 seconds** against
three identical enemies. Burning premium units there buys nothing and leaves the hard
Acts to a depleted bench.

**It worked.** Easy Mode cleared 1 Nov 2026, all three stars, total 1m 16s:

| Act | Team | Time | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aino · Fischl · Kachina · Flins | **0:20** | 80s |
| 2 | Aino · Fischl · Kachina · Varesa | **0:25** | 65s |
| 3 — Jadeplume, Climactic Showdown | Zhongli · Flins · Yelan · Varesa | **0:31** | 70s |

By Act 3 the three 4★ had run out of Vigor and left the Principal Cast entirely, leaving
exactly five characters for four slots. The three golds arrived untouched and the boss died
in under half the limit. Spending them earlier would have meant meeting that boss with a
drained bench.

The Acts that bite in the longer modes:

| Act | What's there |
|---|---|
| 3 | Jadeplume Terrorshroom — Electro fills its Fury gauge 2%/hit; when full it enrages, then lies paralysed ~15s at **0% RES** against its usual 25%. Hit the head. |
| 10 | **Radiant Moonfly** — Dendro/Pyro boss with high RES to *both* its own elements |

## Season 29 cast

**Required elements: Hydro · Electro · Geo.**
Available reactions are therefore only **Electro-Charged, Lunar-Charged and Lunar
Crystallize** — every team wants Hydro + Electro paired, with Geo for Crystallize shields.

### Opening Characters — all six offered as Lv. 90 Trials

| Element | 5★ *(gold card)* | 4★ *(purple card)* |
|---|---|---|
| Geo | **Zhongli** | **Kachina** |
| Hydro | **Yelan** | **Aino** |
| Electro | **Flins** | **Fischl** |

One 5★ and one 4★ per element. The card background colour is the tell on the cast row —
gold is 5★, purple is 4★ — and it is what makes the "open weak" rule actionable: the three
4★ already cover all three elements between them, so an opening team is *the three purples
plus one gold*.

**You own Fischl.** Owning an Opening Character grants **+20% Max HP, ATK and DEF for the
whole season, inside *and* outside the theater** — so the Lv. 50 Fischl in the open world
is buffed until 1 December. Still field the **Trial** Fischl inside the theater; Lv. 90
beats Lv. 50.

### Special Guest Stars — you own none

Qiqi, Odette, Vesna, Jahoda. These are the only off-element units usable this cycle, and
Blessing text promising Guest Stars **+100% buff effect** is dead weight on this account.

### Alternate Cast — what you can actually contribute

| Character | Element | Level |
|---|---|---|
| Varesa | Electro | 80 |
| Barbara | Hydro | 70 |
| — | **Geo** | **none** |

Alternate Cast must match one of the season's three elements *and* clear a minimum level,
which is why only 2 of 25 characters qualify.

> **When a Battle offers two Alternate Cast members, take the Geo one almost regardless of
> who it is.** Geo cannot be replenished from this roster — the best owned Geo is Traveler
> at Lv. 40.

## Team split

**You can only field the Principal Cast.** Alternate Cast members are *not* playable until
they are drafted into the Principal Cast after a won Battle. At Act 1 that means four of
the six Trials and nothing else — Varesa and Barbara are bench stock for later rounds, not
an opening team.

**Act 1 — open with this**
> **Aino · Fischl · Kachina** (all three 4★) **+ Flins** (on-field)

The three purples already cover Hydro, Electro and Geo, so only one gold has to come along.
Spend **Flins**, because Electro-Charged is the reaction sitting at Blessing Lv. 1 while the
other two are at Lv. 0, and because Act 1 asks for damage rather than survivability —
**80 seconds** against three identical enemies.

This leaves **Zhongli and Yelan at full Vigor**, which is the whole point.

**Hold for the late Acts**
> **Zhongli** — the shield and the RES shred, worth most where damage actually threatens
> **Yelan** — the strongest off-field applier and damage buff in the cast

Pair them with whichever Electro still has Vigor, plus whatever got drafted in.

**Normal needs 6 of your own**, at Lv. 60+ *and* in the season's three elements.

| Qualifying now | |

## The levelling plan — *costs pulled from char_\*.json, 1 Nov 2026*

### What it costs

| Character | From → To | Hero's Wit | Ascensions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fischl | 50 → 60 | ~42 | A3 |
| Lisa | 50 → 60 | ~42 | A3 |
| Dahlia | 50 → 60 | ~42 | A3 |
| Traveler | 40 → 60 | ~70 | A2 + A3 |
| **All four** | | **~196** | **280,000 Mora** |

Lv. 50→60 is **854,125 EXP** (≈42 Hero's Wit) and ~171,000 Mora in EXP alone; the A3
ascension is another 60,000. Across all four that is roughly **1,080,000 Mora and 200
Hero's Wit** — this is not a weekend.

### Two bosses cover every non-gatherable material

**Electro Hypostasis** — Cape Oath, Mondstadt. Drops **Lightning Prism** *and* **Vajrada
Amethyst**, which is both the boss material and the gem for **Fischl and Lisa together**.
Need 8 Prism + 12 Amethyst Fragment for the pair.

**Secret Source Automaton: Overseer Device** — Atocpan, Natlan; swim southwest by
Koholasaurus. Drops **Secret Source Airflow Accumulator** *and* **Varunada Lazurite**, so it
covers both of Dahlia's. ~2.5 accumulators a clear, so **2 runs** at 40 resin. **Cryo
roughly doubles** against its Flow Momentum gauge — bring Skirk. The same boss also feeds
**Escoffier**, so nothing here is wasted.

**The Traveler needs no world boss at all** — only Windwheel Aster, Damaged/Stained Masks
and Brilliant Diamond. Cheapest of the four in materials despite needing twenty levels.

### Gatherable, free, all in Mondstadt

20 Small Lamp Grass (Fischl) · 20 Valberry (Lisa) · 20 Calla Lily (Dahlia) ·
30 Windwheel Aster (Traveler). Common drops: 24 Sharp Arrowhead, 12 Slime Secretions,
15 Damaged Mask, 12 Stained Mask.

### Order to do it in — and why not to chase Normal this month

> **Do not level for Season 29.** The required elements rotate on the 1st, and December is
> leaked as **Pyro/Hydro/Geo** — which drops Fischl, Lisa *and Varesa* out of eligibility
> and leaves Barbara alone. Four characters raced to Lv. 60 for November expire in thirty
> days.

Level for breadth instead, worst-first by how many seasons each one survives:

1. **Dahlia** (Hydro) — eligible in November *and* December
2. **Traveler** (Geo) — eligible in both, and needs no boss
3. **Fischl** (Electro) — only +10 levels, carries this season's +20% Opening Character
   bonus, and is a genuinely good unit outside the Theater
4. **Xiangling** (Pyro, Lv. 40) — not eligible now, but December is Pyro and she is one of
   the best 4★ in the game

That bench survives rotation. Chasing Normal in November buys six Acts once.

## Mechanics worth remembering

- **Only Battle Events award Fantasia Flowers.** Every other card *costs* them.
- After each Battle you **pick one of two Alternate Cast members** to join the Principal
  Cast. They arrive with 2 Vigor.
- **Wondrous Boon** costs 60 Flowers for one of three buffs; **Companion: Select** costs 60
  for a random character. While the bench is this thin, bodies beat buffs.
- Blessing Level 1 gives +800 Max HP, +50 ATK, +50 DEF, +20 EM during challenges.
- Unlock is AR 35 plus the World Quest *Unbegun, Unending Story*.

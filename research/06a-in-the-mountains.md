# In the Mountains — Dragonspine unlock walkthrough

Split out of `06-artifact-plan.md` on 2026-10-27. The artifact reason for doing this quest
(Blizzard Strayer) is **superseded** — see `06-artifact-plan.md`. The quest is still worth
clearing for the **Cryo Hypostasis** (Shivada Jade) and the **Frostbearing Tree**.

---

## Starting it

**Required AR 30** ✅ · No prerequisite quest.

Talk to **Iris** at the **Dragonspine Adventurer Camp** (northern side) and ask about the
blue crystals.

---

## Before you go

> ⚠️ **Bring a Cryo character.** Step 2 requires triggering Cryo Monuments. Skirk, Kaeya,
> Mika or Chongyun. A party with no Cryo will stall there.

> **Bring a claymore** — Noelle — for Scarlet Quartz nodes and ore.

### Sheer Cold
Dragonspine drains you with **Sheer Cold**. You do **not** have Goulash yet — that recipe
comes from *Ah, Fresh Meat!*, which unlocks **after** this quest. What actually works:

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Scarlet Quartz** | Red crystal nodes — break with a claymore, pick up the shard. Clears the gauge and buffs your attacks. |
| **Lit torches / braziers** | Scattered along the paths. Stand next to one. |
| **Ruin Braziers** | Light with Pyro; they stay lit. |
| **Warming Seelie** | Follows you and holds the cold off. |

---

## The steps

| # | Step |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Strange ice.** Break a **Scarlet Quartz** node, grab the quartz, hit the ice. Takes **three** separate quartz hits — the blue gauge drops each time. Then activate the orb it leaves. |
| 2 | **Wyrmrest Valley — Cryo Monument order puzzle.** Interact with the cube, watch the **Warming Seelie** travel between monuments, note the order, then hit them in that same order **with a Cryo character**. A Precious Chest spawns; opening it triggers **two Ruin Guards and a Ruin Grader**. |
| 3 | **Summit / Skyfrost Nail.** Teleport to the Statue of the Seven and take the previously blocked cavern up. Thaw **three more shards** at the main pillar — these need only **one hit each**. Activate each orb. |
| 4 | **The pillar rises.** Drop into the space revealed beneath it. |
| 5 | **Open the red-aura Luxurious Chest.** This is the final step and what grants domain access. |

Loot in the revealed space: 3 Precious Chests, 1 Exquisite, 1 red-aura Luxurious.

---

## What it unlocks

- **Peak of Vindagnyr** domain — Blizzard Strayer + Heart of Depth
- **Frostbearing Tree** — Dragonspine's offering system, its own reward track
- **Cryo Hypostasis** boss — drops **Shivada Jade**, needed by Skirk, Mika, Kaeya and
  Chongyun (see `03-character-materials.md`)

## Optional achievement

From the third Scarlet Quartz node there's a path up: light the torches with **Pyro**,
activate the **Anemo totem** for a wind current, glide through the rings, then grab the
floating pillar and climb to the top.

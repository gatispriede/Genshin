# 17 · What each team needs for the hardest content (22 Nov 2026)

Owner's question: for **each** team — Varesa first, then Skirk, then Mizuki — what it
takes to clear the hardest content, sorted by what is easiest: levels and talents,
then artifacts, then weapons, then new characters (with options). Local notes only.

**Sources:** Stygian.moe (this cycle's boss HP, mechanics and verified clears);
community calcs found on Google (Genshin Scientist / KQM investment, r/SkirkMains day-1
calc, a Reddit team-DPS table, r/Varesa_GI "125k KQM dps calcs", Mizuki Stellar Swirl
posts); the wiki (kits, availability); the owner's dummy runs (Stygian Dire preset,
zero-Energy start) and HoYoLAB read of 16 Nov. Community numbers are against a
standard enemy without the zero-Energy start, so the owner's dummy reads lower for
the same team.

## The targets

Stygian Onslaught, 2 minutes a battlefield. DPS floor = boss HP ÷ 120s, before
mechanic payoffs (which can be cancelled by shield phases):

| Boss (this cycle) | Fearless (Lv 105) | Dire (Lv 110, no Exalted bonus) |
|---|---|---|
| Wavebinder | 15.31M HP → 128k | 33.52M → **279k** |
| Emperor of Fire and Iron | 8.55M → 71k | 14.04M → **117k** |
| Radiant Moongecko | 10.25M → 85k | 20.95M → **175k** |

Bosses change every cycle; the targets show the scale, the mapping below is for this cycle.

**Verdict:** fully built at C0 with the right supports, all three clear **Fearless**.
**Dire** is in reach only for Skirk on the Emperor (117k vs ~135k); the other two Dire
fights need constellations or signature weapons on top.

Fearless mapping that fits each team's kit to the boss (supported by the clears):
- **Wavebinder ← Mizuki Stellar team**: Stellar Swirl / Stellar-Conduct are Stellar Glimmer
  reactions (wiki), which cut its Courage gain; the cheapest clears were Cryo Traveler teams.
- **Emperor ← Skirk team**: Hydro advantage through Furina/Mona; Cryo and Hydro break the Pyro horns.
- **Moongecko ← Varesa Overload**: healing breaks the cocoon (Iansan, Bennett, Chevreuse); most
  cheap Moongecko clears were Iansan + Chevreuse Overload teams. (Overload is Pyro, which the
  Emperor penalises — another reason Varesa is not on the Emperor.)

## Skirk team (target: Skirk · Furina · Escoffier · Mona)

1. **Levels and talents** (easiest): Skirk 87 → 90; **Skill 7 → 9/10** (her Seven-Phase Flash
   hits are in the Skill table: ×1.107 / ×1.160 by tools/upgrade-calc.py); Escoffier 70 → 90 and
   her talents (built, she is ~15% of the team: ~20k of 135k); **Furina Burst 4 → 10**
   (Fanfare 0.13% → 0.25% per point: ×1.156); Furina 70 → 90; Mona 57 → 80+ and her Burst.
2. **Artifacts**: Skirk's goblet ATK% → **Cryo DMG** (×1.12–1.14 in every roll case); sands with
   more crit rolls (×1.03–1.05); **keep her circlet** (5 good rolls; every swap case loses);
   stop Elemental Mastery substats (~7 wasted rolls today). Goal stats (Game8): ATK 2,100+,
   CRIT 70–80% / 200%+. **Escoffier: every piece is +0 Golden Troupe** — level them; this is
   the biggest single artifact gap on the account. Furina's Golden Troupe +0 to +9 → +20.
3. **Weapons**: Finale of the Deep R5 is the community baseline (100%); The Black Sword is not
   on that ranking. Azurelight (signature) 132.5%, Haran 116.6%, Jade Cutter 112.3%.
4. **New characters / constellations** (options, Genshin Scientist, vs the C0 team):
   Skirk signature +23%, Escoffier C1 +19%, Skirk C1 +16%, Skirk C2 +15%,
   **Vodyanitsa** (5★ Hydro, limited) in Mona's slot +13%. None is needed for Fearless.

## Varesa team (target: Varesa · Iansan · Chevreuse · Pyro)

1. **Levels and talents**: Varesa Normal Attack and Burst to 10 (the top-1% build runs 10/3/10;
   Skill stays low); **Iansan 20 → 80/90 and Burst 9–10** (her buff reads her ATK, capped by
   Burst level: 610 at Lv 8); Xiangling (owned, Lv 43) is a Pyro option to level now.
2. **Artifacts**: Varesa's crit value ≈100 → aim for ~200 (the top-1% card: 230 at ~110–130% ER);
   with Iansan and Chevreuse feeding Energy, move Energy Recharge substats to crit. Iansan:
   Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City from a Natlan Tribal Secret Space; ER first, then ATK%.
3. **Weapons**: The Widsith R2 stays; Iansan: Prototype Starglitter (Kachina's copy).
4. **New characters** (options):
   - **Chevreuse** (4★ Pyro, standard pool) — the piece every strong Varesa team has; C6 ideal.
   - **Bennett** (4★ Pyro, standard) — Pyro, healing and an ATK buff.
   - Durin or Mavuika (5★ Pyro, limited) — the premium fourth slot.
   - Budget without Chevreuse: Pyro Traveler, Bennett, Fischl.

## Mizuki team (target: Mizuki · Cryo Traveler · Odette · Sucrose)

1. **Levels and talents**: Mizuki **Skill 6 → 9/10** (×1.171 / ×1.229 by the calculator, approximate);
   Cryo Traveler 40 → 80/90; Sucrose 60 → 80/90.
2. **Artifacts**: Mizuki's pieces were +12 on 16 Nov → +20; **keep her EM circlet** (a CRIT Rate
   circlet loses 9–14% at her CRIT); Sucrose EM / Instructor.
3. **Weapons**: Sacrificial Fragments R2 stays; Traveler's Exaiphanes Blade (owned) is what the
   cheap Stellar clears use (at R5).
4. **New characters** (options):
   - **Odette** (5★ Cryo sword, limited, released 12 Aug 2026) — the piece every Stellar Swirl
     benchmark has.
   - **Faruzan** (4★ Anemo, standard) — the alternative to Sucrose.
   - Fischl (owned, C1) brings Electro for Stellar-Conduct on Electro-weak bosses.

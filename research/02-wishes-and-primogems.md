# Wishes & Primogem Income

**Researched:** 2026-10-23 · **Game version:** 7.1
**Note:** you already know achievements as a source — they're listed here only for completeness.

---

## The economics

- **1 wish = 160 Primogems**
- **Hard pity = 90 pulls** on a character banner
- **50/50** — your first 5-star may be a standard-banner character instead of the featured one
- **Guaranteed featured 5-star = 180 pulls worst case ≈ 28,800 Primogems**

That last number is the one to internalise. A guaranteed specific 5-star can cost nearly
29,000 Primogems. Against F2P income of ~6,000–7,000/month, that is **four to five months
of saving** for one worst-case character.

## Recurring income (F2P)

Community consensus: **~6,000–7,300 Primogems/month ≈ 37–46 wishes.**

| Source | Monthly | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily commissions** | **~1,800** | 60/day: 30 from four commissions + 30 activity bonus |
| Spiral Abyss | ~1,200 | Resets 1st and 16th. Up to 600/reset for 36 stars, Floors 9–12 |
| Events | ~2,000–2,500 | The largest variable. Do every event. |
| Imaginarium Theater | ~420–800 | Endgame mode, needs a deep roster |
| HoYoLAB daily check-in | ~60 | One tap a day. **Set this up.** |
| Livestream codes | ~150–300 | Three codes per version livestream, expire in ~24h |
| Maintenance compensation | ~300–600 | Free, automatic |

**With Welkin Moon (~$5/mo):** ~9,000–10,300 → 56–64 wishes/month.

### Per-patch figures
One calculator put a recent 6-week version at **14,540 Primogems / 107 pulls** for F2P,
rising to ~131 pulls with Welkin and ~139 with Battle Pass.

> ⚠️ These are community estimates from aggregator sites, and several sources disagree
> wildly (one claims 3,000/mo, another 14,000+). The **6,000–7,000/month** range is the
> most credible consensus.

| **Shrines of Depths** | ~40 Primogems each, 10 per region |
| **Archon & story quests** | Large lump sums per act |
| **World quests** | 20–60 each, hundreds exist |
| **Achievements** | *(you already know these)* |
| **First-time domain clears** | Small but adds up |
| **Free 5-star selectors** | Version 7.1 anniversary — **two of them** |

**A new account exploring Mondstadt and Liyue thoroughly is worth several thousand
Primogems.** That is worth more to you right now than optimising daily income.

## Wishing strategy for this account

**Don't pull.** Save.

Reasoning:
1. You have **one** character approaching built (Skirk, Lv 40, Lv 1 talents). A second
   5-star splits resin, Mora and EXP books you don't have.
2. A 5-star at low level with no artifacts loses to a well-built 4-star.
3. Characters rerun. Missing one costs nothing permanent.

### When to break the rule
- **Beginner's Wish** (if still available): 20 pulls at a 20% discount with Noelle
  guaranteed. Best Primogem value in the game.
- A character that fills a **structural hole** in your account, not one that's merely strong.

### Banner mechanics worth knowing
- **Soft pity** starts around pull 74 — 5-star rates spike sharply there.
- **Losing 50/50** guarantees the *next* 5-star is the featured one.
- **Capturing Radiance** (newer mechanic) can override some 50/50 losses — verify current
  rules before relying on it.
- **Epitomized Path** applies to weapon banners only.

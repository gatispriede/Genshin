# Wishes & Primogem Income

**Researched:** 2026-10-23 · **Game version:** 7.1
**Income section rebuilt:** 2026-11-08 against **7.2**, from the wiki's own reward tables
rather than aggregator prose. See the revision note at the bottom of this section.
**Note:** you already know achievements as a source — they're listed here only for completeness.

---

## The economics

- **1 wish = 160 Primogems**
- **Hard pity = 90 pulls** on a character banner; **soft pity from 74**, where the rate
  climbs 6 percentage points per pull
- **50/50** — your first 5-star may be a standard-banner character instead of the featured one
- **Guaranteed featured 5-star = 180 pulls worst case ≈ 28,800 Primogems**

That last number is the one to internalise. A guaranteed specific 5-star can cost nearly
29,000 Primogems — **four to five months of saving** for one worst-case character.

## Recurring income (F2P)

**Answer to "is 50 wishes a month realistic": yes, and it's the middle of the range.**
The floor is about **40** — the repeatable sources below, nothing else. The ceiling for
someone who clears a whole patch is about **61**. Version 7.2 (42 days) is estimated at
**12,697 Primogems + 7 Intertwined + 10 Acquaint Fates ≈ 86 limited pulls**, which is
~61/month. Estimates cluster at **83–86 pulls**; the spread is mostly disagreement over
whether Imaginarium Theater resets once or twice in the patch.

### Verified per-source, from the wiki's reward tables

| Source | Payout | Cycle |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily commissions** | **60/day** — 4 × 10, plus a **20 bonus** for reporting back to the Adventurers' Guild | daily → **1,800/mo** |
| **Spiral Abyss**, floors 9–12 | **800** for a full clear | **resets the 16th, monthly** |
| **Imaginarium Theater** | **1,000** per season | seasonal |
| **Stygian Onslaught** | **450** — 3 × 150 for Normal, Advancing, Hard | once per version, 10 days |
| Maintenance compensation | ~600 | per version |
| Livestream codes | ~300 | per version, expire in ~24h |
| HoYoLAB daily check-in | ~60 | monthly. One tap a day. **Set this up.** |
| Events | ~1,000 flagship + ~420 per sub-event | per version |
| **Paimon's Bargains** | **5 Intertwined Fate** at 75 Masterless Stardust each | monthly |

Stygian's higher tiers — Menacing, Fearless, Dire — pay **Dust of Enlightenment and
cosmetics, not Primogems**, so the Primogem ceiling there is only the first three medals.

The **Intertwined Fates from Paimon's Bargains are 5 free pulls a month**, worth 800
Primogems — but Masterless Stardust comes from 3-star wish results, so this income only
exists once you are already pulling. It is not available to an account that is saving.

**With Welkin Moon (~$5/mo):** +90/day = +2,700/mo, roughly **+17 wishes**.

### The Frostbearing Tree pays no Primogems — it pays Fates and a Crown

*Checked 2026-11-08.* An Offering System, like Reputation, not a Statue of The Seven. The
Primogem-bearing tree-shaped collectible is the **Statue** (oculi); the Frostbearing Tree
is a different system and its 12 offering levels pay **zero Primogems**.

| All 12 levels pay | |
|---|---|
| **Primogems** | **0** |
| **Acquaint Fate** | **4** |
| **Intertwined Fate** | **2** |
| **Crown of Insight** | **1** |
| Mora | **600,000** |
| Adventure EXP | 2,400 |
| Mystic Enhancement Ore | 120 |
| Hero's Wit | 10 |
| Northlander Polearm Billet | 1 |
| Wings of Concealing Snow | wind glider (Lv 12) |
| Frostbearing: Ancient Tree | namecard (Lv 9) |
| Diagram: Warming Bottle · Memory of the Frostbearing Tree | Lv 4 and Lv 10 — the latter is the Frostbearer catalyst |

**The 35 Primogems come from achievements, not the tree**: *Scarlet Sprouts* Tier 1 at
Lv 4 pays **5**, Tier 2 at Lv 8 pays **10**, Tier 3 at Lv 12 pays **20**. A further **5**
comes from *Untellable Tale*, the hidden achievement on the chest the foxes leave in
*Lost in the Snow* — which is also where the **80th** Crimson Agate is.

**In wish terms the Fates dwarf the Primogems.** 6 free pulls is **960 Primogems of pull
value**, 27× what the achievements pay. The **Crown of Insight** is the bigger prize
still — crowns are among the scarcest items in the game and this is a permanent source.

**The cost, which is the part worth planning around:**

- Levels cost agates in **groups of 10**; Lv 12 needs **110 total**.
- Only **80 exist** in the overworld and chests. That caps world collection at **Lv 9**.
- The last **30 must come from Crimson Wish** — 5 quests spawning **every Monday and
  Friday**, 1 agate each, unlocked at Tree Lv 8. So after the 80th agate it is **three
  more reset cycles minimum** to reach Lv 12.
- Crimson Wishes pay **only the agate** — no Primogems, no Mora listed.
- Crimson Wish stops refreshing permanently at Lv 12, and leftover agates are kept. No
  reason to rush; no way to lose anything by going slowly.

**How much of this is unclaimed is unknown.** On 8 Nov I wrote "Dragonspine is 5.9%
explored" — that came from the README's **23 Oct** figure, and `account.md`'s **28 Oct**
read already said **29.5%**. Two numbers for the same thing in the same repo, and I
quoted the older one without checking. Neither is current. Do the tree for the
**600,000 Mora** and the crown rather than for Primogems — see `01-mora-farming.md`,
where that Mora is the largest one-off listed — but check the Offering level in game
before assuming none of it is taken.

## Wishing strategy for this account

**Don't pull.** Save.

Reasoning:
1. You have **one** character approaching built (Skirk, Lv 40, Lv 1 talents). A second
   5-star splits resin, Mora and EXP books you don't have.
2. A 5-star at low level with no artifacts loses to a well-built 4-star.
3. Characters rerun. Missing one costs nothing permanent.

### When to break the rule
- **Beginner's Wish** (if still available): 20 pulls at a 20% discount with Noelle
  guaranteed. Best Primogem value in the game.
- A character that fills a **structural hole** in your account, not one that's merely strong.

### Banner mechanics worth knowing
- **Soft pity** starts at pull 74 — the 5-star rate climbs **6 percentage points per
  pull** from 0.6%, hitting 100% at 90.
- **Losing 50/50** guarantees the *next* 5-star is the featured one.
- **Pity carries between banners** of the same type — 89 pulls on one Character Event
  Wish means the first pull on the next is a guaranteed 5-star. It does **not** carry
  across to the Weapon banner or Wanderlust Invocation.
- **Capturing Radiance** — *resolved 2026-11-08, this file previously said "verify before
  relying on it."* It only fires **on a 50/50**, never when you are already guaranteed,
  and it upgrades the result to the featured character. **The consolidated chance of
  winning the featured 5-star is 55%, not 50%.** Base trigger chance is 0.018%, but it
  is **guaranteed to fire if you have lost the 50/50 three consecutive times** — so a
  fourth consecutive loss is impossible.
- **Epitomized Path** applies to weapon banners only.

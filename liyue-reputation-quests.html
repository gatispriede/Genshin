Liyue Reputation — the quests

For the Geo Treasure Compass at Reputation Level 6 (2,200 EXP). Quest list taken verbatim from the wiki, 28 Oct 2026.

✅ Measured, 30 Oct 2026: Reputation Level 4, 40/460. Cumulative 1,300 EXP. To Level 6 and the Geo Compass you need 420 → Lv 5 then 480 → Lv 6 = 900 EXP, which is 9 world quests. The in-game menu reads 8/60 done, so 52 remain — you need a sixth of what's left.

The 460 figure confirms Liyue uses Mondstadt's level curve (400 / +420 / +440 / +460 / +480), which is how the 900 is derived rather than guessed.
⚠️ Still claim what's waiting first. Reputation EXP from world quests and exploration is retroactive but must be claimed manually — the 1,300 above is only what's been claimed. Liyue at 22.7% has earned the 20% exploration milestone (100 EXP); check whether it's been taken. Mondstadt at 70.4% has earned all three (300 EXP) on its own separate track.
Talk to Ms. Yu, not Katheryne. Liyue's Reputation NPC is Ms. Yu — in Liyue Harbor, east of the teleport waypoint and north of the blacksmith. (Mondstadt's is Hertha.) Katheryne runs the Adventurers' Guild, which is a different menu — I had that wrong. Then: Reputation → World Quests / World Exploration → Claim.

Correction, 30 Oct: I wrote here that you might already be at or near Level 6. You're at Level 4 — so the quests do matter, just 9 of them rather than the 16 I first estimated.
If the Reputation menu isn't open yet: Liyue's unlocks after Archon Quest Chapter I Act II Farewell, Archaic Lord plus AR 25, and specifically after the world quest Ministry Missions. At AR 51 the rank is long since met — if you can't find Ms. Yu's menu, Ministry Missions is the thing to do first.

⏱ The quickest ones

Same 100 EXP each as a two-hour chain. These are the ones guides single out as short.

Every world quest pays 100, regardless of length. A five-minute fetch quest and a multi-act saga are worth exactly the same. So pick on time, not on content — you want the 9 shortest ones you can find.

Tier 1 — flagged as genuinely quick

1
Big Business Described as "a few minutes." Collect invoices around Stone Gate and Dihua Marsh. Also pays 30 Primogems and 30,000 Mora.
2
Book in the Woods Find Little Nine's book on Wuwang Hill — a short wander in the nearby forest. You already have a page for that area: wuwang-statue-puzzle.html.
3
A Little Game Talk to Childish Jiang, north of Mt. Tianheng. Simple hide-and-seek, no combat.
4
Temporary Acclimatization Set up two devices for Woliu. Starts east of Yilong Wharf.

Tier 2 — single-NPC starts inside Liyue Harbor

All begin by talking to one person in the city. No travel to reach them, and you're standing next to Ms. Yu anyway.

Tier 3 — short, just outside the city

Will of Stone Blackcliff Woes The Ocean Pearl A Lone Ship in Guyun An Adeptal Summons The Secret of Nantianmen An Ode to Yonder City — Qingzhou, Wangshu Inn The Tree who Stands Alone Treasure Lost, Treasure Found
That's 25 candidates for 9 slots, nearly all of them in or beside the Harbor — which is the same 25-pin cluster on the map above. You can finish this without ever crossing Liyue.
How "quick" was decided: these are the quests that multiple guides describe as short, single-objective, or single-NPC starts. I haven't timed them, and Genshin doesn't publish quest lengths — so treat the tiers as a sensible ordering, not a stopwatch. If one turns out to be a sprawling chain, drop it and take the next.

How I'd actually spend the afternoon

1
Claim everything first Reputation menu, both tabs. Free, instant, and it may finish the job on its own.
2
Finish the Teapot chain you already started Both parts, 200 EXP, and you wanted the teapot anyway.
3
Clear the Liyue Harbor cluster The city quests are short, dialogue-heavy and sit on top of each other — no travel between them. A Quiet Day in Liyue Harbor, Big Business, Necessary Procedures, Pressing Deadlines, Old Tastes Die Hard, Chili Con Cloudy, Endless Research, Snapshots and The Tales Behind the Fan are all city-based.
4
Then the treasure-hunt pairs out in the field Treasure Lost, Treasure Found (2 parts), Tianqiu Treasure Trail, Trails in Tianqiu, And This Treasure Goes To... — these also pay chests, so they double as exploration progress.

Don't start The Chasm for this Long gating sequence and 0% exploration. It's worth doing eventually for the ore (chasm-ore-map.html) — just not as a reputation shortcut.

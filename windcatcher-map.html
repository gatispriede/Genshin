Wind Catcher — how to fill it

Charge it by walking up to Anemograna — small floating wind spirits. Just get close; no button needed. Holds 5 charges.

You may not even need the gadget. Collect all three Anemograna in an area and a Wind Current spawns right there. They also cut Stamina consumption by 30% while attached.

Spot 1 — east of the Dawn Winery statue

Spot 2 — south of the Windrise statue

The gadget itself

UnlockMondstadt Reputation Lv 3 → blueprint → craft at a blacksmith
Craft cost10 Hurricane Seeds · 30 Windwheel Aster · 50 Crystal Chunks
ChargingWalk near Anemograna. Max 5 charges
UseZ key (or the icon on mobile)
EffectOne wind current per charge — about a mid-sized tree's height, ~10s
Cooldown~100 seconds
RestrictionsCannot be used in combat or while gliding
Where the materials come from: Hurricane Seeds drop from the Anemo Hypostasis at Stormbearer Point. Windwheel Aster grows around Mondstadt and Windrise. 50 Crystal Chunks — see chasm-ore-map.html; the Chasm Underground Mines cover that in one trip.

What they look like: pale blue-white motes like spinning dandelion seeds, low to the ground, always in sets of three. Found in Mondstadt including Dragonspine — the Dragonspine ones look different, having "mutated to their environment."

Zones are approximate — they mark where Anemograna concentrate, not individual spirits.

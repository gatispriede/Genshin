How her kit actually works

She runs on Serpent's Subtlety (SS), not Elemental Energy. Her Burst is paid for in SS. Her Elemental Energy bar is never spent — which is precisely why 4pc Finale of the Deep Galleries is built for her: its condition is 0 Elemental Energy, and hers is permanently 0, so the 4-piece is always on.
E
Elemental Skill — +45 SS Tap enters Seven-Phase Flash. Hold absorbs Void Rifts. Most of her on-field damage comes from this talent.
◇
Void Rifts — +8 SS each Created when a nearby party member triggers Frozen, Superconduct, Cryo Swirl or Cryo Crystallize. Melt does not make them. One every 2.5s, max 3 on the field. She absorbs them with a Charged Attack during Seven-Phase Flash, with Havoc: Extinction, or by holding her Skill.
Q
Elemental Burst — costs 50 SS Every SS point above 50 adds Burst damage, up to 12 extra points. So 62 SS is the ceiling — Skill (45) plus two Rifts (16) lands on 61, which is as close as C0 gets.
⚡
Seven-Phase Flash Up to 12.5s, or until SS runs out — SS drains continuously. Her Burst is replaced by Havoc: Extinction, which costs no SS and buffs her next 10 Normal Attacks. Any leftover SS is cleared when the state ends.
Enemies with a permanent Pyro aura shut her down. They block Void Rift generation, which cuts off her SS income. Don't bring her to Pyro-aura content — send Varesa instead.

⭐ The passive that decides her team

Return to Oblivion (Ascension 4). Every time a nearby Hydro teammate lands a Hydro hit, or a Cryo teammate other than Skirk lands a Cryo hit, she gains a stack of Death's Crossing for 20s. Max 3 stacks — one per teammate. At 3 stacks it's roughly +170% Normal Attack damage in Seven-Phase Flash and +160% Burst damage, as a base-damage multiplier.
This is not a small bonus — it's most of her damage. Each teammate can give only one stack and she can't give herself one, so three stacks means three Hydro/Cryo teammates, all of them actually hitting enemies. The step from two stacks to three is the biggest one. Any non-Hydro, non-Cryo character in the slot is a stack you don't get.
Mutual Weapons Mentorship (utility passive): if every party member is Hydro or Cryo, with at least one of each, everyone's Elemental Skill level +1. A mono Hydro/Cryo team gets this on top of the three stacks.

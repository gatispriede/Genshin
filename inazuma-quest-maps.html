Inazuma reputation quests — where each one starts

All 42 world quests that pay Reputation EXP, ordered shortest first, with 139 map and location shots pulled from the wiki.

Liyue · Inazuma · Sumeru · Fontaine
Target: Level 9 and the Electro Treasure Compass. 42 world quests at 100 plus 3 Archon or story acts at 200 = 4,800 EXP, against the 5,000 needed — short by 200. The gap has to come from weekly Requests (80 each, capped at 3 per week across every nation) and exploration milestones.

Only the 42 world quests appear below; the acts have no start location of their own, so there is nothing to map for them.

7 of these are already struck off, so you are in this region and the Reputation board is open. The exploration percentage on file predates that and is deliberately not quoted here. The order below is by measured length, so work from the top.
These maps are the wiki's own in-game crops, taken from each quest page and each giver's NPC page — not pins I placed. The white ring marks the spot; the blue teardrop nearby is the waypoint to teleport to.
Why there is no single map with every dot on it. The official interactive map's World Quest layer has 576 pins, every one of which returns a null name, and the wiki stores no coordinates. A combined map would have been guesswork; these per-quest crops are exact. Full sortable table across all regions: reputation-quests.html.
6 quests have no location shot anywhere: Katheryne in Inazuma, Gourmet Supremos, Assemble!, Treatment on the Island, Yougou Cleansing, The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent, The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna. Those cards say so rather than showing a guess.

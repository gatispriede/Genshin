Liyue reputation quests — where each one starts

All 57 world quests that pay Reputation EXP, ordered shortest first, with 189 map and location shots pulled from the wiki.

Liyue · Inazuma · Sumeru · Fontaine
Level 6 and the Geo Treasure Compass landed 30 Oct 2026. 44 quests remain here, 13 struck off.

Next is Level 8 and Wings of Golden Flight, the glider that caps Liyue. From the Level 6 threshold of 2,200 that is 1,020 EXP, about 11 quests.

The 11 marked do this one need 59k of dialogue and 55 objectives; the same number taken from the longest would cost 260k and 165 for identical EXP.

That count is a ceiling. EXP already earned past the threshold, plus exploration milestones and weekly requests, comes off the top.
These maps are the wiki's own in-game crops, taken from each quest page and each giver's NPC page — not pins I placed. The white ring marks the spot; the blue teardrop nearby is the waypoint to teleport to.
Why there is no single map with every dot on it. The official interactive map's World Quest layer has 576 pins, every one of which returns a null name, and the wiki stores no coordinates. A combined map would have been guesswork; these per-quest crops are exact. Full sortable table across all regions: reputation-quests.html.
3 quests have no location shot anywhere: An Adeptal Summons, Shrouded Vale, Hidden Hero, On the Stage, Behind the Stage. Those cards say so rather than showing a guess.

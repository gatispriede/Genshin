Coins come from open-world duellists (a few hundred early, about 1,500 from a Level 7–8 opponent) and four weekly Tavern guests from Player Level 2. Coins cap at 50,000. The Casket of Tomes' locate function pins every nearby duellist on your map; the Cat's Tail has its own teleport icon when you zoom into Mondstadt.

A Skirk Frozen deck

Skirk · Kaeya · Xingqiu, the shell HoYoverse uses for her own Invitational (Skirk · Xingqiu · Escoffier, with free Kaeya in Escoffier's slot). Her Burst Havoc: Ruin spends all Serpent's Subtlety: at the 7 cap it deals 7 Cryo plus 3 Piercing to each standby enemy, for 3 dice. Everything in the deck exists to reach 7.

Source of Serpent's SubtletyGainLimit
Elemental Skill Havoc: Warp+2once per round
Void Rift (made when your side triggers Frozen, Superconduct, Cryo Swirl or Cryo Crystallize)+2, by discarding a card whose original cost is 33 per round
Mutual Weapons Mentorship discarded on a switch or at round start+1—
  • Never a third Cryo. Mono-Cryo triggers no reaction, so no Void Rifts. Xingqiu's Rainbow Bladework puts Hydro on every Normal Attack: Frozen, a rift, and a lost turn for the opponent. Fischl is the free substitute (Oz makes Superconduct every End Phase).
  • Pack 6–8 cards that cost exactly 3 dice as rift fuel: Sacrificial Sword, Favonius Sword, Noblesse Oblige, Paimon, Dawn Winery, Aquila Favonia, Skyward Blade. All three characters use swords, so no weapon card is ever stranded.
  • Skirk banks from the bench. Her passive and Void Rift say "your side" and "your Skirk", not "active". She steps in only for her Skill and the Burst.
  • Wait for 7. The Burst is legal at 2 and does 2 + 2 Piercing; at 7 it does 7 + 3 for the same dice. Plan: set up Frozen in round 1, bank to 7 in round 2, fire in round 3.
  • Higher ceiling: Mona instead of Xingqiu. Her Illusory Bubble doubles the next Skill DMG after additive bonuses; with Adeptus' Temptation (+3) a 7-stack Burst becomes 20. Whether the Piercing part doubles too is unverified.

Cards that reward switching

Efficient Switch makes the next switch cost 1 less die; Agile Switch makes it a Fast Action that does not end your turn (the expensive one). Useful for any deck that swaps every round, such as Varesa's, whose Skill switches out and whose Apex Drive fires when she is switched back in.

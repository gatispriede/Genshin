Derelict Masonry Dock

Atocpan, Natlan — Remnants of Tetenanco. The one domain that gears both Skirk and Varesa.

Two sets, both already on your characters. Finale of the Deep Galleries (Skirk) and Long Night's Oath (Varesa) drop here and nowhere else. You're not farming for the sets — you're farming for a Cryo DMG goblet and an Electro DMG goblet.

Where it is — Natlan

Atocpan is the red volcanic island in the south-west, separate from the Natlan mainland.

Where it is — Atocpan

◈
The domain South-west Atocpan, below the Remnants of Tetenanco.
▾
Teleport here The waypoint north-east of the domain, south-west of Remnants of Tetenanco. From it, follow the path west — it's a short run downhill.

⚠️ Bring Varesa, not Skirk

Ley Line Disorder: when a character's Plunging Attack hits, that character gets +30% CRIT DMG and +20% ATK for 15s. And one enemy — the Furnace Shell Mountain Weasel — has a phase that only a Plunging Attack can break.

This domain is built around plunging. Skirk doesn't plunge; Varesa does — she's the single best character in the game for this room, and she's your highest-level character anyway. Run it with her and hand the Cryo pieces to Skirk afterwards.

1
Varesa — Lv 70, your DPS here Electro, plunge-scaling, 4pc Long Night's Oath already equipped. The disorder and the weasel's shield are both hers to handle.
2
Fischl or Xiangling — sub-DPS Off-field damage while Varesa cycles plunges.
3
Sucrose — Anemo Grouping, and Anemo is one of the recommended elements here. Arm her first — she's on a Lv 1 weapon.
4
Barbara or Mika — heals Mika also gives party ATK SPD, which helps a plunge rotation.
Recommended elements: Anemo, Electro, Dendro, Hydro. No element is penalised. Other enemies: Tatankasaurus Warriors and Whelps, Ferocious Warriors.

Which level to run

Level III is the one. It's where 5★ pieces start appearing — roughly a 1-in-3 chance per 20-resin run rather than the guarantee you'll get at AR 45. That's still the best artifact resin on your account, because nothing else drops the two sets you're already wearing.
Keep every 5★ you don't use, at +0. At AR 45 the Artifact Strongbox turns any three 5★ pieces into one piece of a set you choose. Levelling a piece you're going to sacrifice is wasted Mora.

Fontaine reputation quests — where each one starts

All 40 world quests that pay Reputation EXP, ordered shortest first, with 99 map and location shots pulled from the wiki.

Liyue · Inazuma · Sumeru · Fontaine
Target: Level 9 and the Hydro Treasure Compass. 40 world quests at 100 plus 5 Archon or story acts at 200 = 5,000 EXP, against the 5,000 needed — quests cover it, with 0 to spare.

Only the 40 world quests appear below; the acts have no start location of their own, so there is nothing to map for them.

You have explored 2% of this region, so treat this as a route plan rather than a to-do list — the Reputation board itself unlocks behind that region's Archon quest, and most of these start points sit behind waypoints you have not opened. The flagged ones are simply the shortest, for whenever you do arrive.
These maps are the wiki's own in-game crops, taken from each quest page and each giver's NPC page — not pins I placed. The white ring marks the spot; the blue teardrop nearby is the waypoint to teleport to.
Why there is no single map with every dot on it. The official interactive map's World Quest layer has 576 pins, every one of which returns a null name, and the wiki stores no coordinates. A combined map would have been guesswork; these per-quest crops are exact. Full sortable table across all regions: reputation-quests.html.
7 quests have no location shot anywhere: Another Horizon of Adventure, Hereafter..., Fortune Plango Vulnera, Mary-Ann's Story, Rowboat's Wake (Quest), Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog, Both Brains and Brawn. Those cards say so rather than showing a guess.

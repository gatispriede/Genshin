## The tools

| Tool | What it answers |
|---|---|
| `secrets run flux-node tools/site-index.mjs` | Crawls the live site from `/genshin-notes` and joins the local catalogue → `reference/data/site-index.json`: every page's source file, words, screens, pictures, tables, links out and in, click depth, title, canonical, and what a crawler without JavaScript sees |
| `site-map.html` (local dev server, `/site-map.html`) | That index as an interactive graph (size = text, colour = section, red ring = breaks a rule) plus a sortable, filterable table and the list of local-only pages |
| `node tools/content-overlap.mjs` | Duplicated wording between pages: the share of each page's 8-word passages found on another page |
| flux MCP `seo.preflight` | Per page: missing title, description, share image, canonical |

## Reputation quests: one page per area (20 Nov)

Owner: the route pages must also list the quests that lead to each reputation quest, and a
region on one page was too big. Now:
- `tools/fetch-quest-prereqs.py` follows the wiki's requirement **links**, not only `prev`:
  42 quests gained steps, e.g. Treasure of Wisdom: A New Plan needs the hidden objective
  Drusus's Riddles. Hidden objectives (`reference/data/hidden-objectives.json`) carry their
  place and step-by-step solution; series and part pages are never counted as steps.
- `tools/build-rep-route.py` writes a short region page (area map + a card per area + side
  lists) and one page per area, at most 12 quests: an area splits by sub-area, a city into even
  parts, and a city's small neighbours share an "Around <city>" page. Every stop shows
  **To unlock** (the wiki's requirement) and its steps in order, hidden objectives folded.
- The blurry 4x city close-up is gone; area crops cover at least 384 px of the official map and
  enlarge at most 2x (the official tiles stop at half scale). A CMS option to open a map zoomed
  on its pins would let city maps be sharper still.
- The area pages nest under their region in `flux-tree.mjs`, read from
  `reference/data/rep-route-areas.json`, so rebuilding a region needs no tree edit.

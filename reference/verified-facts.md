## Resin costs — *verified 28 Oct 2026*

| Activity | Resin |
|---|---|
| Artifact domain / talent domain / ley line | 20 |
| **Weekly boss, discounted** | **30** |
| World boss | 40 |
| Weekly boss, full price | 60 |

- The 60→30 discount is **3 claims per week, shared across all Trounce
  Domains** — not 3 per boss. Resets Monday 04:00 server time.
- One reward per weekly boss per week.
- **Condensed Resin does not work on bosses** — domains and ley lines only.
- Weekly boss materials are the **only** source for talent levels 7→10.
  Roughly 18 weeks to max one character 10/10/10.
- **Dream Solvent** converts one weekly boss material into another, so which
  boss you kill matters less than that you kill one.

## Artifacts — *verified 27 Oct 2026*

- **5★ artifacts begin dropping at AR 40** (domain level III, and all world
  bosses). AR 45 adds level IV, which *guarantees* one per run. Earlier advice
  to wait until AR 45 was wrong.
- **Artifact Strongbox / Mystic Offering unlocks at AR 45.** Three 5★ pieces →
  one of a chosen set. Inputs need not match. Sacrifice at **+0**.
- Main stat beats set bonus beats substats at low investment.

**Sets confirmed this project**

## Character kits — *verified 27–28 Oct 2026, KeqingMains + Fandom*

**Skirk** — Cryo sword. Spends **Serpent's Subtlety**, not Elemental Energy, so
her energy sits at 0 permanently and 4pc Galleries is always active.
**Energy Recharge and Elemental Mastery are dead stats on her.**
A4 *Return to Oblivion*: each nearby **Hydro or Cryo** teammate landing a hit
grants a stack of Death's Crossing, max 3, one per teammate — roughly **+170%
Normal Attack damage** in Seven-Phase Flash at 3 stacks. Utility passive gives
the whole party **Elemental Skill +1** if everyone is Hydro or Cryo.
Her Normal Attack is Physical; she applies little Cryo herself.

**Varesa** — Electro catalyst, plunge DPS. **Ascension stat is CRIT Rate**
(+19.2% at max, so ~24% total before gear). Runs on Nightsoul points: Skill +20,
Plunge +25, 40 enters Fiery Passion. Short Burst costs 30 energy, no cooldown,
and counts as **Plunging Attack damage**. Rotation unit is **ECP** — Skill,
Charged Attack, Plunge. Talents: Normal Attack > Burst > level > Skill.

**Fischl** — Oz **snapshots her stats on summon**, so buffs must land before the
Skill. ER requirement is 120–140% solo Electro but only **100% with a second
Electro**. Talents: Skill first.

**Mika** — C6 gives **+60% Physical CRIT DMG**, which is useless to Cryo-
converted or Electro carries. Healing scales on **Max HP**.

**Chongyun** — Skill infuses sword/claymore/polearm Normal Attacks with Cryo,
+8% Normal Attack speed in the field, −10% Cryo RES when it expires.

**Kaeya** — current guidance is **Cryo/Freeze, not Physical**. 4pc Blizzard
Strayer.

**Alyosha** — A4 gives **+0.35% Skill and Burst DMG per 1% ER**, capping at
+70% at **200% ER**. A1 heals for **120% of his ATK**. His Stellar-Conduct buff
needs an enabler in the party. Talent books: **Teachings of Fortitude**
(Tue/Fri/Sun, Relics of the Fallen Grace, Fellfrost Peak).

## Exaiphanes Blade — *verified 28 Oct 2026*

Free 5★ sword for the Traveler from the Snezhnaya Archon Quest. **608 base ATK
and 33.1% CRIT Rate at Lv 90.** Costs **no** enhancement materials, ascension
materials or Mora.

Upgrade: Traveler in **Cryo** form, holding the blade, hits a **Snezhnaya
Statue of the Seven** with a Cryo Skill or Burst → interact → *View Exaiphanes
Blade*. **One upgrade per statue**, so each of the five is used once.

**Five statues reach Lv 90.** Full *refinement* needs seven upgrades, so R5 is
unreachable while only five statues exist — but refinement only scales the
**Traveler-only** passive. Base ATK and CRIT Rate apply to **any** character, so
a non-Traveler wielder loses nothing by the refinement cap.

## Genius Invokation TCG — *verified 28 Oct 2026*

- Character cards come **only** from beating that character at the Invitation
  Board in The Cat's Tail. **Cannot be bought.**
- Talent cards come from clearing *all* of that character's objectives.
- **Match Invitation Letter: 1,000 Lucky Coins**, needs Player Level 2. Stock is
  level-gated and only unlimited at **Player Level 10**; Levels 2–9 give 8 free.
- Shop tiers: **500** events and basic weapons · **700** all Supports and
  mid-tier weapons · **1,000** top-tier weapons and letters. Max 2 copies.
- Weekly Guest Challenge: 4 random guests inside Cat's Tail, unlocks at Player
  Level 2, they rotate out weekly whether beaten or not.

**Cards at exactly 3 dice** (Void Rift fuel for Skirk):
Swords — Sacrificial Sword, Favonius Sword, Aquila Favonia, Skyward Blade,
Light of Foliar Incision, Sapwood Blade.
Also — **Noblesse Oblige** (artifact, +1 Energy on play), **Paimon** and
**Dawn Winery** (both supports, both in the **Starter Deck**).

**Confirmed *not* 3 dice**: Changing Shifts, Leave It to Me!, Knights of
Favonius Library, Woven Ice, Northern Smoked Chicken (0) · Strategize,
Shattering Ice, Mondstadt Hash Brown, Far to Fall (1) · Instructor's Cap,
Liyue Harbor Wharf, Favonius Cathedral, Wangshu Inn, Adeptus' Temptation,
Starsigns, The Bestest Travel Companion! (2).

**Skirk's card**: 10 HP, Cryo, Sword. Burst consumes all Serpent's Subtlety for
that much Cryo DMG + 2 piercing to standby (3 if all 7 spent). Void Rift needs
a **3-cost card discarded** to give +2. The game's own Skirk deck is
**Skirk / Xingqiu / Escoffier**, built on repeated Frozen.

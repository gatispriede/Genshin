Local Legends — all 36 on the map

Every Local Legend and Valiant Chronicles pin in Teyvat. Click a dot to tick it off, or a numbered circle to fly to it. Each is named by its nearest landmark, because the map data gives these pins no names of their own.

The answer to "what AR do I need": 55 — but only for the rewards. The enemies stand in the open world at any rank, and you can fight them today. What needs Adventure Rank 55 is the Valiant Chronicles book from Katheryne, which is what actually pays out the Primogems. At AR 45 you can beat one and get nothing recorded for it.

Several are also gated behind specific World or Archon Quests regardless of rank — Nod-Krai's three all are, and Snezhnaya's Banje needs Great Deeds on the Tundra.
They cost no resin and respawn daily. Local Legends are resinless, and the overworld ones return at the 04:00 server reset. So every attempt is free and a failure costs you nothing but time — worth retrying a boss that beat you rather than saving up for it. The Totem Challenge sort can be retried immediately, without even waiting for reset.
Expect them to outlevel you. These are tuned for the AR 55 bracket. Churldric at Windrest Peak is Lv. 83 against your Lv. 70 ceiling — a 13-level deficit cuts your damage and raises his. Losing to one is usually the level gap, not your play.

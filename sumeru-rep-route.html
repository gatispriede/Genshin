Sumeru reputation quests: route, shortest first

33 quests that pay reputation, 19 of them behind a chain. Each number is a quest; 7a, 7b… are the quests you must do before quest 7, in order. Dots mark where each one starts. Generated 16 Nov 2026 from the official map's quest pins and the wiki.

Reading the map. Green pins are the quests that pay; small red pins are chain pre-quests. Yellow dots are approximate: the wiki names only the area. A pin ending in + is several starts at one spot; its caption lists them all. Every quest has its own toggle under the map, and its chain goes with it. City starts are on the close-up below.

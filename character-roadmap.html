Character roadmap

Where to spend levels, talent books and Mora — ordered by what the three teams actually need. Levels before artifacts, because which artifacts matter depends on who is in the team, and not the other way round. Written 12 Nov 2026 against the live roster.

The Four to Lv. 60 goal is finished. Dahlia, Fischl, Lisa and the Traveler all reached 60, which unlocked Normal Mode in the Theater and produced a five-act run against a previous best of three. That page is retired; this one replaces it.

First: the plunge set was not a waste

4-piece Long Night's Oath is still Varesa's best-in-slot — +100% Plunging Attack DMG. KQM and Prydwen both rate Obsidian Codex as the alternative, landing within 1–5% of it and chosen for being cheaper to farm, not better. The resin you spent at Derelict Masonry Dock bought the right thing.
Corrected 14 Nov: she is wearing it. This block used to say she had no set bonus, reading Lightkeeper's, Nightingale's Tail, Undying One's, A Horn Unwinded and Dyed Tassel as five sets. They are the five pieces of Long Night's Oath, and HoYoLAB confirms all five equipped. There is no missing set to recover; her gap is the ATK% goblet and circlet, not the set.

The rule that decides the order

Not every character's contribution scales with level. Some are paid in levels, some in talent books, some only in gear. Spending levels on a character whose job is level-independent is the same mistake as farming artifacts before you know the team.
Paid in…WhoWhy
LevelsXiangling · Mizuki · Bennett · Rosaria Their damage or buff scales directly with character level
Talent booksFurina · Escoffier · Mona The number that matters is a talent multiplier, not a stat
Gear onlyKachina · Sucrose · Traveler (Cryo) Their job is triggering a set or a passive — level changes nothing
The sharpest example is Bennett against Furina. Bennett's party ATK buff is a percentage of his Base ATK, which rises every time he levels — so levelling him directly increases the buff. Furina's party DMG buff comes from Fanfare × burst talent level and ignores her level entirely. Same role, opposite investment.

Tier 1 — do these

Highest return on the account. Each one unlocks something no amount of artifacts can.

#WhoNowTarget
1Xiangling40→ 70/80
The only elemental hole on the account. Pyro breaks Cryo and Frozen wards at ×2; right now no team has any and Team 3 chips them at ×1. She applies Pyro off-field through Guoba and Pyronado, so she costs no field time, and she founds the fourth team with Bennett. Pair with "The Catch" from the Fishing Association — refines to R5 from the shop without duplicates. See enemy-coverage.html.
2Traveler (Cryo)40→ 60, no further
Cheapest high-impact item here. He is the only Stellar Jubilee passive you own, and his presence converts Cryo Swirl into Stellar Swirl — which puts Mizuki into Radiance and roughly doubles her biggest hit. His own contribution is level-independent (the +7% needs 2,000 ATK he will never have), so level him only enough to survive. Unlock the Cryo form at the Snezhnaya Statues of the Seven first.
3Bennett when you get him—→ 80/90
Levels here are buff, not just survivability. His ATK bonus is up to 100.8% of Base ATK at talent 10, and character base ATK climbs with level. Wants high base ATK and ~200% Energy Recharge — ATK% does nothing for the buff. C1 removes the HP>70% condition and adds another 20%. Careful with C6: it Pyro-infuses sword, claymore and polearm users in his field, which would overwrite Skirk. Catalysts are safe.
4Yumemizuki Mizuki60→ 80
Team 2's carry. Stellar Glimmer base damage scales on level, and her payload is 1,000% of her EM, twice. Her artifacts are all at +0 to +9, so levels and artifact levels are both still unspent on her. See mizuki-build.html.

Tier 2 — needed, but check what you are buying

These characters are in teams. Levels are not always the right currency for them.

#WhoNowSpend on
5Furina27 Levels to 60 and stop. Then burst talent 1 → 10.
Lv 60 is the Theater gate and buys survivability — but her party buff ignores her level. The burst talent takes that buff from +21% to +75%, and it buffs everyone. That one talent is the best return on the account. Books are available every Sunday. See furina-build.html.
6Rosaria1→ 60/70
The only character wanted by two teams who is not levelled — Skirk's 4th slot and Mizuki's second Cryo. She brings up to 15% team CRIT Rate and turns on Cryo resonance (+15% CRIT Rate vs frozen), which matters now that Mizuki's payload crits. Expensive from Lv 1, which is the only reason she is not Tier 1.
7Mona58Gear first — 200%+ ER
Team 1's buffer. Her job is Omen and passing Thrilling Tales of the Dragon Slayers to Skirk, both of which want Energy Recharge far more than levels. Level her for the Theater gate, not for damage.

Charlotte

4★ Cryo catalyst · C2 · Lv 60 · healer in the Varesa team · talents 2 / 4 / 4 · read 16 Nov 2026 (HoYoLAB).

Why the healing feels thin: two of her five main stats do nothing for it. Her healing scales on ATK only. Her goblet (HP 31.3%) and circlet (HP 27.7%) add HP, which her heals ignore. Healing Bonus reads 0%.

How her healing works

Burst: Still Photo: Comprehensive Confirmation. On cast it heals all nearby party members based on her ATK. Then the Newsflash Field keeps healing only the active character, and only while they stand inside it, also based on her ATK.
  • Passive, Diversified Investigation: +5% Healing Bonus for each Fontainian in the party besides her. In the Varesa team that is Furina: +5%. (Varesa and Valeriy, as non-Fontainians, give her +10% Cryo DMG instead.)
  • Why the team needs it: Furina's Salon members drain the party's HP. Her Fanfare buff stacks on every HP change, so healing both keeps Varesa alive and builds Furina's damage buff. Too little healing costs damage as well as safety.

The percentages are main-stat values at +20. Steps 1 and 2 are both main-stat swaps, so together they should roughly double her healing per Burst before talents. That is an estimate, not measured in game.

How to play her for healing

  1. Burst before Varesa's damage window, centred on the enemy. Varesa fights in place with plunges, so she stays inside the field and keeps getting healed.
  2. Swap Charlotte → Varesa straight after the Burst. Thrilling Tales' +48% ATK goes to the character you swap to, so the order matters.
  3. If HP still runs low, recast the Burst as soon as it's ready instead of saving it. With Furina in the team, the heal also feeds Fanfare.
Not chosen: a full healing set (Ocean-Hued Clam), because it costs Varesa the Noblesse +20% ATK. Prototype Amber (a forgeable catalyst that heals) would cost her the Thrilling Tales +48% ATK hand-off, which is worth more to this team than the extra healing. Fix the main stats first.

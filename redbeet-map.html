Red Beet — Everfrozen Earth

Your own map, with the densest reported corridor shaded. The band is approximate — it marks the stretch guides point to, not individual nodes.

Look for small blue petals, not a red root. The pink-red body is mostly buried in snow; the only thing visible from standing height is a little blue flower. This is almost certainly why you've walked past them.

What the shaded band means

Guides place the densest Red Beet growth between Morepesok and the Inverted Glacier Domain. Morepesok is labelled on your map; the southern end is anchored on the domain icon directly south of it. Confirm that domain's name on your own map — if it isn't Inverted Glacier, shift the band to whichever one is.

How length is measured. You suggested deducting it from how much text a quest has, and that works — the wiki stores the full script. Dialogue is the size of each quest's dialogue section; Steps is its numbered objective list. Sorting on the two together turns "which are quickest" from a guess into a measurement. Treat it as an ordering, not a stopwatch: a short script with a long walk still takes time.
On pins. You asked for exact per-quest dots. The official map's World Quest layer has 576 pins with no names on them — every one returns a null label, so a pin cannot be tied to a quest. Rather than invent coordinates, this page gives the start NPC and subarea, which the wiki does record exactly. Searching the NPC's name in-game puts a marker on them.
Mondstadt and Snezhnaya are not here — Mondstadt by your request, and Snezhnaya has no Reputation system at all. Natlan is absent because it has no such category: it uses per-tribe reputation, a different system with its own tracks.

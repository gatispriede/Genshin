Shortest first, measured. Every quest that pays Reputation EXP is ranked by how much there is to do: Steps is its numbered objective list on the wiki, Dialogue the size of its script. Together they turn "which are quickest" from a guess into a measurement. Treat it as an ordering, not a stopwatch: a short script with a long walk still takes time.

Use the game as your checklist. The region's reputation NPC (Liyue: Ms. Yu, Reputation → Quests) lists every qualifying quest with an Achieved / Not achieved mark. Neither the wiki nor HoYoLAB knows which ones you have done; the game does. Mondstadt is not covered yet. Natlan uses per-tribe reputation, a different system, and Snezhnaya has none.

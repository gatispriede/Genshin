  1. 3a Drusus's Riddles hidden objective
    Bulletin Board · Sumeru City (map dot is approximate: area only)
    Step 1: Interact with the bulletin board outside the Adventurers' Guild in Sumeru
    Hidden objective: how to solve it
    1. Interact with the bulletin board outside the Adventurers' Guild in Sumeru
    2. 'First Riddle:' "In the light of alternating day cycles, one can see the homeward figure of the lighthouse on the bridge."
    3. 'Solution:' Below where the morning shadow of the lighthouse in Sumeru City's harbor meets the bridge section at the docks.
    4. Find the first Riddle Note in a floating crate beneath the docks
    5. 'Second Riddle:' "A mirror not made of glass reflects every drama."
    6. 'Solution:' In the reflective water basin around the fountain in front of the Zubayr Theatre at the Grand Bazaar.
    7. Find the second Riddle Note in a floating crate in the fountain of the Grand Bazaar
    8. 'Third Riddle:' "The sole path to knowledge."
    9. 'Solution:' Below the bridge in the antechamber of the Akademiya's library which one has to cross to access it.
    10. Find the third Riddle Note in an Exquisite Chest underneath the walkway in the House of Daena
    11. 'Fourth Riddle:' "The flowers in the pond are blooming, with watery pearls there lingering."
    12. 'Solution:' Near the blooming lotus flowers in the pond of the gardens on the Akademiya's upper levels.
    13. Find the fourth Riddle Note in a floating crate in the Razan Garden
    14. 'Fifth Riddle:' "Please go to the front door of the Temple of Knowledge at noon."
    15. 'Solution:' Meet Drusus around noon on the terrace in front of the Sanctuary of Surasthana at the top of the tree.
    16. Speak to Drusus at 10:00 – 14:00 outside the Sanctuary of Surasthana

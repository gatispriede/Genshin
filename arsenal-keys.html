The Arsenal — Keys to Some Place

Tatarasuna, Inazuma. The gate under the island needs three keys, each in a Common Chest. Maps and close-ups are the wiki's own location screenshots; the ringed marker on each map is the chest.

Inside: three Ruin Sentinels spawn the first time. Then a Luxurious Chest (30 Primogems, 30,000 Mora, 3 Hero's Wit, Diagram: Katsuragikiri Nagamasa — a free 4★ claymore), an Electroculus and an Enigmatic Page. The gate is on the east side of the cave, by the water.

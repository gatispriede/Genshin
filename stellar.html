Stellar Glimmer — and whether Varesa can run it

Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl are upgraded versions of Superconduct and Cryo Swirl that only exist under one condition. This is that condition, what they do, and exactly who on your account can take part.

The trip that unlocks all of this is one you already need to make. Switching the Traveler to Cryo happens at a Snezhnaya Statue of the Seven — which is the same place, and the same action, that upgrades the Exaiphanes Blade from Lv. 20 toward Lv. 90. One journey, two unlocks.
This resolves an open question in the notes. open-questions.md asked whether Alyosha had a Stellar-Conduct enabler anywhere on this roster, and recorded that no candidate was found, so his headline passive might be inert. The answer is yes — the Cryo Traveler. His passive was never dead, just unreachable.
Reaction and field text is quoted from the wiki rather than summarised, because the difference between "replaces Superconduct" and "adds to Superconduct" changes how you build. Portraits and element data come from the local avatar index.

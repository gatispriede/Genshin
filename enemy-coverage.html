Enemy shields

What the three teams can break, what they cannot, and the one gap that is not a gear problem. Mechanics verified against the Genshin Impact Wiki's Ward and Ward/Gauge Data pages, 12 Nov 2026.

Enemy shields come down on elemental reactions, not on damage. Each element removes gauge units from a ward at a fixed rate. Hitting a ward with the wrong element is close to pointless no matter how hard you hit it.
Anemo and Geo are 0.5 against every ward. Swirl and Crystallize — two of this account's main damage engines — contribute almost nothing to breaking shields. Mizuki and Sucrose are passengers in these fights.

And the enemy's own element

Separate from wards: most enemies resist the element they are made of, so the team that breaks the shield is not always the team that kills the thing behind it.

Cryo enemies are the one place both effects stack against you. A Cryo-aligned enemy with a Frozen ward resists Team 1 and is immune to its shield-breaking. Teams 2 and 3 chip it at ×1 with Electro.
Worst case on the board: the Cryo Hypostasis. An 88 GU Frozen revival ward you can only chip at ×1 with Electro (Varesa, or Valeriy in team 2), on a boss that revives if you do not break it in time — and the Skirk team cannot help at all.
This already showed up in play. In the 7.2 Tactical Sweep, Mondstadt's Cryo Regisvine was the worst of the four stages and the only one still short of 18,000. 11a-tactical-sweep-bosses.md records that it takes 18.5% damage while shielded. Same cause.

The fix is one character, not gear

Xiangling, currently Lv 40. Pyro, and she applies it off-field through Guoba and Pyronado — so she breaks Cryo wards without taking field time from a carry. She is also widely rated the best 4★ in the game, and already the Pyro slot in the Mizuki swirl variant.

She does not just patch a gap — she gives you the one answer nobody has. Right now Cryo / Frozen is ×1 at best. One Pyro slot makes it ×2, and makes it available from whichever team she joins rather than limiting you to Electro chip damage.

Pair her with “The Catch” from the Inazuma Fishing Association — Elemental Burst DMG and Energy Recharge, refined to R5 from the shop without duplicates. She also unlocks Burning against Dendro wards and Melt alongside your Cryo teams.

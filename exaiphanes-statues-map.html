The five Snezhnaya statues

Each one levels the Exaiphanes Blade. No Mora, no ore, no ascension materials.

You've done one. Four left. Your blade is at Lv 20, which is upgrade 1 of 5. Finishing the other four takes it to Lv 90 — 608 base ATK and 33.1% CRIT Rate, and then it goes on Skirk.
Red = the one you've almost certainly already done (the Archon Quest puts you next to it).
Green = the four that are still worth a trip.

What to do at each statue

1 · Switch Traveler to Cryo 2 · Equip the Exaiphanes Blade 3 · Hit the statue with a Cryo Skill or Burst 4 · Interact → "View Exaiphanes Blade" 5 · Confirm
One upgrade per statue. You can't farm the same statue twice — each of the five gives exactly one. That's why the trip is worth making.

The five, in the order the story reaches them

1
Volkodlak Tundra — Sretomorozsk The southernmost region, connected to Nod-Krai. The Archon Quest Everwinter Without Mercy drops you here — after the train crash the Traveler wakes up beside it. This is almost certainly your Lv 20.
2
Everfrozen Earth — Snezhnograd Just outside Central Station. Take the Funicular up from the train station; going up the stairs unlocks it and uncovers a large chunk of map.
3
Flamefeather Valley — Morepesok The port town in the west. Take the train from Rankova Zorya station, set the route to Morepesok, then follow the tracks on foot.
4
Fellfrost Peak — Svetloledovka Unlock the Svetloledovka waypoint, then head directly south. A few cliffs to climb; the region has teleporting ice constructs that shortcut it.
5
White Birch Snowgrave — Pale Crown Palace In a cave, which is why it's the one people miss. Teleport to the Korolevskiy Theater waypoint, head north to the train tracks, then follow them east to a fork. Gliding east along the railway from the Theater works too.

While you're there

Bring your Cryoculi. You have 19 collected. Statue levels are what raise your stamina cap, and each level pays 100 Primogems, Adventure EXP and Cryo Sigils — plus an Acquaint Fate at Lv 2 and Snezhnaya Shrine of Depths Keys at Lv 4 and Lv 6. Offering at any one of the five raises the shared regional level, so it doesn't matter which you use.
Then move the blade. Its passive is Traveler-only, but base ATK and CRIT Rate apply to anyone. Skirk is a sword user at 20.5% CRIT Rate whose current weapon (Prototype Rancour, Physical DMG 28.4%) contributes nothing to her damage, because Seven-Phase Flash converts her attacks to Cryo. Hand her the blade and give Prototype Rancour to Noelle or Razor, where physical damage works — and where your C6 Mika adds +60% Physical CRIT DMG.
Verified 28 Oct. Five statues do reach Lv 90 — 608 base ATK and 33.1% CRIT Rate. Full refinement needs seven upgrades, so R5 is out of reach while only five statues exist — but refinement only scales the Traveler-only passive. Base ATK and CRIT Rate apply to anyone, so Skirk loses nothing from the cap. No materials, no Mora, one upgrade per statue.

Quest map — every reputation quest

The shortest-first list from Reputation quests, put on the real game map. What the reputation is for: the treasure compasses. Click a dot for the start NPC, the measured length and the wiki's own start-location screenshot. Colour is how long the quest is.

Dots are area-accurate, not pin-accurate. The official map's 576 World Quest pins carry no names, so none of them can be tied to a quest. What the wiki does record exactly is each quest's subarea, and the map's anchor list gives every named area a position — so each dot sits on its named place, fanned out where several quests share one. The screenshot in the panel is the precise spot.

How her kit actually works

She runs on Nightsoul points, not on her Burst. Her Skill gives 20, each Plunge gives 25 (if she's in combat when she lands, or the shockwave connects). At 40 points her next Plunge puts her into Fiery Passion — and if she crosses 40 with a Plunge, she enters it immediately. So Skill + one Plunge is enough. Two Plunges also work.
E
Elemental Skill +20 Nightsoul points. Gains an extra tap charge while in Fiery Passion.
C
Charged Attack Launches her into the air. This is the only reason it's in the rotation — it sets up the Plunge.
P
Plunging Attack Damage along the path plus a large AoE on impact. This is her damage. Her Ascension 1 passive raises the impact damage after a Skill, and raises it further during Fiery Passion.
Q
Short Burst (sQ) 30 energy, no cooldown. Used right after a Plunge drops her out of Fiery Passion. It counts as Plunging Attack damage, so every plunge buff applies to it. Usually better than her full Burst — cheaper and a shorter animation.
Fiery Passion boosts her Normal, Charged and Plunging attacks for up to 15 seconds. A Plunge during it spends all her points and ends it — that's intended, it's the payoff hit. It also ends early if she runs out of points or leaves combat.

Rotation at C0

The building block is ECP — Skill, Charged Attack, Plunge.

E · Skill C · Charged Attack P · Plunge (sQ) · if energy allows
1
First rotation only: one ECP, then swap out Banks Nightsoul points before the team buffs land.
2
Set the team up Fischl's Oz, your Anemo grouping, heals — then bring Varesa back in.
3
The loop: ECP (sQ) · ECP · ECP (sQ) Short Burst whenever a Plunge drops her out of Fiery Passion and she has 30 energy.
4
Finish with two CPs Charged Attack → Plunge, twice, with no Skill. This resets the cycle so she starts the next rotation with an enhanced Skill ready.
Talent priority: Normal Attack first (her plunges scale off the Normal Attack talent), then Elemental Burst, then character level, then Elemental Skill last. Materials and the domain schedule are in 03-character-materials.md.

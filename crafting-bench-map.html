Where to craft bait

Any Crafting Bench — the alchemy table. Not the blacksmith, not the cooking pot.

You don't need this map. Crafting Benches are already marked on your own map with the alchemy symbol. Open the map, look for that icon, teleport to the nearest one. The map below is just for the two you'll use most.
Also permanent:
Chihu Rock, Liyue Harbor — south of this view
Albedo's Campsite, Dragonspine — handy during In the Mountains
Recipes — each set of ingredients makes 10 bait:
Fruit Paste — 1 Sunsettia + 1 Wheat (you have this by default)
Redrot — 1 Dendrobium + 1 Fowl
False Worm — 1 Slime Condensate + 1 Berry
Fake Fly — 1 Sakura Bloom + 1 Horsetail

The last three need blueprints from Nantuck at the Mondstadt Fishing Association, just outside the city — 3 Medaka each. Catch a few Medaka with Fruit Paste first, then buy the rest.

Shortcut: place an Alchemist's Crafting Bench inside the Serenitea Pot once it's unlocked and skip the trip entirely.

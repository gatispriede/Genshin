5-star artifact troves: every fixed spot on the map

22 places in the open world hand out 5-star artifacts with no resin. Most are Hidden Troves: collect the keys from chests nearby, open the trove, take two full 5-star sets. Each opens once and never refills, so they are free gear for every account at Adventure Rank 45 or higher.

How it works. Each trove takes the keys of its own area (seals, rings, guides), which drop at random from Precious and Luxurious Chests there. Once you hold a full set of keys, the trove can be marked on the map from the item in your inventory or with that region's Treasure Compass. Inazuma, Sumeru and Fontaine have no trove: their 5-star pieces come from domains and bosses (see Artifacts). Click a pin to see the spot; numbers match the cards below each map.

Mondstadt

One trove: the Noblesse Secret Treasury on Windrest Peak, the only trove that needs two keys instead of three.

1 Noblesse Secret Treasury Trove · Windrest Peak
Unlock: 2× Ancient Mondstadt Royal Seal, from random Precious and Luxurious Chests in Windrest Peak.
Inside: Two full 5-star sets: Aubade of Morningstar and Moon and A Day Carved From Rising Winds, plus a Dust of Enlightenment.

Liyue

No trove, but one quest vault with a guaranteed 5-star piece. It is a single flower, not a set.

2 Nine Pillars of Peace Quest vault · Cuijue Slope
Unlock: 9× Stone of Remembrance, from offerings to the Geo Statues of The Seven in Liyue; read the inscription at the pin to start the world quest.
Inside: One 5-star piece: Royal Flora (5-star Noblesse Oblige flower).
The back room also holds a Luxurious and a Precious Chest. Only the host can claim it in co-op.

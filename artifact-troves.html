5-star artifact troves: every fixed spot on the map

22 places in the open world hand out 5-star artifacts with no resin. Most are Hidden Troves: collect the keys from chests nearby, open the trove, take two full 5-star sets. Each opens once and never refills, so they are free gear for every account at Adventure Rank 45 or higher.

How it works. Each trove takes the keys of its own area (seals, rings, guides), which drop at random from Precious and Luxurious Chests there. Once you hold a full set of keys, the trove can be marked on the map from the item in your inventory or with that region's Treasure Compass. Inazuma, Sumeru and Fontaine have no trove: their 5-star pieces come from domains and bosses (see Artifacts). Green numbered pins are the troves; small blue pins are the Precious and Luxurious Chests in each trove's key areas, from the official map (it does not mark area borders, so a chest near an edge may belong to the next area). Toggling a trove under its map shows or hides its chests. Click any pin for a picture of the spot; numbers match the cards below each map.

Mondstadt

One trove: the Noblesse Secret Treasury on Windrest Peak, the only trove that needs two keys instead of three.

1 Noblesse Secret Treasury Trove · Windrest Peak
Inside: Two full 5-star sets: Aubade of Morningstar and Moon and A Day Carved From Rising Winds, plus a Dust of Enlightenment.
To open it:
  1. Reach Adventure Rank 45: troves stay shut below it.
  2. Collect 2× Ancient Mondstadt Royal Seal: they drop at random from Precious and Luxurious Chests in Windrest Peak. The official map has not tagged chest tiers in Windrest Peak yet, so there are no chest pins for it: open every Precious and Luxurious Chest you find there.
  3. Mark the trove on the map from the key in your inventory (or with that region's Treasure Compass) and go to the pin.

Liyue

No trove, but one quest vault with a guaranteed 5-star piece. It is a single flower, not a set.

2 Nine Pillars of Peace Quest vault · Cuijue Slope
Inside: One 5-star piece: Royal Flora (5-star Noblesse Oblige flower).
To open it:
  1. Make offerings to Liyue's Statues of The Seven (they take Geoculi) until you hold 9 Stones of Remembrance. The statues are the small pins on this trove's layer.
  2. Read the inscription at the pin to start the world quest Nine Pillars of Peace, then set a stone in each of the nine pillars around Cuijue Slope.
  3. Win the fight inside the vault, then take the Royal Flora from the back room, with a Luxurious and a Precious Chest. Only the host can claim it in co-op.

5-star artifact troves: every fixed spot on the map

22 places in the open world hand out 5-star artifacts with no resin. Most are Hidden Troves: collect the keys from chests nearby, open the trove, take two full 5-star sets. Each opens once and never refills, so they are free gear for every account at Adventure Rank 45 or higher.

How it works. Every trove opens from Adventure Rank 45. Its keys drop at random from Precious and Luxurious Chests in its own area; once you hold them all, mark the trove from the key in your inventory (or with that region's Treasure Compass) and go to the pin. Green numbered pins are the troves, small blue pins the chests that can drop their keys; toggling a trove under its map shows or hides its chests. Inazuma, Sumeru and Fontaine have no trove: their 5-star pieces come from domains and bosses (see Artifacts). The official map does not mark area borders, so a chest near an edge may belong to the next area.

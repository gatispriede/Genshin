5-star artifact troves: Nod-Krai

8 fixed spots in Nod-Krai that give 5-star artifacts with no resin.

Every trove opens from Adventure Rank 45. Its keys drop at random from Precious and Luxurious Chests in its own area; once you hold them all, mark the trove from the key in your inventory (or with that region's Treasure Compass) and go to the pin. Green numbered pins are the troves, small blue pins the chests that can drop their keys; toggling a trove under its map shows or hides its chests. All regions.

Nod-Krai

Kuuhenki's Hidden Troves (Lunar Treasuries): six on the main map, two more on the Lunar Highlands and Moontide Sea map below. Each needs three seals named after its own sub-area.

1 Lunar Treasury: Lempo Isle Trove · Lempo Isle
Inside: Two full 5-star sets: Night of the Sky's Unveiling and Silken Moon's Serenade, plus a Dust of Enlightenment.
To open it: Collect 3× Evermoon Seal (Lempo Isle) from chests while exploring Lempo Isle. 9 of those chests are on the map (small pins on this trove's layer).

Vault of Qingce

The cavern behind the waterfall north of Qingce Village, with the floating golden orb inside.

That gate isn't a puzzle. No lever, no torch sequence, nothing to hit. It opens through the world quest The Chi of Yore — the same questline as the statues.
What to do
  1. Talk to Granny Ruoxin in Qingce Village to start it, if you haven't
  2. Collect all three Chi fragments:
    • Shrine, east Qingce — 5 statues, N → SE → NW → NE → SW
    • Wuwang Hill summit — 8 statues, pick 4 (no order)
    • West of Qingce, bamboo-covered hill
  3. Return — the Vault opens, and a secret room at the back becomes reachable
Statue solutions in chi-of-yore-puzzles.html and wuwang-statue-puzzle.html.
Lore: the vault is rumoured to hold the remains of the Chi, and the secret room is built in Qingce Village style.

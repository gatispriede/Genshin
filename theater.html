Imaginarium Theater — who to field, by face

The game never puts a name on the cast row, so this is the same six characters as pictures, with the only two things that decide the pick stamped on each one: rarity and element.

How to read the cast row in game. The card background is the rarity tell — gold is 5★, purple is 4★. This season gives you one of each per element, so the three purple cards already cover Hydro, Electro and Geo between them. An opening team is the three purples plus one gold, never two golds.

Imaginarium Theater — how to plan a run

One page for the whole mode: the rules that decide a run, how to spend Vigor, which cards to take, and this season's cast and bosses. Merged from four older pages (season picks, the Normal-mode plan, the run log and the retry plan).

The one rule. Spend your weakest viable four on the early Acts and keep the strongest characters fresh, so the last Act meets a full-strength team. A run that opens with its best four arrives at the final boss with passengers.

  • Every character has 2 Vigor and spends one per Act. Normal is 12 characters for 24 slots: zero slack, so everyone plays exactly two Acts.
  • The count includes the six Opening Characters (Lv 90 trials). Your own characters must be in the season's three elements and above the level floor.
  • A failed Act costs no Vigor. Retry freely. Rewards bank per Act, so a partial run still pays.
  • Rewind returns to the last cleared Climactic Showdown and un-ticks the Acts after it. Never finalise straight after a rewind.
  • Owning an Opening Character gives it +20% Max HP, ATK and DEF for the whole season, in and out of the Theater.

Plan in this order

  1. Sort the cast into carries and passengers. Twelve eligible characters are not twelve usable ones: a bare Lv 60 cannot clear an Act. Every Act needs one carry.
  2. Your best four each play one early Act plus the final Act. Holding one back entirely wastes their second Vigor.
  3. Act 1 can only use the six trials. Field the three 4★ (purple cards) plus one 5★ (gold card). The 4★ usually cover all three elements between them.
  4. Check each Act's Star Challenge (often a 65–80 s timer). A team without a carry clears a soft Act but misses its star.
  5. Invite a friend's Supporting Cast character at the start: one more body, +2 Vigor of slack.

Act 1: Aino · Fischl · Kachina plus one gold. Spend Zhongli or Flins there and keep Yelan fresh. Special Guest Stars: Qiqi, Odette, Vesna, Jahoda.

Act 3: Jadeplume Terrorshroom

Electro fills its Fury gauge (2% per hit). When it fills, the boss enrages, then lies paralysed for about 15 s at 0% RES (normally 25%). Hit the head then.

Act 6: Lord of the Hidden Depths

The boss raises a Deepdark Shield (45% of its HP) and summons four Fishers. Only elemental damage hurts the shield; Lunar reaction damage does 300% to it; each Fisher killed removes 25%. If the shield is not broken within ~18 s, the boss eats the Fishers, hits the whole arena (200% ATK, true damage), then drops all RES by 70% and is paralysed for 10 s. Failing the shield is how you get the damage window.

  1. Shield phase: shield up, kill three Fishers, leave the fourth, build energy.
  2. Arena attack: take it near full HP, behind a shield.
  3. Paralysis, 10 s: everything goes here. Zhongli's −20% RES stacks with the −70%. Let a Lv 90 trigger Electro-Charged: the reaction scales with the triggering character's level and EM.
  4. Repeat.

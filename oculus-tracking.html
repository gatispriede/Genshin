Tracking which oculi you've already collected

Checked against the live HoYoLAB map in your own logged-in browser, 27 Oct 2026.

There is no automatic sync. Nothing reads what you picked up in-game and ticks it off the map. The marking is manual — but once you mark a pin it is saved to your HoYoLAB account, so it follows you across devices.
What that buys you: turn on Hide Marked Pins and the map stops showing anything you've already done. From then on every pin you can see is a pin you still owe. That is the closest thing to a real tracker.

1 · Mark a pin

Click any pin → the card has two buttons at the bottom.

A
Mark Pin Ticks off this one pin. It greys out immediately and is stored against your account.
B
Batch Mark The one worth knowing. Puts the map into a mode where every pin you click gets marked, without reopening a card each time. Use it after a collecting run — sweep the area you just cleared in one pass.

2 · Hide what you've done

Gear icon, top right.

1
Hide Marked Pins — turn this ON Marked pins vanish from the map. What's left is your to-do list. This is the whole point of marking.
2
Hide Exploration Progress Leave it off. The map does pull your real in-game exploration percentage per region from your account — that part is automatic. It's region %, though, not which individual oculi you have.
The right-hand rail (marked ⚙ ◎ ⤳ ▽ on the screenshot above): gear = settings, ◎ = centre on your in-game position, ⤳ = Routes, ▽ = My Marked Pins.

3 · My Marked Pins

The ▽ icon. Lists every category you've marked anything in.

★
The checkbox per row Toggles that category's layer on the map.
⋯
The ⋯ menu — two entries Timer Settings — respawn countdown for things that regrow (ore, local specialities). Cancel all markers — wipes every mark in that category. There is no undo, so don't press it to "clean up".
Your account already has marks in Anemoculus, Mondstadt keys and Luxurious Chest. So someone — you — has been marking. Worth continuing rather than starting over.

The in-game tracker that does work automatically

1
Resonance Stones Place one and it points at the nearest uncollected oculus of that type — the game knows what you have, so this never sends you to one you already took. One charge, 30s to re-place.
2
How to get one — corrected 28 Oct They are not sold by a vendor; I had this wrong. You receive Instructions: Anemoculus Resonance Stone as the Mondstadt Reputation Level 2 reward, then craft it via Alchemy. The Geoculus version is the same at Liyue Reputation Level 2; Electro, Dendro and Hydro arrive at Reputation Level 8 in their regions. Reputation itself opens at AR 25 and Level 2 is only 400 EXP — about four world quests. See reputation-compass.html.
3
The minimap check Stand near a known oculus location. If the floating diamond isn't on your minimap, you already took it. Crude, but it's free and it's authoritative.

Also on that map, worth knowing

Routes (the ⤳ icon) — pre-built harvest circuits for ascension materials. Winter Icelea, Moonfall Silver, Portable Bearing, Skysplit Gembloom, Dracolite, Withering Purpurbloom, Glowing Hornshroom, Rainbow Rose, Saurian Claw Succulent, Brilliant Chrysanthemum — each one a node order with counts. You can also build your own under My Routes. Combined with Timer Settings this is a real farming tool, not just a map.
What does not exist: importing collected oculi or opened chests from the game. The only in-game import is your own self-placed in-game pins, and it is one-way with no un-sync. Collected state does not transfer.

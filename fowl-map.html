Fowl — the easy way

Don't chase birds. Buy it.

Draff, in Springvale. Sells 10 Fowl daily at 200–240 Mora each. Restocks every day. One trip beats an hour of shooting pigeons.
Finding him: he moves with the clock. In front of Springvale during the day (06:00–19:00, near the water at the village entrance), and in front of his house at night. He also stocks Raw Meat and Eggs.

If you'd rather farm it

1
Bridge outside Mondstadt's gates Reliable bird spawns on the approach to the city. Flat ground, quick loop.
2
Any lakeside or beach Birds cluster near water. Drops that land in the water can be swum out to.
3
Konda Village, Inazuma The best open-world source by far — the repeatable Warden of Konda event gives roughly 40–60 Fowl across 10 runs a day. You have 0 Electroculi, so Inazuma isn't open yet.
Use a bow. Birds flee the moment you get close — Fischl or Amber from range. Melee characters will just watch them fly off.
What you need it for: Redrot Bait — 1 Dendrobium + 1 Fowl, makes 10 bait. That's the bait for Venomspine Fish, Akai Maou, Betta, Lunged Stickleback and Snowstrider, all of which are night-only. See fishing-spots-map.html.

Also feeds Sweet Madame, Smoked Fowl, Chicken-Mushroom Skewer and Jueyun Chili Chicken.
Unverified: some guides claim Chef Mao in Liyue Harbor and Tsuyuko in Inazuma also sell Fowl. Better sources only list Draff and Ashpazi in Gandharva Ville, Sumeru — so don't make a trip to Liyue Harbor on that basis alone.

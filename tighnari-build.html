Tighnari — Dendro bow, two ways to run him

Same character, two jobs. Main DPS keeps him on field and lives off his Charged Attack. Quickswap uses Skill and Burst, then leaves. The gear is genuinely different, so pick a lane before you farm artifacts.

Weapons are 3★ and 4★ only, as asked — no 5★ listed. Anything you already own is marked Owned, read from your weapon data.
One correction worth having up front. Tighnari is not a true off-field sub-DPS. Unlike a Xingqiu or Fischl, nothing in his kit deals damage while someone else is on field — his Burst fires its vines over a few seconds and his Mine needs him present to place it. What people call his "sub-DPS" build is a quickswap: a short window where he does Skill, Burst, and leaves. If you want genuine zero-on-field Dendro, that is a different character.

Build 1 — Main DPS (Charged Attack)

Hold to the second aim stage; the arrow splits into Clusterbloom Arrows that track. Everything here is judged on whether it helps that shot — which is why one bow you own is a trap.

Wanderer's Troupe best on paper, unfarmable

2pc — Elemental Mastery +80

4pc — Charged Attack DMG +35% for Catalyst and Bow users

Written for exactly this build and unconditional. But no domain drops it — it only comes from weekly bosses and the Artifact Strongbox, so you cannot farm towards it. Treat it as something you may eventually assemble, not a plan.

Farm: no domain drops it — weekly bosses and the Artifact Strongbox only

Deepwood Memories farm this one

2pc — Dendro DMG +15%

4pc — after a Skill or Burst hits, target's Dendro RES −30% for 8s

Still excellent on him, because the shred boosts his own Dendro damage too, and unlike Wanderer's Troupe it is actually farmable. It does not stack, so only one holder per team. This is the realistic main-DPS set.

Farm: Spire of Solitary Enlightenment, Avidya Forest, Sumeru

Gilded Dreams reaction teams

2pc — Elemental Mastery +80

4pc — after a reaction, EM and ATK buffs from teammates' elements

Farmable from a domain, so the easiest of the three to finish. His A4 scales damage off Elemental Mastery, so the EM is not wasted.

Farm: Spire of Solitary Enlightenment, Avidya Forest, Sumeru

Instructor 3–4★ stopgap

4pc — on reaction, +120 EM to the whole party for 8s

Caps out quickly, but a finished Instructor set beats three good pieces and two empty slots while you farm the real one.

Farm: not a domain set — world bosses, weekly bosses and chests

Main DPS stats — Sands: ATK% or EM · Goblet: Dendro DMG · Circlet: CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG. Substats: CRIT at roughly 1:2 rate-to-damage, then ATK% and EM. Energy Recharge barely matters — you are not bursting every rotation.

Weapons for main DPS

Prototype Crescent best 4★

ATK% substat

Charged hits on a weak point give +36% ATK for 10s. His Clusterbloom Arrows home in, so the passive stays live more than it looks. Craftable at a blacksmith — costs no wishes, which makes it the realistic target.

Slingshot Owned best 3★

CRIT Rate substat

Hits landing within 0.3s of firing deal +36%; beyond that, −10%. Punishing at range, excellent up close. At R5 it is reckoned equal to The Stringless — this is a real weapon, not a placeholder, and you have it today.

Sharpshooter's Oath 3★

CRIT DMG substat

+24% DMG against weak points, which is where he aims anyway. A CRIT DMG substat on a 3★ bow is rare enough to matter.

Blackcliff Warbow

CRIT DMG substat

Stacking ATK after a kill. Strong stat line, but it costs Masterless Starglitter in Paimon's Bargains — spend that on Fates first.

Rust Owned do not use

ATK% substat

Raises Normal Attack DMG 40%, lowers Charged Attack DMG 10%. His whole kit is Charged Attacks, so it is an active downgrade here — a fine bow on somebody else.

Rotation

Supports first to pre-buff, then Skill → three Charged Attacks → Burst, and stay on field for more Charged Attacks. Skill-then-Burst-then-charge is the simplest ordering and also the weakest; the Mine wants to be down before the arrows land.

Build 2 — Quickswap (the "sub-DPS")

In, Skill, Burst, out — about three seconds. The job is Dendro application and the Deepwood shred for somebody else's damage, so the gear optimises for getting the Burst back every rotation rather than for crits.

Deepwood Memories the whole point

2pc — Dendro DMG +15%

4pc — after a Skill or Burst hits, target's Dendro RES −30% for 8s

The shred keeps working after he leaves the field, which is exactly why this build exists. If he is not holding Deepwood in a quickswap role, he is doing very little for the team.

Instructor cheap and genuinely fine here

4pc — on reaction, +120 EM to the whole party for 8s

A quickswap Tighnari is not trying to crit, so low-rarity main stats cost far less than they would on the DPS build. Good holding pattern until Deepwood is done.

Quickswap stats — Sands: EM, or Energy Recharge if your Burst is not coming back · Goblet: Dendro DMG or EM · Circlet: EM. Target 130–150% Energy Recharge; with a second Dendro character feeding particles you can sit at the lower end. CRIT is a bonus, not a goal.

Weapons for quickswap

Favonius Warbow Owned best owned

Energy Recharge substat

CRIT hits generate particles. On the main DPS build this is a damage loss — here it is the point, because it solves the one problem the build has. You already own it, so this role costs you nothing.

The Stringless best 4★ if you pull one

Elemental Mastery substat

+24% Skill and Burst DMG — which is all this build's damage. The EM substat feeds his A4 as well. Gacha-only, so do not count on it.

Sacrificial Bow if you get one

Energy Recharge substat

Chance to reset the Skill cooldown — a second Mine in the same window means more particles and more Dendro application.

Rotation

Skill → Burst → swap out. Do not stay for Charged Attacks; the next character's window is worth more. The Deepwood shred and the Burst's vines keep running without him.

What to do today: you own both halves. Run him quickswap with Favonius Warbow while his artifacts are bad — it needs no crit and no investment. Switch to main DPS with Slingshot or a crafted Prototype Crescent once you have a real Wanderer's Troupe or Deepwood set.
New to you at AR 45: the Artifact Strongbox. At a crafting bench, Mystic Offering converts three 5★ artifacts into one random piece of a set you choose. That is the only route to sets no domain drops — Wanderer's Troupe and Gladiator's Finale among them — and it is a good home for the 5★ junk World Level 6 is about to start giving you.
One run, two materials. The Jadeplume Terrorshroom drops both his Nagadus Emerald and all 46 Majestic Hooked Beaks, so one boss covers two columns. The Gemstones need bosses at Lv. 75+, which World Level 6 now gives you.
What you cannot start yet. The Meaning of Aeons comes from the Raiden Shogun trounce domain in Inazuma — 18 for maxed talents. With no Inazuma progress, talents stop at level 6 until you get there. Inazuma quests are on the quest map.

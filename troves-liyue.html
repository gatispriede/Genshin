5-star artifact troves: Liyue

1 fixed spot in Liyue that gives 5-star artifacts with no resin.

Every trove opens from Adventure Rank 45. Its keys drop at random from Precious and Luxurious Chests in its own area; once you hold them all, mark the trove from the key in your inventory (or with that region's Treasure Compass) and go to the pin. Green numbered pins are the troves, small blue pins the chests that can drop their keys; toggling a trove under its map shows or hides its chests. All regions.

Liyue

No trove, but one quest vault with a guaranteed 5-star piece. It is a single flower, not a set.

1 Nine Pillars of Peace Quest vault · Cuijue Slope
Inside: One 5-star piece: Royal Flora (5-star Noblesse Oblige flower).
To open it:
  1. Make offerings to Liyue's Statues of The Seven (they take Geoculi) until you hold 9 Stones of Remembrance. The statues are the small pins on this trove's layer.
  2. Read the inscription at the pin to start the world quest Nine Pillars of Peace, then set a stone in each of the nine pillars around Cuijue Slope.
  3. Win the fight inside the vault, then take the Royal Flora from the back room, with a Luxurious and a Precious Chest. Only the host can claim it in co-op.

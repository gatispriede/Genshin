The three teams

All three were built and played by the account owner and all three work. This page records why they work, the artifact set each slot wants, and which alternatives exist on this roster. Verified 12 Nov 2026 against the Genshin Impact Wiki for mechanics and against KQM, Prydwen, Mobalytics and the character subreddits through Google's AI search for set and team consensus.

Two domains cover almost everything below. Sanctum of Rainbow Spirits (Obsidian Codex + Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City) serves Varesa, Escoffier and Kachina. Derelict Masonry Dock (Finale of the Deep Galleries) serves Skirk.

1 · Skirk freeze

Skirk Lv 80 · main DPS · Mona Lv 58 · buffer · Escoffier Lv 70 · Cryo + heals · Mika Lv 45 · 4th

Why it works — her two passives, read from the wiki

Reason Beyond Reason. When nearby party members trigger Frozen, Superconduct, Stellar-Conduct, Cryo Swirl, Stellar Swirl or Cryo Crystallize, a Void Rift spawns near that opponent — once every 2.5s, max 3 at a time. Skirk absorbs them for Death's Crossing stacks, each worth +10% / +20% / +40% ATK.

That is the real constraint: the team has to keep Cryo reactions happening. Mona's Hydro onto Escoffier's Cryo makes Frozen on repeat, which is the engine.

Correcting a widely repeated claim. Community guides say Skirk "heavily punishes" non-Hydro / non-Cryo teammates. She does not. The relevant passive, Mutual Weapons Mentorship, reads: "When all party members are either Hydro or Cryo, and if there is at least 1 Hydro and 1 Cryo character, your own party members' Elemental Skill Level is increased by 1." It is a bonus you forgo, not a penalty — useful to know before you refuse a better off-element support.

2 · Mizuki Stellar Swirl

Yumemizuki Mizuki Lv 60 · on-field · Funisimo (Traveler, Cryo) Lv 40 · Stellar enabler · Rosaria Lv 1 · second Cryo · Barbara Lv 70 · Hydro, heals

This team is correct and I argued against it. Mizuki's damage is not Swirl — it is Vast Be the Dream, which adds 1,000% of her Elemental Mastery to her next AoE Anemo hit, and Radiance: Stellar Swirl, which adds a second instance at another 1,000% of EM. The Cryo Traveler is the only Stellar Jubilee passive on this account, and he roughly doubles her biggest hit.

Full build, sets and the rotation are on mizuki-build.html. The short version: EM sands, EM goblet, CRIT circlet, 4pc Gilded Dreams or 2pc/2pc EM, and move Viridescent Venerer onto Sucrose.

Why Rosaria and not another Hydro: a second Cryo turns on Cryo resonance, +15% CRIT Rate against Cryo-affected and Frozen targets — which matters here because Mizuki's payload is an ordinary damage instance that crits.

Mizuki cannot Normal Attack in Dreamdrifter, so Xingqiu- and Yelan-style appliers that key off the active character's Normal Attacks do nothing. Every applier in this team has to tick on its own.

3 · Varesa Electro-Charged

Varesa Lv 90 · main DPS · Furina Lv 27 · Hydro + buff · Charlotte → Kachina Natlan buffer · Sucrose Lv 50 · VV

Charlotte is working against this team. Cryo in an Electro + Hydro line-up does two unhelpful things: Cryo + Furina's Hydro freezes the target, which locks the aura and shuts Electro-Charged off entirely; and Cryo + Varesa's Electro is Superconduct, which shreds Physical RES and is worth nothing to her.
Swap her for Kachina on 4pc Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City. Kachina triggers the set through Crystallize and, because she is Natlan and in Nightsoul's Blessing, reaches its big half — +40% Elemental DMG Bonus for the whole party. She is free from the Archon Quest and needs almost no investment to do this one job.

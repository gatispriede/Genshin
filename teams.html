The three teams

All three were built and played by the account owner and all three work. This page records why they work, the artifact set each slot wants, and which alternatives exist on this roster. Verified 12 Nov 2026 against the Genshin Impact Wiki for mechanics and against KQM, Prydwen, Mobalytics and the character subreddits through Google's AI search for set and team consensus.

Two domains cover almost everything below. Sanctum of Rainbow Spirits (Obsidian Codex + Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City) serves Varesa, Escoffier and Kachina. Derelict Masonry Dock (Finale of the Deep Galleries) serves Skirk.

1 · Skirk freeze

Why it works — her two passives, read from the wiki

Reason Beyond Reason. When nearby party members trigger Frozen, Superconduct, Stellar-Conduct, Cryo Swirl, Stellar Swirl or Cryo Crystallize, a Void Rift spawns near that opponent — once every 2.5s, max 3 at a time. Skirk absorbs them for Death's Crossing stacks, each worth +10% / +20% / +40% ATK.

That is the real constraint: the team has to keep Cryo reactions happening. Mona's Hydro onto Escoffier's Cryo makes Frozen on repeat, which is the engine.

Correcting a widely repeated claim. Community guides say Skirk "heavily punishes" non-Hydro / non-Cryo teammates. She does not. The relevant passive, Mutual Weapons Mentorship, reads: "When all party members are either Hydro or Cryo, and if there is at least 1 Hydro and 1 Cryo character, your own party members' Elemental Skill Level is increased by 1." It is a bonus you forgo, not a penalty — useful to know before you refuse a better off-element support.

2 · Mizuki Stellar Swirl

Yumemizuki Mizuki Lv 70 · on-field · Funisimo (Traveler, Cryo) Lv 40 · Stellar enabler · Rosaria Lv 20 · second Cryo · Sucrose Lv 50 · second Anemo, EM share

Rotation

#WhoWhat
1SucroseSkill: shares 20% of her EM with the party; Instructor adds +120 party EM after the reaction.
2Traveler (Cryo)Skill: Cryo on the enemy. Stellar Swirl only exists because he is in the party. From the 2nd rotation also Burst + charged attack; skip both on the first.
3RosariaSkill, then Burst: the Ice Lance keeps off-field Cryo up for 12s (her C2), so Mizuki always has Cryo to swirl.
4SucroseBurst every OTHER rotation: it refreshes her EM share each time it hits.
5MizukiSkill, and stay on field. With Sucrose’s Burst up, take 4 nukes; without it, take 3 and swap out.
6MizukiPull-out tech: as her 3rd shot lands, dash out of her Skill range and straight back. That moves a nuke earlier. Then time her Burst between shots 10 and 11, so the 4th nuke lands on shot 11.

Zajef77, “The Ultimate Mizuki Guide (Part 1)”, 1:15:19 and 1:26:11 (Sucrose team rotations). His team runs Odette in Rosaria’s slot. He wants Sucrose on Sacrificial Fragments for the extra Skill; on Fruit of Fulfillment you will sometimes Burst instead of Skill. Two rotation shapes alternate: A = Sucrose Burst, 4 nukes; B = Sucrose Skill only, 3 nukes.

✅ Working since 16 Nov: Vast Be the Dream unlocked at Lv 70. ~23k hits often, ~65k crits. Valeriy moved to the Varesa team; Sucrose took his slot (a second Anemo who also triggers Stellar Swirl, with no Electro to eat the Cryo). See mizuki-build.html.
This team is correct and I argued against it. Mizuki's damage is not Swirl — it is Vast Be the Dream, which adds 1,000% of her Elemental Mastery to her next AoE Anemo hit, and Radiance: Stellar Swirl, which adds a second instance at another 1,000% of EM. The Cryo Traveler is the only Stellar Jubilee passive on this account, and he adds a hit about four times her enhanced one (Stellar reaction DMG ignores DEF and gets a large EM bonus).

Full build, sets and the rotation are on mizuki-build.html. The short version: EM sands, EM goblet, CRIT circlet. Keep 4pc Viridescent Venerer on Mizuki and put Sucrose on 4pc Instructor: two VV sets don't stack.

Why Rosaria and not another Hydro: a second Cryo turns on Cryo resonance, +15% CRIT Rate against Cryo-affected and Frozen targets — which matters here because Mizuki's payload is an ordinary damage instance that crits.

Mizuki cannot Normal Attack in Dreamdrifter, so Xingqiu- and Yelan-style appliers that key off the active character's Normal Attacks do nothing. Every applier in this team has to tick on its own.

3 · Varesa Electro-Charged

Varesa Lv 90 · main DPS · Furina Lv 60 · Hydro + buff · Charlotte Lv 60 · heals, Noblesse · Valeriy Lv 60 · C6 · Electro buff, shield

As played from 16 Nov, hitting ~60k. Valeriy replaced Sucrose.

Rotation

#WhoWhat
1VaresaFirst rotation only: one Skill → Charged Attack → Plunge (ECP), then swap out. This banks Nightsoul points before the buffs land.
2FurinaSkill (Salon members), then Burst (Fanfare: party DMG buff that grows with HP changes).
3ValeriySkill: shield. Burst when ready: at C6 its shells deal double damage, and enemies they hit take +10% Electro CRIT Rate for 7s.
4CharlotteBurst: party heal, Noblesse +20% ATK, and a healing field. Swap to Varesa right after: Thrilling Tales gives the next character +48% ATK.
5VaresaThe loop inside Charlotte’s field: ECP (short Burst if 30 energy) · ECP · ECP (short Burst).
6VaresaFinish with Charged Attack → Plunge twice, no Skill. Her next rotation then starts with an enhanced Skill.

Varesa’s loop is from varesa-build.html (Rotation at C0); the support order is set for this line-up, so that Charlotte is last and Thrilling Tales goes to Varesa. Valeriy wears Tenacity of the Millelith, whose +20% ATK lasts only 3s after his Skill hits, so most of it is over before Varesa’s loop. His value here is the shield and his C6 crit debuff.

Charlotte is working against this team. Cryo in an Electro + Hydro line-up does two unhelpful things: Cryo + Furina's Hydro freezes the target, which locks the aura and shuts Electro-Charged off entirely; and Cryo + Varesa's Electro is Superconduct, which shreds Physical RES and is worth nothing to her.
Swap her for Kachina on 4pc Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City. Kachina triggers the set through Crystallize and, because she is Natlan and in Nightsoul's Blessing, reaches its big half — +40% Elemental DMG Bonus for the whole party. She is free from the Archon Quest and needs almost no investment to do this one job.

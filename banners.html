What any of this is worth to you

research/02's standing position is don't pull, save, and nothing in this lineup changes that. Four notes against the specific items:

Skirk's signature		 Skip. The free Exaiphanes Blade already fixes her weapon slot — 608 base ATK and 33.1% CRIT Rate, no ascension materials, no Mora. It is sitting on your Traveler at Lv 20.

You have three weeks to decide, so decide later, not today.

The selector — pick one of eight, free, by tomorrow

Pool: Jean · Diluc · Qiqi · Mona · Keqing · Tighnari (already owned — don't) · Dehya · Yumemizuki Mizuki.

Monamy pick

Hydro, so she is Skirk's third Death's Crossing stack — and that step is the biggest in Skirk's kit: two stacks multiply her Seven-Phase Flash Normal Attacks by 1.20, three by 1.70. That is +42% damage from one character. All-Hydro/Cryo also switches on Skirk's utility passive, giving the whole party Elemental Skill +1.

The decisive part for this account: her value is a buff, not her own damage. Omen is +42% DMG at Burst talent 1, rising to +60% at talent 10 — so she is worth having before you spend a single artifact, Mora or EXP book on her. Nothing else in the pool is useful while unbuilt. Her Hydro also freezes with Skirk's Cryo, which is what generates Void Rifts and her Serpent's Subtlety.

Qiqiclose second

Cryo, so she also gives the third stack and keeps the mono team. Her C6 is reckoned the most valuable constellation in the standard pool. Ranked below Mona here only because you already have two healers — Barbara at Lv 66 and a C6 Mika — and no second damage amplifier.

Yumemizuki Mizukithe consensus pick

General tier lists put her first of the eight, and I am deliberately not following them. She is an Anemo Swirl carry: she would eventually replace Sucrose, hold 4pc Viridescent Venerer and group enemies under Varesa's plunge — all real. But a carry needs the artifacts, EXP and Mora you do not have, and Anemo breaks Skirk's mono Hydro/Cryo team. Pick her if you would rather have the strongest character and build her in three months than the most useful one tonight.

The restno

Jean — Anemo healer, flexible, but her shred wants C4 and Sucrose covers the grouping. Diluc / Keqing — you do not need a third carry; you cannot feed the two you have. Dehya — niche. Tighnari — you own him at C1.

5-star artifact troves: Mondstadt

1 fixed spot in Mondstadt that gives 5-star artifacts with no resin.

Every trove opens from Adventure Rank 45. Its keys drop at random from Precious and Luxurious Chests in its own area; once you hold them all, mark the trove from the key in your inventory (or with that region's Treasure Compass) and go to the pin. Green numbered pins are the troves, small blue pins the chests that can drop their keys; toggling a trove under its map shows or hides its chests. All regions.

Mondstadt

One trove: the Noblesse Secret Treasury on Windrest Peak, the only trove that needs two keys instead of three.

1 Noblesse Secret Treasury Trove · Windrest Peak
Inside: Two full 5-star sets: Aubade of Morningstar and Moon and A Day Carved From Rising Winds, plus a Dust of Enlightenment.
To open it: Collect 2× Ancient Mondstadt Royal Seal from Precious and Luxurious Chests in Windrest Peak. The official map has not tagged chest tiers in Windrest Peak yet, so there are no chest pins for it.

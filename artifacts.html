Artifacts & budget weapons — what to wear and farm

The sets worth farming, sorted by job; main stats per slot; when 5★ pieces start dropping; and the 3★ weapons that hold their own. Merged from the artifact-sets research and the budget weapons guide. Every set's full text is on the full list of artifact sets.

Main stat first, set bonus second, substats third. A correct-element goblet and a CRIT circlet do more than a matched 4-piece with the wrong main stats.

When 5★ pieces drop: from Adventure Rank 40 (domain level III and world bosses). AR 45 adds domain level IV, a guaranteed 5★ per run, and the Artifact Strongbox, so keep spare 5★ pieces at +0 as Strongbox fuel. At AR 45 also open the 5★ artifact troves: 22 fixed spots that give full 5★ sets once, no resin. Level 4★ pieces to +12 at most; destroy, never sell, the rest.

Instructor and The Exile are 3★ sets that stay useful for a long time; keep them for supports. Defender's Will, Archaic Petra and Husk of Opulent Dreams are defensive or Geo niches; Scholar, Lucky Dog, Adventurer and Traveling Doctor are fodder.

100k on screen

What it takes on Varesa and Skirk · computed 1 Nov 2026 from the 28 Oct Chronicle snapshot and the Project Amber talent data on disk.

Character level is not the gate, and neither of them reaches 100k at C0 on weapons you own. Varesa at Lv 70 crits for about 7.6k today. Taking her to Lv 90 and nothing else gets her to roughly 17k. The remaining 6× is talents, main stats, CRIT and a resistance shred — and the last stretch needs her signature catalyst. Skirk's realistic ceiling at C0 is about 74k. One of those rows has its own section: where Varesa's +35% ATK comes from.

The honest target

Aim for 50–75k crits. That is reachable on this account with gear you can farm. 100k needs either Vivid Notions (Varesa's 5★) or constellations.

Nightsoul Burst — where that +35% comes from

You own exactly one Natlan character: Varesa. That is enough to make Nightsoul Bursts happen at all — but only at the slowest interval, which caps her A4 passive at one stack, about 67% of the time. The second stack is arithmetically out of reach until you own a second Natlan unit.

How the mechanic actually works

A Nightsoul Burst is a trigger, not an attack — it deals no damage of its own, it just fires Natlan-tagged passives. It goes off when a party member deals Elemental DMG to an opponent, provided the party holds at least one Natlan character. Then it goes on cooldown, and the cooldown depends on how many Natlan characters are in the party:

Natlan characters in partyBurst interval Varesa's A4 (12s, max 2 stacks)
1 — all you can field18s 1 stack, 67% uptime. 2nd stack impossible
212s1 stack at ~100%; 2nd stack only momentarily
3+9s2 stacks genuinely sustainable

The buff lasts 12 seconds. At a 18-second interval it expires six seconds before the next burst can fire, and two stacks would need two bursts inside one 12-second window — which needs the 12s or 9s interval. That is the whole constraint, and no amount of levelling changes it.

What each stack is worth

Her A4 is +35% ATK per stack, and ATK% applies to base ATK — character plus weapon — so at Lv 90 on a Lv 90 Widsith each stack is worth +303 ATK. On the polished Lv 90 build from the ladder above:

Does Varesa's own hit count?

Her A4 reads "when a nearby party member triggers a Nightsoul Burst." It matters, because she is the on-field unit landing most of the Electro damage — if her own hits don't count, she could consume the 18s cooldown and never get her own buff.

Reading the data on disk, they almost certainly do count. Skirk's A4 in the same payload format says "nearby Cryo party members apart from Skirk" — the game spells out self-exclusion when it means it. Varesa's text has no such clause. Prydwen also reports one stack is generally maintainable with her as the sole Natlan unit, which fits. Marked as a reasoned inference, not verified.

Either way the insurance is the same, and it is already in her recommended team: Fischl. Oz ticks Electro damage off-field continuously, so the instant the 18s cooldown lapses something that is not Varesa lands an elemental hit. Barbara's Melody Loop and Xiangling's Guoba do the same job less reliably.

The one real fix: a second Natlan character, free

Kachina is a free 4★ Natlan Geo unit from the Natlan Archon Quest, Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn. Picking her up takes the burst interval from 18s to 12s, which turns Varesa's one stack from 67% uptime into near-permanent — and removes the trigger-attribution worry entirely, because Kachina is unambiguously not Varesa.

You do not need to grind Fontaine first: the quest normally wants Chapter 4 Act 6 behind you, but Quick Start opens it at AR 28 and you are AR 40. Natlan is at 2% exploration, so this is almost certainly unclaimed.

Unverified: sources disagree on which act hands Kachina over, and one notes the Turbo Twirly event page closed after 6.0 while the event itself stayed permanent. Check the in-game Events menu before planning around it. One copy only — her constellations still need banner pulls.

What character level is actually worth

Lv 70 → 90 on Varesa moves her base ATK from 272 → 357 and her free CRIT Rate from 14.6% → 24.2%. Because weapon and artifacts dominate the ATK pool, that is only about +13% total ATK. The enemy-DEF multiplier improves from 0.496 to 0.523 — another +5%. Level is worth roughly 1.2×; everything else on this page is worth more.

Enemy level barely moves the number either. Varesa's ceiling-with-Widsith crit against an enemy at Lv 1 is 92.3k; against Lv 95 it is 69.7k. Hitting something weak gets you 32% more, not the 35% you'd still need. There is no cheap target that makes 100k appear.

How these numbers were made

tools/hit-calc.py. Talent multipliers come straight from reference/data/char_10000111.json and char_10000114.json, so they can't go stale. Base ATK per level is rebuilt from the same payloads (initValue × curve(L) + ascension bonus); the curve was solved from Skirk's published per-level table and then checked against Varesa's independently published Lv 80 and Lv 90 values — it reproduces both to within a point.

Damage formula: ATK × MV × (1 + DMG%) × finalMult × (1 + CD) × defMult × resMult, with defMult = (charLv+100) / ((charLv+100) + (enemyLv+100)) and resMult = 1 − RES. "Average" folds CRIT Rate in; "On a crit" is the number you actually see pop.

Assumptions worth knowing. Varesa's plunges are treated as high plunges (a low plunge is ~20% less). Long Night's Oath is at 5 stacks in the Lv 90 rows and 3 in the early ones. Skirk's Serpent's Subtlety bonus is applied to the final slash as written ("the DMG dealt by an instance of this Elemental Burst"). Her artifact ATK% / flat split is back-solved from the Chronicle total, and Prototype Rancour's Lv 70 base ATK (≈458) is estimated from its 565 Lv 90 tier — it only affects that split, by a couple of percent.

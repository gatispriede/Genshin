Escoffier — Cryo polearm, off-field sub-DPS and healer

She is not a carry and should not be built like one. Her job is Cryo and Hydro RES shred, off-field Cryo damage, and healing — and the character she does all that for is already on your account.

She is Skirk's best teammate, and your notes already demanded her. Skirk's A4 gives a Death's Crossing stack per Hydro or Cryo teammate, worth roughly +170% Normal Attack damage at three stacks. Escoffier is Cryo, so she gives a stack, keeps the mono Hydro/Cryo passive alive, and shreds the RES Skirk is hitting into. Skirk's page is the other half of this.
Weapons are 3★ and 4★ only, as asked. Worth saying plainly: you own no good polearm. Beginner's Protector, Halberd and Black Tassel are what you have, and none of them is a real answer. The fix is craftable — see below.

Artifacts

Four-piece. The split is whether you want her shred and healing to be reliable, or her own numbers to be bigger. For a Skirk team, reliability wins.

Golden Troupe her damage

2pc — Elemental Skill DMG +20%

4pc — Skill DMG +25%, and a further bonus while off field

Built for exactly her pattern: cast the Skill, leave, let it tick. The off-field clause is the part that matters — most sets go dead the moment she swaps out.

Farm: Denouement of Sin, Belleau region, Fontaine

Blizzard Strayer freeze teams

2pc — Cryo DMG +15%

4pc — +20% CRIT Rate on Cryo-affected enemies, more if frozen

Enormous when freeze uptime is real, worthless against anything that cannot be frozen. A condition set — take it only if the team actually freezes.

Farm: Peak of Vindagnyr, Dragonspine — needs AR 30 and the In the Mountains quest

Tenacity of the Millelith support mode

2pc — HP +20%

4pc — Skill hits give all nearby party members +20% ATK for 3s

Give up her own damage, hand Skirk the ATK instead. Sensible when the carry out-damages her so heavily that her personal numbers are rounding error.

Farm: Ridge Watch, Liyue — AR 25

Instructor 3–4★ stopgap

4pc — on reaction, +120 EM to the whole party for 8s

Caps out fast, but a finished cheap set beats three good pieces and two empty slots. She is a support — low-rarity main stats cost her far less than they would a DPS.

Farm: not a domain set — world bosses, weekly bosses and chests

Stats — Sands: ATK% (or Energy Recharge if her Burst is late) · Goblet: Cryo DMG · Circlet: CRIT DMG. Substats: Energy Recharge until the Burst is reliable, then ATK%, CRIT DMG, CRIT Rate. Her healing scales with ATK, so ATK is never wasted.

Weapons — 4★ and 3★

Prototype Starglitter craft this

Energy Recharge substat

The Energy Recharge substat is the single thing her build most needs, and it is craftable at a blacksmith — no wishes, no Starglitter, no banner. Given you own no good polearm, this is the realistic answer today.

Favonius Lance best if you pull one

Energy Recharge substat

CRIT hits make particles for the whole team. The standard support polearm and a common 4★, so it will turn up eventually.

Sacrificer's Staff best 4★ on paper

ATK% substat

Skill hits stack up to +24% ATK and +18% Energy Recharge — both of her needs at once, and it keeps working off field. Gacha-only, so do not plan around it.

Deathmatch damage pick

CRIT Rate substat

The highest personal damage of the 4★ options, but it gives her no energy. Only once her Burst already comes back on time.

Halberd Owned use for now

ATK% substat

The least bad thing you own. ATK% feeds both her damage and her healing, so it is not useless — just temporary. Put it on her until Prototype Starglitter is forged.

Black Tassel Owned wrong stat

HP% substat

HP does nothing for her — her healing scales on ATK, not HP. A fine stick for an HP-scaling character; the wrong one here.

Rotation

Skill, Burst, swap to Skirk. The Skill keeps applying Cryo and the shred off field, the Burst heals and keeps the team topped up. Do not linger — nothing she does on field beats Skirk being on field.

What to do today: forge Prototype Starglitter, run Halberd until it is ready, and farm a cheap finished Instructor set rather than half a Golden Troupe. She works at C0 with a 4★ polearm — this is a low-investment character who makes an expensive one much better.
New to you at AR 45: the Artifact Strongbox. At a crafting bench, Mystic Offering converts three 5★ artifacts into one random piece of a set you choose. That is the only route to sets no domain drops — Wanderer's Troupe and Gladiator's Finale among them — and it is a good home for the 5★ junk World Level 6 is about to start giving you.
Farm her and Skirk together — the overlap is unusually large. Identical Shivada Jade requirements, from the same two Dragonspine bosses. Identical Clockwork Meka gears, from the same Fontaine enemies. Their talent books differ (Justice Tue/Fri/Sun versus Contention Mon/Thu/Sun) but Sunday covers both. Only the local specialty splits: Beryl Conch for her, Skysplit Gembloom for Skirk.
Her conches are underwater. Of the 105 Beryl Conch nodes on the map, 103 are below the surface — this is a swimming trip, not a walking one. The route is mapped, 20 warps, the biggest serving 17.

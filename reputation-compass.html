Treasure Compasses

What each one costs in Reputation, and the fastest way there. Figures from the wiki Reputation table, 28 Oct 2026 · Sumeru plan added 12 Nov 2026.

Worked plans: Sumeru → Dendro · Inazuma → Electro · Fontaine → Hydro

⚠️ Added 28 Oct — claim before you grind. Reputation EXP from world quests and exploration is retroactive, but you have to claim it by hand. It waits in the Reputation menu under World Quests and World Exploration. At AR 51 with Mondstadt at 70.4%, you are almost certainly sitting on a large unclaimed pile in both cities. Go and collect it before doing a single new quest — it may finish the job outright.
Which quests, and where. Reputation quests ranks every quest in Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru and Fontaine shortest first, using each one's measured objective count and dialogue length — they all pay the same 100 EXP, so the short ones are pure profit. The quest map puts the same list on the real game map with every teleport, and a click gives the start NPC and the wiki's start-location screenshot. (Liyue only, older and kept for its claim walkthrough: liyue-reputation-quests.html.)
The headline: do world quests, not bounties. Weekly bounties and requests are capped and shared across every nation — 3 bounties and 3 requests a week, total, not per region. World quests are uncapped and worth 100 Reputation EXP each. For Mondstadt and Liyue the quests alone are enough to get the compass with no waiting at all.

The shared cap is the whole trap. Three bounties and three requests a week is your limit across every region at once — spending them in Mondstadt means none left for Liyue that week. That's about 840 EXP a week maximum, so grinding Mondstadt to Level 6 on bounties alone would take roughly three weeks of waiting. The same 2,200 comes from 22 world quests you can do back to back this afternoon. Weekly reset is Monday 04:00 server time.

So, the fastest order

1
Mondstadt world quests → Anemo Compass 22 world quests at 100 each is 2,200 — exactly the threshold, and the three Archon acts add 600 more as margin. No weekly gating at all. Level 6 also hands you Instructions: Portable Waypoint on the way.
2
Liyue world quests → Geo Compass Same 2,200 threshold, but 6,300 EXP available. You'll trip over Level 6 long before you run out of quests — and Liyue is your second-most-explored region at 22.7%, so you're partway already.
3
Burn the weekly 3+3 on whichever region you're not questing in Since they're shared and uncapped sources aren't, use bounties and requests to top up the region you're neglecting. Never let a week pass unused — that EXP doesn't bank.
4
Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine — much later Level 9 and 5,000 EXP, and the quests alone don't cover it. Each is also gated behind its Archon Quest line. Sumeru now has a worked plan: jump to it — it needs all 33 world quests, all 5 Archon acts and one week of weeklies, and the exploration grind turns out not to be worth doing.

I told you to buy the Anemoculus Resonance Stone from Marjorie. That's wrong. It isn't sold anywhere. Sumeru → the Dendro Treasure Compass

Researched 12 Nov 2026 against the wiki Reputation tables. Every figure below is exact except the bounty rate, flagged in Sources.

Sumeru cannot reach the compass from quests and exploration alone. It is short by 100 EXP. Level 9 needs 5,000. Every uncapped source in the region added together comes to 4,900. There is no subset to optimise and no slack to find — at least one weekly bounty is mandatory, and that is the whole puzzle.

Do not grind exploration for this. All three exploration milestones together pay 600 — the same as six short world quests — and they ask you to take Sumeru from 0% to 60% across both the rainforest and the desert. One week of weeklies pays roughly 840. Skip exploration as a reputation target; take it only if you happen to pass the thresholds while questing.

The perfect list

1
Unlock the system — two gates, both mandatory Archon Quest The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings (Chapter III Act II, so Act I first), then the world quest Corps of Thirty Recruitment. The Reputation NPC is Effendi. Adventure Rank 25 is also required — you are AR 51, so that one is already clear.
2
All 5 Archon Quest acts → 1,000 EXP You need Acts I–II anyway to open the system. The remaining three are the best EXP per minute in the region at 200 each, double a world quest.
3
All 33 world quests → 3,300 EXP Uncapped, no waiting, 100 each. Every single one is required — there is no room to skip. Do them shortest-first: the ranked list orders all 33 by measured objective count, and the quest map puts them on real terrain with start NPCs.
4
One week of weeklies → closes the last 100 After steps 2–3 you are at 4,300 and need 700 more. Three bounties plus three requests in a single week is about 840 — enough on its own. Spend that week's allowance in Sumeru and nowhere else, since the 3+3 is shared across every nation. Reset is Monday 04:00 server time.
5
Forge the diagram Level 9 gives Diagram: Dendro Treasure Compass, not the compass. Take it to a blacksmith and budget the ore.

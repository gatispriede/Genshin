Treasure Compasses

What each one costs in Reputation, and the fastest way there. Figures from the wiki Reputation table, 28 Oct 2026.

⚠️ Added 28 Oct — claim before you grind. Reputation EXP from world quests and exploration is retroactive, but you have to claim it by hand. It waits in the Reputation menu under World Quests and World Exploration. At AR 51 with Mondstadt at 70.4%, you are almost certainly sitting on a large unclaimed pile in both cities. Go and collect it before doing a single new quest — it may finish the job outright.
Which quests, and where. Reputation quests ranks every quest in Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru and Fontaine shortest first, using each one's measured objective count and dialogue length — they all pay the same 100 EXP, so the short ones are pure profit. The quest map puts the same list on the real game map with every teleport, and a click gives the start NPC and the wiki's start-location screenshot. (Liyue only, older and kept for its claim walkthrough: liyue-reputation-quests.html.)
The headline: do world quests, not bounties. Weekly bounties and requests are capped and shared across every nation — 3 bounties and 3 requests a week, total, not per region. World quests are uncapped and worth 100 Reputation EXP each. For Mondstadt and Liyue the quests alone are enough to get the compass with no waiting at all.

Mondstadt and Liyue are the cheap ones by a mile — Level 6 and 2,200 EXP, versus Level 9 and 5,000 for the Inazuma/Sumeru/Fontaine trio. And Liyue has 57 world quests worth 5,700 EXP, nearly three times what the compass needs.

The shared cap is the whole trap. Three bounties and three requests a week is your limit across every region at once — spending them in Mondstadt means none left for Liyue that week. That's about 840 EXP a week maximum, so grinding Mondstadt to Level 6 on bounties alone would take roughly three weeks of waiting. The same 2,200 comes from 22 world quests you can do back to back this afternoon. Weekly reset is Monday 04:00 server time.

So, the fastest order

1
Mondstadt world quests → Anemo Compass 22 world quests at 100 each is 2,200 — exactly the threshold, and the three Archon acts add 600 more as margin. No weekly gating at all. Level 6 also hands you Instructions: Portable Waypoint on the way.
2
Liyue world quests → Geo Compass Same 2,200 threshold, but 6,300 EXP available. You'll trip over Level 6 long before you run out of quests — and Liyue is your second-most-explored region at 22.7%, so you're partway already.
3
Burn the weekly 3+3 on whichever region you're not questing in Since they're shared and uncapped sources aren't, use bounties and requests to top up the region you're neglecting. Never let a week pass unused — that EXP doesn't bank.
4
Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine — much later Level 9 and 5,000 EXP, and the quests alone don't cover it. These need world quests plus exploration milestones plus weeks of bounties. Each is also gated behind its Archon Quest line.
For your account specifically: Inazuma and Sumeru are at 0% and Fontaine at 2% — you haven't run those Archon Quests, so their reputation systems aren't open yet regardless of EXP. Your Adventure Rank is fine (Sumeru needs AR 35, Fontaine AR 40; you're AR 51). Treat "all cities" as Mondstadt and Liyue for now and come back to the rest after you've actually opened those regions. ⚠️ Correction — the Resonance Stone

I told you to buy the Anemoculus Resonance Stone from Marjorie. That's wrong. It isn't sold anywhere. You get Instructions: Anemoculus Resonance Stone as the Mondstadt Reputation Level 2 reward, and then craft it via Alchemy. Same for the Geoculus version at Liyue Reputation Level 2 — and the Electro, Dendro and Hydro ones at Reputation Level 8 in their regions.
That's good news, though. Reputation Level 2 is only 400 EXP — four world quests. So the resonance stone that finds your remaining 16 Anemoculi is four quests away, not a shopping trip, and it's on the same path as the compass.

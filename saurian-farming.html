Tyrant's Fang — Natlan saurian farming

Where the Saurians that drop Tyrant's Fang stand, the routes that clear the most of them per lap, and the rules that decide how many days it takes. Merged from the plan, the written routes and the spawn map pages.

Only Saurians at Lv 60 or above drop Tyrant's Fang (World Level 6+ in the open world). Below that they drop Juvenile and Seasoned Fang, which still count: fangs convert 3 : 1 upward at any Alchemy table, so one Tyrant's Fang is nine Juvenile Fangs.

Rules that change the plan

RuleWhat it means
RespawnOpen-world Saurians reset at the daily server reset (04:00). A multi-day farm: one best route a day.
Crafting3 Seasoned Fang → 1 Tyrant's Fang for a small Mora fee. Never leave Seasoned Fangs in the bag when short.
Shop top-upStardust Exchange sells Juvenile Fang only, 3 for 5 Masterless Stardust, monthly cap. A couple of Tyrant's Fang a month at most.
SpeciesEach species clusters near its own tribe, but all drop the same fangs. Farm the densest region, not a favourite species.

Seven shorter loops (6–8 each) exist in the same three tribes. Route totals by tribe: Children of the Echoes 58, Scions of the Canopy 56, People of the Springs 44.

The best route: Scions of the Canopy, Route 1

  1. From Tecicpac Peak waypoint: 8 at the water's edge and the cliff to the southeast. If you have five minutes a day, take these 8 and leave.
  2. Climb the cliff: 1 to the north, 1 to the northeast; then 2 under the southern cliff.
  3. East along the river: 4 on the cliff near the bridge.
  4. North to the wooden scaffolding: 2 in the valley below it.
The official map knows a tighter spot than any route: its 946 Saurian pins hold a cluster of 28 around Tezcatepetonco Range, against 17 for the best written route. Open the HoYoLAB interactive map, filter Saurians, and park there.

The Lv 60 gate still holds. Drop rarity by name colour: blue = Tyrant's Fang, green = Seasoned, grey = Juvenile.

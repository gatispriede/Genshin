Domains — all 74 entrances

Every domain entrance in Teyvat on the real map. Click a dot to tick it off, or a numbered circle to fly to it. The list on the right names each one by its nearest landmark, since the map data gives domains no names of their own.

One-time domains are free Primogems. First completion costs no resin — the chest just opens — and the payout is fixed: it is the same at AR 45 as at AR 60. Adventure Rank only gates access, never reward size, and World Level does not affect domains at all. So nothing was lost by waiting, and nothing is gained by waiting longer.
The map cannot tell the two kinds apart, so you tag them. The official map has a single Domain category and the pins carry no names at all. Nothing else fills the gap either: the game lists 48 rotating talent and weapon domains, but those are instances sharing a handful of physical doors, so no source maps a name to a coordinate.

So: open one, and if it is a talent, weapon or artifact domain that charges resin, press tag on its row. It greys out, drops out of the count, and Hide resin domains removes it from view. One pass and this page shows only the free one-time clears you actually want.
The gate is party level, not Adventure Rank. Travels of the Moonlit Venturer asks for party level 70; your roster runs Varesa 70, Barbara 66, Skirk 60, Fischl 50. Domains scale on their own fixed level rather than yours, so the high ones are a real fight. Clear the low-recommendation ones first — those are free.

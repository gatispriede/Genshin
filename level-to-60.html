Four to Lv. 60 — what to farm, and where

Everything needed to take Dahlia, the Traveler, Fischl and Lisa to Lv. 60, which is the gate on Normal Mode in the Theater. Grouped by where it comes from rather than by character, because that is the order you do it in.

Do the free things first. The Brilliant Diamond may already be waiting at Katheryne, the gathering costs no resin and runs on a 48-hour respawn, and the common drops accumulate while you do anything else. Only two bosses need resin at all, and each one drops both the boss material and the gem for the characters it serves.
Don't race the season. The Theater's element requirement rotates on the 1st, and December is leaked as Pyro/Hydro/Geo — which would drop Fischl, Lisa and Varesa out of eligibility. The order above puts Dahlia and the Traveler first precisely because they survive that rotation. Chasing Normal Mode in November buys six Acts once.
Quantities come from the per-phase ascension costs in the local character data, so they cannot drift from the rest of the project. The reasoning, the difficulty gates and the Easy Mode run that prompted all this are in research/10-imaginarium-theater.md.

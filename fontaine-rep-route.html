Fontaine reputation quests: route, shortest first

39 quests that pay reputation, 18 of them behind a chain. Each number is a quest; 7a, 7b… are the quests you must do before quest 7, in order. Dots mark where each one starts. Generated 18 Nov 2026 from the official map's quest pins and the wiki.

Reading the map. Green pins are the quests that pay; small red pins are chain pre-quests. Yellow dots are approximate: the wiki names only the area. Each pin is captioned with who to talk to. A pin ending in + is several starts of one quest at one spot; its caption lists them. Every quest has its own toggle under the map, and its chain goes with it.

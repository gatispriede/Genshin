5-star artifact troves: Snezhnaya

5 fixed spots in Snezhnaya that give 5-star artifacts with no resin.

Every trove opens from Adventure Rank 45. Its keys drop at random from Precious and Luxurious Chests in its own area; once you hold them all, mark the trove from the key in your inventory (or with that region's Treasure Compass) and go to the pin. Green numbered pins are the troves, small blue pins the chests that can drop their keys; toggling a trove under its map shows or hides its chests. All regions.

Snezhnaya

Shadow Realm Treasure Vaults: three documented on the wiki, plus two the official map added with Version 7.2.

1 Shadow Realm Vault: Everfrozen Earth Trove · Everfrozen Earth
Inside: Two full 5-star sets: Scarlet Proof and Heart of the Furnace, plus a Dust of Enlightenment.
To open it: Collect 3× Remnant Incense Guide - Everfrozen Earth from Precious and Luxurious Chests in Everfrozen Earth and Flamefeather Valley. 11 of those chests are on the map (small pins on this trove's layer).
2 Shadow Realm Vault: Fellfrost Peak Trove · Fellfrost Peak
Inside: Two full 5-star sets: Scarlet Proof and Heart of the Furnace, plus a Dust of Enlightenment.
To open it: Collect 3× Remnant Incense Guide - Fellfrost Peak from Precious and Luxurious Chests in Fellfrost Peak and Volkodlak Tundra. 9 of those chests are on the map (small pins on this trove's layer).
3 Shadow Realm Vault: White Birch Snowgrave Trove · White Birch Snowgrave
Inside: Two full 5-star sets: Scarlet Proof and Heart of the Furnace, plus a Dust of Enlightenment.
To open it: Collect 3× Remnant Incense Guide - Snowgrave from Precious and Luxurious Chests in White Birch Snowgrave. 6 of those chests are on the map (small pins on this trove's layer).
4 Shadow Realm Vault (7.2): west of Teplo Oasis Trove · west of Teplo Oasis
Inside: Contents not yet confirmed on the wiki; the older vaults give two 5-star sets and a Dust of Enlightenment.
To open it: Collect 3 Remnant Incense Guides for this vault. The wiki does not list it yet; guides report they drop from Precious and Luxurious Chests in Teplo Oasis, Hallowed Plain and Wingspan Peak.

Eye of Graeae — 3 fragments

They aren't lying in the open world. Each is the reward for finishing a separate Snezhnaya questline. Any order.

2 · Flamefeather Valley — not in this view. Different part of Snezhnaya.
Fellfrost Peak (step 2 of fragment 1) is south of Svetloledovka, also off-view.
1
Pebble of Freezing Heart — Hesperides of Love and Hate Go to the central Svetloledovka waypoint, walk forward and drop into the valley below. Interact with the glowing spot on the wooden mining platforms for the Invitation — or talk to the Snegovik by the waypoint. Then head south to the Fellfrost Peak Statue of the Seven and meet Ikhor.
2
Pebble of Serpentine Algae — In the Dwelling of Life → Chorus of Solace Go to the Flamefeather Valley Statue of the Seven and defeat The Anvil nearby. That unlocks the world quest at its marker.
3
Pebble of Crystalline Essence — On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb Starts automatically after fragment 2. Inspect the Eye of Graeae at Central Station — the fragment is inside one of the train carriages. The quest then carries you to Snowgrave and opens the Pale Crown Palace.
Prerequisite: the finale Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard only appears after the Archon Quest Gunfire in the Silent Lands.

If the finale won't start, one chain is unfinished — the door to the Courtyard of Carefree Light opens only with all three. Talk to the Eye of Graeae there and choose to return its powers.

Why bother: Goggles stays usable for exploration, and it's what gives you Prime Ice Constructs — see 08-snezhnaya-mechanics.md.

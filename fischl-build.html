Fischl

Lv 50 · C0 · Electro bow · off-field sub-DPS. Live loadout read 27 Oct 2026.

She is wearing five 3-star artifacts, four of which roll the same ATK% 5.2%. Her weapon is fine. Her artifacts are the worst set on the account, and she has 7.1% CRIT Rate — the lowest of all 23 characters. This is the cheapest large fix left.

7.1% CRIT Rate means she crits about one hit in fourteen. 54.2% CRIT DMG is barely above the 50% every character starts with — so essentially none of her gear is contributing crit at all.
Her Energy Recharge is only fine because of Varesa. At C0 a solo-Electro Fischl needs 120–140% ER; with a second Electro character on the team it drops to 100%. She has 103.5%. Run her with Varesa and you can ignore ER entirely — split them up and she starts missing Bursts.

Weapon — keep Rust, but know what it isn't doing

✓
Rust — 4★, Lv 50, R1, ATK% 26.8% Keep it. Oz snapshots Fischl's ATK the moment he's summoned, so base ATK and ATK% both feed straight into his damage. As a stat stick it's the best bow you have for her.
!
Its passive is dead on this build Rust gives +40% Normal Attack DMG and −10% Charged Attack DMG. Oz's damage is Elemental Skill damage — neither half touches it. Don't let the passive influence what you compare it against; judge it on base ATK and ATK% alone.
✗
Don't move Slingshot over from Amber It tempts because of its CRIT Rate substat, but it's a 3★ with far less base ATK, its own passive is also dead for Oz, and it would leave Amber with nothing. Get the crit from the circlet instead — a 4★ CRIT Rate circlet at +16 beats it outright and costs Amber nothing.
·
If you want to craft something KQM's free 4★ picks are Fading Twilight, and the craftable Hamayumi and Prototype Crescent — all stat sticks for Oz too. Polar Star is her 5★ best-in-slot if you ever pull one.

How her kit actually works

Oz snapshots. When you cast her Skill, Oz locks in Fischl's stats at that instant and keeps them for his whole duration. Any ATK buff has to land before the Skill, not after. Recasting the Skill resummons him and re-snapshots.
E
Elemental Skill — summons Oz · 25s cooldown He attacks on his own with an 11m range and generates roughly 0.67 Electro particles per second — he is both her damage and the team's energy.
Q
Elemental Burst — 15s cooldown Also puts Oz on the field. The Burst-summoned Oz snapshots when Fischl leaves her Oz form.
A4
Her Ascension 4 passive — free damage you already have While Oz is out, when the active character triggers an Electro-related reaction, it deals extra Electro damage equal to 80% of Fischl's ATK, every 0.5s, within 15m of whoever is on the field. With Varesa on field and Barbara applying Hydro, Electro-Charged fires this constantly. Spread does not trigger it.
Talent priority: Elemental Skill first — Oz's damage scales off the Skill level, so it's the single highest-value talent on her. Then Burst (worth taking to level 6). Normal Attack barely matters; she isn't holding the field.

Rotation

1 · E — Oz out 2 · Swap to Varesa 3 · Q when it's up 4 · Re-cast E every 25s
1
Open with her Skill Oz first, before Varesa starts her ECP loop — so his damage overlaps the whole rotation and he's feeding particles the entire time.
2
Alternate Skill and Burst on cooldown Skill is 25s, Burst is 15s. At C0 her personal rotation is 25 seconds — don't try to force her into a shorter one.
!
Don't end a fight on her Burst She'll start the next fight with no energy and no Oz.

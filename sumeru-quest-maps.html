Target: Level 9 and the Dendro Treasure Compass. 33 world quests at 100 plus 7 Archon or story acts at 200 = 4,700 EXP, against the 5,000 needed — short by 300. The gap has to come from weekly Requests (80 each, capped at 3 per week across every nation) and exploration milestones.

Only the 33 world quests appear below; the acts have no start location of their own, so there is nothing to map for them.

You have explored 0% of this region, so treat this as a route plan rather than a to-do list — the Reputation board itself unlocks behind that region's Archon quest, and most of these start points sit behind waypoints you have not opened. The flagged ones are simply the shortest, for whenever you do arrive.
These maps are the wiki's own in-game crops, taken from each quest page and each giver's NPC page — not pins I placed. The white ring marks the spot; the blue teardrop nearby is the waypoint to teleport to.
Why there is no single map with every dot on it. The official interactive map's World Quest layer has 576 pins, every one of which returns a null name, and the wiki stores no coordinates. A combined map would have been guesswork; these per-quest crops are exact. Full sortable table across all regions: reputation-quests.html.

One-time collectibles

What the game knows, what the map knows, and what's actually left. Account data pulled live 28 Oct 2026.

There is no automatic sync, and there isn't going to be one. Nothing reads your collected oculi, chests or puzzles and ticks them off the map. I checked this against the live HoYoLAB map — the marking is manual. Full mechanics: oculus-tracking.html.
But two things are automatic, and together they get you most of the way. Your Battle Chronicle tells you how many of each you have — live, accurate, no effort. The map with "Hide Marked Pins" on tells you which ones are left, once you start marking. Chronicle for the count, map for the locations.

The thing your numbers actually say

This isn't a tracking problem. It's an exploration problem. Seven of your eight regions are between 0% and 6% explored. There's almost nothing marked on your map because there's almost nothing collected yet — a tracker would mostly be showing you empty regions.

So what should you actually do

1
Finish Mondstadt's Anemoculi — 16 left of 66 It's the only collectible on the account within reach of done. The Anemoculus Resonance Stone points at the nearest one you haven't taken — that's the real auto-tracker, and it's in the game already. Correction: it isn't bought from Marjorie as I first said — you craft it via Alchemy after Mondstadt Reputation Level 2, which is only four world quests away. See reputation-compass.html. Statue levels raise your stamina cap.
2
Then pick one region and commit to it Spreading across eight regions at 2% each is why nothing feels finished. Liyue at 22.7% has the most momentum, and its Geoculus Resonance Stone comes the same way — Liyue Reputation Level 2, then craft it.
3
Mark as you go, not retroactively Don't try to reconstruct 50 Anemoculi you've already taken. Turn on Hide Marked Pins and Batch Mark each area the moment you clear it. From then on every pin you can see is a pin you still owe.
4
Don't bother tracking chests There are several hundred per region and no published total, so a chest tracker can never show progress. Clear enemy camps and puzzles as you pass them and let the count rise on its own.
One consequence worth knowing. Natlan is at 2%, and Derelict Masonry Dock — the artifact domain for Skirk and Varesa — is in Atocpan. You have the Pyroculi to prove you've been there, but expect some travel to reach it rather than a short teleport.

Wood farming routes

The best 11-tree loop for each of the 33 wood types, with the teleport to start from.

How wood farming works. Each tree gives up to 3 wood, then stays empty until you have chopped ten other trees (or logged out and in). So a loop of 11 trees of one type never runs dry: every route here is the tightest 11-tree group of that wood on the official map, numbered in walking order from the nearest teleport. You can chop up to 2,000 of each wood type a day. Chops are Physical Normal, Charged and Plunging Attacks by sword, claymore and polearm users; infused attacks do not count, nor do Clorinde's or Skirk's Charged Attacks. Hitting several trees at once still drops wood from only one.

Talent farming — what is open today

Talent domains gate by day of the week, so the only question worth answering when you log in is which books you can get right now and whether anyone you own needs them.

Resin, and why it is not your problem

Resin income is fixed at 180 a day — one every 8 minutes, capped at 200. Nothing raises that. A talent domain run costs 20, so a full day is nine runs. The lever is never more resin, it is where it goes.
You are AR 45 This is the top difficulty tier for talent domains — they step at AR 25 / 28 / 36 / 45. You crossed the last one on 31 October. Same 20 resin, best payout. Check the tier selector inside the domain if you have not since.
Serenitea Pot Not unlocked yet — it needs the quest A New Star Approaches. Finishing it opens Transient Resin: 60 resin every week, free, for 1,200 Realm Currency at the Realm Depot (Trust Rank 6). It also pays roughly 300,000 Mora a week. This is the single biggest resin gain available to you.
Ranking up AR 46 through 49 each hand over 2 Fragile Resin. That is 8 Fragile, 480 resin, already banked ahead of you.
Condensed Resin 40 resin for a double-drop run. Liyue Reputation 4 means you already have the recipe. It saves time, not resin — and it does not work on bosses.
Do not crack Fragile Resin now. During an Overflowing Abundance / Overflowing Mastery double-drop you get double talent books, up to 3 claims a day — and Condensed Resin does not count toward those claims, only Original Resin. That caps the bonus at 60 Original a day, which is exactly one Fragile. Save them.

No 7.2 talent-book double-drop window is confirmed yet. Ley Line Overflow runs 7–14 December but pays Mora and EXP, not books.
The real constraint is weeks, not resin. Talents 7→10 need weekly boss materials and a Crown of Insight — roughly 18 weeks to take one character to 10/10/10. Spreading 180 resin a day across six characters gets all of them to 6 and none of them good. Pick one.

The Grand Defense event, 4 Nov – 16 Dec, includes a Crown of Insight.

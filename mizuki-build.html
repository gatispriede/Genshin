That sentence is true of Swirl itself and it is not where her damage comes from. I had skipped her Witch's Revelation passive. Build her with CRIT, and the Cryo Traveler is the point.

Where her damage actually comes from

Vast Be the Dream. When she triggers a Swirl or Stellar Swirl while in Dreamdrifter, her next instance of periodic AoE Anemo DMG is increased by 1,000% of her Elemental Mastery. Once every 2.5s.

That is an ordinary damage instance carrying a huge additive base — so it crits, and it scales with Anemo DMG Bonus and ATK as well as EM. The swirl is only the trigger; the enhanced Anemo hit is the payload. This is what the first version of the page missed entirely.

Radiance: Stellar Swirl. When she lands that enhanced hit, she deals a second instance at another 1,000% of her EM, counted as Stellar Swirl DMG. She enters the state for 8s whenever a nearby party member triggers a Stellar Swirl.

A Stellar enabler roughly doubles her biggest hit. That is why Stellar Swirl is her meta playstyle rather than a sidegrade, and why KQM, Prydwen and Mobalytics all build her around it.

She also grants nearby party members +10% of her Elemental Mastery while drifting, and her skill raises the Swirl and Stellar Swirl DMG they deal based on her EM.

Stellar Swirl needs an enabler, and you own exactly one

Stellar Swirl is Cryo + Anemo, and it only exists if a character with a Stellar Jubilee passive is in the party. There are four in the game — Odette, Vesna, Sandrone and Traveler (Cryo).

Illusory Frostmirror, the Cryo Traveler's passive: "When a party member triggers a Superconduct or Cryo Swirl reaction, it becomes a Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl reaction instead, and the Base DMG of said reaction is also increased by 0.35% for every 100 points of the Traveler's ATK. A maximum 7% increase."

He only needs to be in the party, not on the field — and he doubles as the Cryo applier. He is Lv 40 and his Cryo form has to be unlocked at the Snezhnaya Statues of the Seven first.

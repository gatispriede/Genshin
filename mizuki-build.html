Yumemizuki Mizuki

5★ Anemo catalyst · Inazuma · C0 · Lv 60 · on-field Swirl carry. Ascension stat is Elemental Mastery, which is exactly the stat she needs. Live loadout read 12 Nov 2026.

Swirl DMG is “fixed damage based only on character level and elemental mastery.” That is the wiki's own wording, and it decides every choice on this page. ATK, CRIT, Elemental DMG Bonus, generic DMG% buffs and talent levels do nothing for her swirl damage. Only her level, her EM, explicit Swirl-DMG bonuses, and the target's RES.

One rule shapes the whole team: a target can take only 2 instances of Swirl DMG every 0.5s from the same attacker — but the limit is separate for each swirled element. Running two swirlable auras roughly doubles her ceiling.

Sucrose's other passive does nothing here. Catalyst Conversion gives +50 EM only to party members whose element matches the swirled element. Mizuki is Anemo, so a Pyro or Electro swirl never matches her. Bring Sucrose for Mollis Favonius alone.

All three teammates also feed Mizuki's own 4th ascension passive, Thoughts by Day Bring Dreams by Night: while she is in Dreamdrifter, whenever other party members hit with Pyro, Hydro, Cryo or Electro, her Elemental Mastery rises by 100 for 4s.

Rotation

Everything is set up before she goes in. Dreamdrifter ends the moment she leaves the field, so there is no swapping out mid-window.

4Mizuki — Skill, then drift into the packSwirl both auras until the window ends
Her 1st ascension passive stretches the window. Bright Moon's Restless Voice adds 2.5s per swirl, twice — so a good Dreamdrifter runs 10 seconds instead of 5, and she deals up to 14 damage instances instead of 7. Sacrificial Fragments can reset the skill outright for a second window.

The swap worth knowing

Escoffier in place of Xiangling — for single targets and bosses.

Her Cooking Mek in Cold Storage mode follows the active character firing Cryo, which suits a carry who is locked on-field, and her kit reduces Cryo RES by up to 55%. Stack that with Viridescent Venerer's −40% to the swirled element and Cryo Swirl lands against deeply negative resistance — worth roughly 1.5× on its own.

The cost is that you drop to a single aura and lose the second swirl stream. Take it when there is one fat target; leave it for a crowd.

Four Viridescent pieces means the 4-piece bonus is already live — +60% Swirl DMG and −40% RES to the swirled element. Set bonuses ignore artifact level, so that part is free. What is not free is the EM: every piece sits between +0 and +9, and levelling those five is the largest EM gain available to her.
The goblet is the one real swap. Royal Silver Urn belongs to Noblesse Oblige, so it contributes no set bonus at all. A Viridescent Venerer's Vessel with an EM main from Valley of Remembrance completes the set properly — but since the 4-piece already runs off the other four slots, this is a stat upgrade rather than an unlock.

Not Thundering Fury

Thundering Fury does nothing for a swirl carry. Its 4-piece lists Overloaded, Electro-Charged, Superconduct, Hyperbloom, Aggravate, Lunar-Charged and Stellar-Conduct — Swirl is not among them. Its 2-piece is +15% Electro DMG Bonus, and Mizuki is Anemo, and swirl ignores elemental DMG bonus regardless. Viridescent Venerer's +60% Swirl DMG and −40% RES are both direct multipliers on her actual damage.

Where it does belong: an Electro character who personally triggers Electro-Charged or Overloaded — on this roster, Fischl with Oz landing on a wet target, or Lisa. Note that in the Mizuki team above, Mizuki is the one triggering the reactions via swirl spread, so the set would sit idle on Fischl there.

Not Varesa either: her damage is direct Electro, not reaction, so Obsidian Codex beats it — see 12-varesa-team.md. That said, her five pieces currently give no set bonus at all, so even a bare 2-piece Thundering Fury (+15% Electro DMG) on her is strictly better than what she wears today.

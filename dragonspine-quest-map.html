Dragonspine — In the Mountains

Your own map. Route to unlock Peak of Vindagnyr → Blizzard Strayer for Skirk.

Blocker: step 3 needs a Cryo character — Skirk, Kaeya, Mika or Chongyun. Also bring Noelle (claymore) for Scarlet Quartz nodes.
1
Iris — Adventurer Camp Ask about the blue crystals to start. AR 30 required; you're 33.
2
Strange ice Break a Scarlet Quartz node, grab it, hit the ice. Three quartz hits — the gauge only drops partway each time. Then activate the orb.
3
Wyrmrest Valley — monument order Watch the Warming Seelie's route between monuments, repeat that order with Cryo. The chest spawns 2 Ruin Guards + a Ruin Grader.
★
Statue of the Seven Already unlocked. The summit route runs through the cavern near it.
4
Skyfrost Nail — summit Three shards, one hit each. Activate every orb.
5
Pillar rises → Peak of Vindagnyr Drop into the gap beneath. Open the red-aura Luxurious Chest — that's what grants domain access. Also unlocks the Cryo Hypostasis (Shivada Jade) and the Frostbearing Tree.
